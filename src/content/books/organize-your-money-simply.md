## About the Book

Money does not have to be confusing.

*Organize Your Money Simply* is a practical Christian guide to understanding where your money is, what it is for, what happened to it, and how to manage it faithfully.

The book combines practical financial organization with biblical principles of stewardship.

## What You'll Learn

- How to understand where your money is
- How accounts and categories work
- How to record transactions clearly
- How to distinguish spending from transfers
- How to create a simple budget
- How to prepare for irregular and unexpected expenses
- How to think about debt
- How giving relates to stewardship
- How to review your finances simply
- How to keep your financial life understandable
