## Meaning

Manasseh is a Hebrew proper name meaning "causing to forget."

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Manasseh was the elder son of Joseph and Asenath.

Joseph named him Manasseh because God had caused him to forget his toil
and his father's house.

Manasseh later became the ancestor of the tribe of Manasseh.
