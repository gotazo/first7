## Meaning

Moabites are a people descended from Moab who inhabited the region east
of the Dead Sea.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the Moabites were descendants of Moab, the son of Lot.

They inhabited the land of Moab and are mentioned throughout the history
of Israel.
