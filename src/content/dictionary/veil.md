## Meaning

A veil is a curtain or covering used to separate or conceal.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the veil separated the Holy Place from the Most Holy Place in the tabernacle and later the temple.

Veils are also mentioned as garments or coverings in various contexts throughout the Bible.
