## Meaning

Restoration is the act of returning something to its former condition or restoring what was lost.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, restoration refers to the return, recovery, or rebuilding of people, places, possessions, or relationships.

The term appears in both literal and figurative contexts.
