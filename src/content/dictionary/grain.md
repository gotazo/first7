## Meaning

Grain is the edible seed or harvested produce of cereal plants such as wheat and barley.

---

## Biblical Usage

Grain was a major part of food production in biblical agriculture. It was harvested, gathered, stored, ground into flour, and prepared as food. Grain was also used in offerings and served as an important agricultural commodity.

The Bible frequently mentions grain in connection with wheat, barley, harvest, threshing, storage, and famine. The KJV often uses the word **corn** for grain or cereal produce.
