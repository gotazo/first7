## Meaning

The heart is the organ that pumps blood through the body.

The word is also used to describe the inner center of thought, emotion, and will.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, heart refers both to the physical organ and, more commonly, to the inner person, including thoughts, intentions, desires, and understanding.
