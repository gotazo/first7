## Meaning

Slaughter is the killing of people or animals, especially in large numbers or by violent means.

---

## Biblical Usage

Scripture uses *slaughter* for deaths resulting from warfare, violence, and judgment. The term may describe the killing of many people during a conflict or the killing of animals.

The word also appears in figurative language and in passages describing judgment. Its precise meaning depends on the passage and its context.

---

## Key References

- **2 Samuel 2:31** — Records the deaths of men during the conflict between the forces of David and Abner.
- **Isaiah 34:2** — Uses slaughter in a declaration of judgment upon the nations and their armies.
- **Jeremiah 12:3** — Uses the expression “the day of slaughter.”
- **Jeremiah 25:34** — Describes rulers as vessels prepared for slaughter.
- **Ezekiel 21:14** — Refers to the sword in connection with slaughter.
- **Matthew 22:7** — Records the king's response to those who rejected his invitation.
