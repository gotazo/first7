## Meaning

A disciple is a learner, follower, or student of a teacher.

The word describes someone who learns from and follows another.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, a disciple is a learner or follower of a teacher.

The term is most commonly used of those who followed Jesus Christ and were taught by Him.
