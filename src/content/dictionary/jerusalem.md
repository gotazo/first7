## Meaning

Jerusalem is an ancient city in the land of Israel, associated with the Jebusites before David captured it and established it as his royal city.

---

## Biblical Usage

Jerusalem appears throughout Scripture as a city of political, military, and religious importance. In 2 Samuel 5:6-9, David captures the stronghold of Zion and establishes his royal city there.

The city later becomes closely associated with Solomon's temple and Israel's worship. Jerusalem also appears in accounts of conquest, judgment, exile, restoration, and the ministry of Jesus Christ.

---

## Key References

- **Joshua 10:1-5** — Records the king of Jerusalem and the conflict involving the city.
- **Judges 1:8** — Describes the taking of Jerusalem by the children of Judah.
- **2 Samuel 5:6-9** — Records David's capture of Jerusalem and the establishment of the City of David.
- **1 Kings 8:1** — Records the gathering of Israel's elders in Jerusalem for the dedication of the temple.
- **Psalm 122:6** — Calls for prayer for the peace of Jerusalem.
- **Isaiah 62:1** — Mentions Zion and Jerusalem in a prophecy concerning their righteousness.
- **Luke 19:41-44** — Records Jesus weeping over Jerusalem.
- **Revelation 21:2** — Describes the holy city, new Jerusalem.
