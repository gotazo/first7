## Meaning

A memorial is something established or preserved to remember a person, event, covenant, or other significant matter.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, a memorial is something intended to preserve the memory of a person, event, act, or covenant. Memorials may consist of objects, offerings, names, written records, or appointed observances.

They serve to bring a matter to remembrance before people or before God. After Israel crossed Jordan, twelve stones were taken from the river and set up as a memorial so that future generations would remember what God had done.
