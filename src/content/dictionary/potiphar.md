## Meaning

Potiphar is an Egyptian proper name traditionally understood to mean
"given by Ra."

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Potiphar was an Egyptian officer of Pharaoh and captain of
the guard.

He bought Joseph as a servant and placed him over his household.

Potiphar's wife later accused Joseph after he refused her advances, and
Joseph was subsequently placed in prison.
