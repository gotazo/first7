## Meaning

Egypt is the ancient land and kingdom associated in Scripture with the
descendants of Mizraim and situated in northeastern Africa.

The Hebrew name is Mitsrayim, while the English name Egypt comes through
Greek and later languages.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Egypt is the land where Abraham sojourned, where Joseph was
elevated to authority under Pharaoh, and where the children of Israel were
enslaved before God brought them out under Moses.

Egypt is also frequently mentioned in relation to Israel, Judah, and the
nations surrounding them.
