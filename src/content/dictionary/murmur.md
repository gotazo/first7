## Meaning

To murmur is to speak or complain in a low, quiet, or dissatisfied manner.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, to murmur means to complain, grumble, or express
dissatisfaction, especially against God, His servants, or circumstances.

The Israelites repeatedly murmured during their wilderness journey, and the
New Testament also warns against murmuring among believers.
