## Meaning

Transgression is the violation of a law, command, rule, or established boundary.

---

## Biblical Usage

Scripture uses *transgression* for violations of commands, laws, and moral obligations. The term can describe the wrongdoing of an individual or the departure of a people from what was commanded.

Transgression often appears alongside *sin* and *iniquity*. Although the terms overlap, *transgression* can particularly emphasize crossing or violating an established boundary or requirement.

---

## Key References

- **Exodus 23:21** — Warns concerning transgression against the angel whose voice Israel was commanded to obey.
- **Psalm 32:1** — Describes the blessedness of having transgression forgiven.
- **Psalm 51:1** — David asks for mercy and the blotting out of his transgressions.
- **Isaiah 53:5** — Refers to the suffering associated with transgressions.
- **Daniel 9:11** — Describes Israel's departure from the law.
- **Romans 4:15** — Discusses the relationship between law and transgression.
- **Galatians 3:19** — Discusses the law in relation to transgressions.

---

## Related Terms

- **Iniquity** — Wrongdoing or moral corruption.
- **Sin** — An act or condition of wrongdoing.
- **Disobedience** — Failure or refusal to obey a command.
- **Law** — A rule or command established by an authority.
