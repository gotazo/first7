## Meaning

Cleansing is the process or action of making something clean or free from
impurity.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, cleansing refers to the process or action by which a person
or thing is made clean or freed from impurity.

The Bible uses the term in physical, ceremonial, moral, and spiritual
contexts, including washing, purification, and cleansing from sin.
