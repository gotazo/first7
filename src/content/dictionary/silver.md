## Meaning

Silver is a valuable metal used as money, currency, jewelry, vessels,
and other objects.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, silver was used as a form of payment and wealth and was
also fashioned into various objects.

Genesis mentions silver in connection with purchasing goods, paying for
property, and the sale of Joseph.

Silver is also frequently mentioned in the construction and furnishings
of the tabernacle and in later biblical accounts.
