## Meaning

A birthright is the rights or privileges belonging to the firstborn.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the birthright refers to the rights associated with being
the firstborn son.

The birthright appears in the account of Esau and Jacob and is also
addressed in the laws concerning the inheritance of the firstborn.
