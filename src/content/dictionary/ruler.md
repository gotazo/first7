## Meaning

A ruler is a person who exercises authority or government over other people or a territory.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, a ruler is a person who exercises authority over people, territory, a household, or an organization. Rulers include kings, princes, governors, judges, and other persons who possess or exercise authority.

The term can refer to political government, local authority, religious leadership, or other forms of oversight. Scripture also uses ruler in descriptions of God's authority and of Christ's rule.
