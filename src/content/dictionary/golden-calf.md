## Meaning

The golden calf was a molten calf made by the Israelites at Mount Sinai
and worshipped during Moses' absence.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the golden calf was made by Aaron from the gold brought by
the children of Israel while Moses was on Mount Sinai.

The people worshipped the calf and offered sacrifices before it.

When Moses came down from the mountain and saw what they had done, he broke
the tablets of stone and destroyed the calf.
