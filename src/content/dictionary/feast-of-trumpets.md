## Meaning

The Feast of Trumpets is an appointed feast observed on the first day of the seventh month.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the Feast of Trumpets was one of the appointed feasts of Israel.

It was observed as a memorial of blowing of trumpets and a holy convocation according to God's commandments.
