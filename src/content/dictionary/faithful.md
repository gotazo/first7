## Meaning

Faithful means loyal, trustworthy, dependable, and true to one's word or commitments. It describes someone who can be relied upon to act honestly and consistently.

## Biblical Usage

In 2 Samuel 20:19, a woman of Abel speaks to Joab during his pursuit of Sheba. She describes the people as “peaceable and faithful” and appeals to Joab not to destroy the city. Her words connect faithfulness with peaceful conduct and the preservation of Israel's inheritance.

## Original Languages

**Hebrew:** אֱמוּנֵי (*emunei*) is the form used in 2 Samuel 20:19. It is associated with the Hebrew root אָמַן (*aman*), which conveys the ideas of firmness, trustworthiness, and faithfulness.

**Greek:** πιστός (*pistos*) means faithful or trustworthy. It is included as a related Greek term, not as the original wording of the Old Testament passage.

## Key Reference
