## Meaning

Judah is a Hebrew proper name associated with "praise."

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Judah was the fourth son of Jacob and Leah.

He became the ancestor of the tribe of Judah. The name Judah is also used in Scripture for the people, territory, and kingdom associated with the tribe.

The name Judah is also prominent in the genealogical record of Jesus Christ.
