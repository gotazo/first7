## Meaning

A redeemer is a person who redeems or rescues another.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, redeemer refers to one who redeems, rescues, or acts on behalf of another.

The term is used of the kinsman-redeemer in the Old Testament and of the Lord as Redeemer.
