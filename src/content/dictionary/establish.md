## Meaning

To establish means to set up, make firm, confirm, or cause something to endure. It may refer to founding something, securing its position, or making it stable.

## Biblical Usage

In 2 Samuel 7:12, the LORD promises David that He will establish the kingdom of David's seed.

In 2 Samuel 7:13, the LORD declares that He will stablish the throne of his kingdom for ever.

In 2 Samuel 7:16, David's house, kingdom, and throne are described as established for ever before him.

These passages concern God's promise regarding David's house and kingdom.

## Original Languages

**Hebrew:** The verb כּוּן (*kun*) means to establish, make firm, prepare, or set securely in place. Forms of this verb occur in 2 Samuel 7:12 and in the statement concerning David's throne in 2 Samuel 7:16.

The Hebrew text of 2 Samuel 7:16 also uses וְנֶאְמַן (*vene'eman*), from אָמַן (*aman*), in the statement concerning David's house and kingdom. This verb conveys the sense of being firm, confirmed, or established.

**Greek:** στηρίζω (*stērizō*) means to establish, strengthen, or make firm. It is a related Greek word, not the original language of 2 Samuel.
