## Meaning

Philistines are a people who inhabited the coastal region of Canaan
known as Philistia.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the Philistines were a people who lived in cities along
the southwestern coast of Canaan.

They appear in the accounts of Abraham and Isaac and later became
frequent opponents of the children of Israel.
