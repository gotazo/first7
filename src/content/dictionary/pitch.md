## Meaning

Pitch has several meanings in ordinary English. It can refer to the highness or lowness of a musical sound, a slope, a persuasive presentation, or a thick, sticky substance. As a verb, it can mean to throw something, set up a tent, or cover something with pitch.

## Biblical Usage

In the Bible, *pitch* refers to a thick, sticky substance used to seal and waterproof materials. In Genesis 6:14, Noah was instructed to cover the ark within and without with pitch. In Exodus 2:3, the ark made for Moses was coated with slime and pitch to make it watertight.

Isaiah 34:9 uses the word in a description of judgment, stating that the streams would be turned into pitch and the land would become burning pitch.

## Key References

- **Genesis 6:14** — Pitch was used to cover the ark within and without.
- **Exodus 2:3** — Pitch was applied to the ark of bulrushes in which Moses was placed.
- **Isaiah 34:9** — Pitch is mentioned in the description of judgment upon the land of Idumea.

## Related Terms

- **Ark** — A vessel or chest; in these passages, a covered structure or small vessel.
- **Bulrushes** — Reeds used in making the ark in which Moses was placed.
