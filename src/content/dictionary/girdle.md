## Meaning

A band or belt worn around the waist or body, often used to secure clothing or carry objects.

---

## Biblical Usage

Scripture uses girdle for a belt or band worn around the waist as part of clothing. Girdles could be made from different materials and could be associated with particular garments, occupations, or status.

The Bible also uses girdle figuratively in expressions concerning strength, readiness, righteousness, and truth. To gird oneself could mean to fasten one's clothing in preparation for movement or work.

---

## Key References

- Exodus 28:4 — A girdle is part of the priestly garments.
- Judges 3:16 — Ehud makes himself a dagger and fastens it under his raiment upon his right thigh.
- 1 Samuel 18:4 — Jonathan gives David his girdle along with his garments and weapons.
- 2 Samuel 18:11 — A reward is described in connection with a girdle.
- 2 Kings 1:8 — Elijah is identified by his hairy garment and leather girdle.
- Proverbs 31:17 — The virtuous woman girds her loins with strength.
- Isaiah 11:5 — Righteousness and faithfulness are described as a girdle.
- Ephesians 6:14 — Believers are instructed to have their loins girded with truth.
- 1 Peter 1:13 — Believers are told to gird up the loins of their mind.
