## Meaning

A foundation is the base or underlying support on which a building or structure rests.

It provides stability and support for what is built upon it.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, foundation refers to the base of a building or structure.

The term is also used figuratively for the beginning, basis, or underlying support of people, cities, and spiritual truths.
