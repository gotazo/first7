## Meaning

A standard is a recognized measure, example, level, or symbol used as a
guide or point of reference.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, standard can refer to a banner, ensign, or signal used to
identify and organize a group of people, especially a tribe or division
of Israel.

The word is also used figuratively for a raised or established sign, and
in some passages for a measure or recognized rule.
