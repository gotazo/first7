## Meaning

Blood is the red liquid that circulates through the bodies of people and animals.

It carries life through the body and is essential for living creatures.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, blood refers to the lifeblood of people and animals.

It is associated with life, sacrifice, covenants, cleansing, and atonement in various passages throughout the Bible.
