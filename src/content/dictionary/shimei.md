## Meaning

Shimei is a Hebrew masculine personal name related to the Hebrew root associated with hearing. It is commonly understood as “he has heard.”

---

## Biblical Usage

Several people in Scripture bear the name Shimei. The Shimei described in 2 Samuel 16 was the son of Gera, a Benjamite associated with the house of Saul. He cursed David and threw stones at him while David fled Jerusalem during Absalom's rebellion.

When David returned, Shimei asked for forgiveness, and David swore that he would not put him to death at that time. Before his death, David gave Solomon instructions concerning Shimei.

## Key References

- **2 Samuel 16:5–13** — Shimei curses David as David flees Jerusalem.
- **2 Samuel 19:16–23** — Shimei meets David on his return and asks for mercy.
- **1 Kings 2:8–9** — David gives Solomon instructions concerning Shimei.
- **1 Kings 2:36–46** — Shimei is put to death after disobeying Solomon's command.
