## Meaning

Presumptuous means acting with excessive confidence or without sufficient
right, authority, or consideration.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, presumptuous describes acting deliberately or arrogantly
without proper authority, restraint, or regard for God's commands.

The Bible especially uses the term for deliberate wrongdoing and actions
taken in defiance of God's instruction.
