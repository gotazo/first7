## Meaning

A unit of weight used in the ancient Near East and also a unit of value or money in biblical times.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the shekel functions as a standard measure of weight and as a monetary value. It is used in transactions, offerings, tribute, wages, and the valuation of people and objects.

The Bible refers to the shekel of the sanctuary as a particular standard, and shekels of silver and gold appear in various historical accounts. In Joshua, Achan took two hundred shekels of silver and a wedge of gold weighing fifty shekels.
