## Meaning

A Hebrew male personal name of uncertain meaning, traditionally connected with the Hebrew word for “fist”; the proposed meaning “pugilist” is uncertain.

---

## Biblical Usage

Hophni was one of the two sons of Eli and served as a priest at Shiloh. Scripture describes Hophni and Phinehas as sons of Belial who did not know the LORD and who acted corruptly in their priestly service.

Hophni and Phinehas continued their wrongdoing despite Eli's rebuke. Both died in the same battle with the Philistines when the ark of God was captured, fulfilling the judgment previously spoken concerning Eli's house.
