## Meaning

Zion is the name of the stronghold captured by David in Jerusalem, later used in Scripture for Jerusalem and associated places.

---

## Biblical Usage

In 2 Samuel 5:7, Zion is the stronghold captured by David from the Jebusites. The passage identifies it as the City of David.

In later biblical usage, Zion can refer to Jerusalem or a place associated with worship and the LORD's dwelling. The name appears in poetry, prophecy, and the New Testament, and its particular reference must be determined from context. <Cite refs={["turn894370search12","turn894370search16"]}/>

## Key References

- **2 Samuel 5:7-9** — Records David's capture of the stronghold of Zion.
- **1 Kings 8:1** — Records the gathering of Israel's elders in Jerusalem for the temple dedication.
- **Psalm 48:2** — Describes Mount Zion in connection with the city of the great King.
- **Psalm 132:13** — States that the LORD hath chosen Zion.
- **Isaiah 2:3** — Describes the law going forth from Zion and the word of the LORD from Jerusalem.
- **Isaiah 62:1** — Mentions Zion and Jerusalem in a prophecy concerning righteousness.
- **Hebrews 12:22** — Refers to Mount Sion and the city of the living God.
- **Revelation 14:1** — Describes the Lamb standing on Mount Sion.
