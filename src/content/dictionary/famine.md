## Meaning

Famine is a severe and prolonged shortage of food affecting a region or
people.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, famine refers to a severe shortage of food affecting a
region or people.

Famines are recorded throughout the Bible, including the famine that
caused Abraham to go to Egypt, the famine in the days of Isaac, and the
widespread famine during the years of Joseph in Egypt.
