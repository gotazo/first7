## Meaning

To wave is to move something back and forth or from side to side, often as
a gesture or signal.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, wave is used both for ordinary movement and for the
ceremonial presentation of offerings before the LORD.

A wave offering was presented before the LORD by a prescribed movement or
presentation as part of the worship of Israel.
