## Meaning

A nephew is the son of one's brother or sister. In older English usage, the word could also refer to a grandchild or another descendant.

---

## Biblical Usage

The KJV uses *nephew* in its older English sense in certain passages. In 1 Timothy 5:4, it translates a Greek word meaning a descendant or grandchild. The passage concerns family responsibilities toward a widow.

In Isaiah 14:22, *nephew* appears alongside *son* and *remnant* in a declaration concerning Babylon. These occurrences should be understood according to their historical wording and context, rather than assuming that the word always has its modern meaning.

---

## Key References

- **Job 18:19** — Uses *nephew* in a description of the loss of descendants and family continuity.
- **Isaiah 14:22** — Includes *nephew* in a declaration concerning Babylon's name and descendants.
- **1 Timothy 5:4** — Refers to children or nephews in instructions about family responsibilities toward widows.
