## Meaning

Marriage is the union or relationship established between a man and a woman as husband and wife.

---

## Biblical Usage

Marriage appears throughout Scripture as a family and social relationship involving a husband and wife. Biblical accounts describe marriage in connection with companionship, children, household life, inheritance, and family relationships.

The Bible also gives laws and teachings concerning marriage, including faithfulness between husband and wife, adultery, divorce, and the responsibilities of husbands and wives. Ruth provides an example of marriage within the wider context of family redemption and inheritance.
