## Meaning

Firstfruits are the first portion of a harvest or produce presented to the LORD.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the firstfruits were presented to the LORD according to God's commandments.

They are mentioned in connection with offerings, feasts, agricultural produce, and later in the New Testament.
