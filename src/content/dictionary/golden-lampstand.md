## Meaning

The Golden Lampstand was a gold lampstand placed in the Holy Place to provide light.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the Golden Lampstand stood in the Holy Place of the tabernacle and later the temple.

It provided light according to God's instructions and is mentioned in both the Old and New Testaments.
