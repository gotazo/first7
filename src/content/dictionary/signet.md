## Meaning

A signet is a seal or engraved object used to mark, authenticate, or
identify something as belonging to its owner.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, a signet was used as a personal or official seal.

It could be worn or used to impress a mark in a substance such as clay
or wax.

Signets are associated in Scripture with authority, ownership,
identification, and official transactions.
