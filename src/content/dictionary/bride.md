## Meaning

A bride is a woman who is about to be married or has recently been married.

The word refers to the female participant in a marriage.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, a bride refers to a woman joined in marriage.

The term is also used figuratively in passages describing the relationship between Christ and His church.
