## Meaning

A city in the plain of Jordan associated with the account of Lot.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Sodom was a city in the plain of Jordan.

It was one of the cities associated with the account of Lot and was
destroyed together with Gomorrah.

Sodom is also mentioned in several books of the Old and New Testaments.
