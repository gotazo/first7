## Meaning

An offering is something that is presented, given, or offered to another person or for a particular purpose.

It may refer to a gift, a religious sacrifice, a contribution, or something made available or presented.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, an offering is something presented to God.

Offerings included animals, grain, oil, drink offerings, incense, and other gifts, and were brought for various purposes according to God's instructions.
