## Meaning

Conscience is an inner awareness that helps a person recognize right and wrong.

It is the inward sense by which a person evaluates thoughts and actions.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, conscience refers to the inward awareness by which a person bears witness to his thoughts and actions.

The term appears frequently in the New Testament.
