## Meaning

Discernment is the ability to recognize, distinguish, or judge between different things.

It involves carefully identifying differences or recognizing what something is.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, discernment refers to the ability to distinguish between things such as good and evil, truth and error, or different spirits.

The term is associated with wisdom, understanding, and sound judgment.
