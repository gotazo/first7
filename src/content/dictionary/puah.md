## Meaning

Puah is a proper name of uncertain meaning, possibly associated with
"girl" or "young woman."

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Puah was one of the two Hebrew midwives whom Pharaoh
commanded to kill the male children born to the Hebrew women.

She and Shiphrah feared God and did not obey Pharaoh's command, but
allowed the male children to live.

God dealt well with the midwives and gave them households because they
feared Him.
