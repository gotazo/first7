## Meaning

A border is the outer limit or boundary of a place, land, territory, or
region.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, border refers to the boundary or outer limit of a land,
territory, region, or possession.

The Bible frequently describes the borders of the territories allotted to
the tribes of Israel and also uses the term for boundaries between peoples
and lands.
