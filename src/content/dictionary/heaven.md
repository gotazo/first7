## Meaning

Heaven is the sky above the earth or the dwelling place of God.

The meaning depends on the context in which the word is used.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, heaven refers to the sky, the expanse above the earth, outer space, and the dwelling place of God.

The meaning depends on the context of each passage.
