## Meaning

A flag is a piece of cloth bearing a design, picture, or symbol, often attached to a pole or rope and used to identify a country, group, or organization, or to give a signal.

## Biblical Usage

In the King James Version (KJV), the word "flags" has an older botanical meaning: reeds or marsh vegetation growing in wet places.

In Exodus 2:3, Moses' mother placed the ark containing him among the flags beside the river. Exodus 2:5 also mentions the flags when Pharaoh's daughter discovered the ark.

Job 8:11 refers to a flag growing where there is water. The Hebrew word used there differs from suph, the word associated with the flags in Exodus 2:3 and 2:5. Isaiah 19:6 mentions reeds and flags in a description of Egypt's waters drying up.

## Key References

- **Exodus 2:3** — Moses was placed in an ark among the flags beside the river.
- **Exodus 2:5** — Pharaoh's daughter saw the ark among the flags.
- **Job 8:11** — The flag is mentioned as a plant that cannot grow without water.
- **Isaiah 19:6** — Reeds and flags are mentioned in the description of Egypt's waters drying up.

## Related Terms

- **Bulrushes** — Reeds used to make the ark in which Moses was placed.
- **Nile River** — The river beside which Moses was placed among the flags.
