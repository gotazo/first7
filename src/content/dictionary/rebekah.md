## Meaning

Rebekah is a proper name of uncertain meaning, traditionally associated
with "to bind."

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Rebekah was the wife of Isaac and the mother of Esau and Jacob.

She was the daughter of Bethuel and the sister of Laban.

Rebekah is mentioned in both the Old and New Testaments.
