## Meaning

Amnon is a Hebrew masculine personal name commonly associated with faithfulness or reliability, although its precise etymology is not certain.

---

## Biblical Usage

Amnon was the firstborn son of David. He became obsessed with Tamar, his half-sister, and assaulted her. After the assault, he rejected her and sent her away.

Absalom, Tamar's full brother, later arranged Amnon's death during a feast. The killing intensified conflict within David's family, and Absalom fled after the incident.
