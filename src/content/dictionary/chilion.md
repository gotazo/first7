## Meaning

A biblical Hebrew proper name of uncertain etymology; traditional explanations of the name are not established with certainty.

---

## Biblical Usage

Chilion was the son of Elimelech and Naomi and the brother of Mahlon. He went with his family from Bethlehemjudah to Moab during a famine.

Chilion married Orpah, but he died in Moab along with his brother Mahlon. After their deaths, Naomi and Ruth returned to Bethlehem.
