## Meaning

A stronghold is a secure or defensible place that is difficult for an enemy to capture. The word can also describe a source of protection or a firmly established position.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, strongholds include fortified places and locations used for defence or refuge. David stayed in strongholds while Saul sought to capture him (1 Samuel 23:14). Such places could provide protection from an attacking army or pursuer.

The term is also used figuratively. The LORD is described as a stronghold or refuge in times of trouble (Psalm 9:9). In 2 Corinthians 10:4, Paul uses the term figuratively in describing the overthrow of arguments and positions raised against the knowledge of God.

---

## Key References
