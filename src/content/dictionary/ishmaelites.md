## Meaning

Ishmaelites are a people descended from Ishmael, the son of Abraham and
Hagar.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the Ishmaelites were descendants of Ishmael.

They are mentioned among the peoples and traders encountered by Jacob's
sons and are also associated with the descendants of Abraham through
Ishmael.
