## Meaning

Joseph is a Hebrew proper name meaning "he will add" or "may he add."

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Joseph was the son of Jacob and Rachel and the brother of Benjamin.

He was sold by his brothers into Egypt and later became ruler over the land under Pharaoh.

Joseph was the father of Ephraim and Manasseh.

Joseph is mentioned throughout the Old Testament and is also mentioned in the New Testament.
