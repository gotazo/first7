## Meaning

A city in the plain of Jordan associated with Sodom and the account of Lot.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Gomorrah was a city associated with Sodom and the cities
of the plain.

It was destroyed together with Sodom because of the wickedness of its
inhabitants.

Gomorrah is also used in later Scripture as a reference to the judgment
of the city.
