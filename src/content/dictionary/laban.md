## Meaning

Laban is a Hebrew proper name meaning "white."

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Laban was the brother of Rebekah and the father of Leah
and Rachel.

He lived in Haran and was the father-in-law of Jacob.

Laban appears in the accounts of Isaac's family, Jacob's marriages, and
Jacob's years of service in Haran.
