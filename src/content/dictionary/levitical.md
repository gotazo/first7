## Meaning

Levitical means relating to Levi, the Levites, or the religious service
assigned to the tribe of Levi.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Levitical refers to matters associated with Levi and his
descendants, particularly the Levites and their duties in the worship and
service of God.

The Levites were set apart for service concerning the tabernacle and later
the temple.

The priests belonged to the family of Aaron within the tribe of Levi,
while the wider tribe included other Levites who performed various duties
of service.
