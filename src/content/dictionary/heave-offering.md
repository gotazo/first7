## Meaning

A heave offering was an offering set apart for the LORD according to God's commandments.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the heave offering was a portion of an offering set apart for the LORD according to the Law of Moses.

It is mentioned in connection with offerings brought by the Israelites and portions assigned to the priests.
