## Meaning

A dove is a small bird belonging to the pigeon family.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the dove is mentioned as a bird used for sacrifices, appears in the account of Noah's flood, and is associated with the Holy Spirit at the baptism of Jesus.
