## Meaning

A spirit believed to be available to or associated with a person who seeks supernatural knowledge or assistance through it.

---

## Biblical Usage

In the Old Testament, Scripture uses familiar spirit for a spirit associated with forbidden supernatural practices. People who sought such spirits were described as consulting them or seeking information through them.

The law of Moses forbids Israel from turning to those with familiar spirits and commands the people to seek God rather than such practices. The account of Saul and the woman of Endor provides a prominent example of consultation with one who had a familiar spirit.
