## Meaning

A token is a sign, mark, object, or other thing used to represent, identify, confirm, or remember something.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, a token is a sign or object that serves as evidence, identification, remembrance, or confirmation of a person, event, promise, or relationship.

The covenant sign of circumcision is described as a token, while other signs and objects serve as reminders or evidence of events and relationships. The term therefore covers several kinds of identifying or confirming signs.
