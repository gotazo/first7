## Meaning

Calling is an invitation, summons, or appointment to a particular purpose or role.

It may refer to being invited, appointed, or summoned to a task or responsibility.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, calling refers to God's invitation, summons, or appointment.

The term is used of God's call to individuals, His purposes, and the vocation or service to which people are called.
