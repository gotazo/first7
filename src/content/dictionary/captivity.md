## Meaning

Captivity is the state of being held as a prisoner or taken into exile.

It refers to being under the control of another person or nation.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, captivity refers to being taken captive by an enemy, removed from one's homeland, or held under the control of another nation or people.
