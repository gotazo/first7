## Meaning

Captivity is the state of being held under the control or power of
another, especially as a prisoner or conquered people.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, captivity refers to the condition of people who have been
taken and held by another power.

The Bible records the captivity of Israel and Judah by foreign kingdoms
and also uses the term in connection with individuals taken as
prisoners.

The term is also used in Scripture in connection with deliverance and
the restoration of those who have been taken captive.
