## Meaning

Discipline is training, instruction, or correction intended to develop proper conduct.

It includes teaching, correction, and guidance.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, discipline refers to instruction, training, correction, and chastening.

The term is used of parental instruction, personal training, and God's discipline of His people.
