## Meaning

Seir is a Hebrew place name meaning "hairy." It was a mountainous region
associated with Esau and the descendants of Edom.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Seir is the name of a mountainous region where the
descendants of Esau, also called Edom, dwelt.

The name is also associated with Seir the Horite, who lived in the region
before the descendants of Esau.
