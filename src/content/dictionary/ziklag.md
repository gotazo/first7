## Meaning

Ziklag was a town in the southern part of the land of Israel given to David by Achish, king of Gath, as a place to dwell.

---

## Biblical Usage

Ziklag is listed among the towns assigned to Simeon and among the cities associated with Judah. During David's flight from Saul, Achish gave him Ziklag as a place of residence.

The Amalekites later raided the town while David and his men were away. David pursued them and recovered the captives and possessions. After Saul's death, David was living in Ziklag when he received the news. The precise modern identification of the site is uncertain. <Cite refs={["turn894370search10"]}/>
