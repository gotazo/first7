## Meaning

A well is a hole or shaft dug into the ground to obtain water.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, wells provided water for people and animals and were
important places in the lives of biblical families and communities.

Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, and their descendants are associated with wells.

Wells also appear in accounts involving Rebekah, Rachel, and Moses.
