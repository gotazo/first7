## Meaning

The Tower of Babel is the tower built in the land of Shinar as recorded in Genesis 11.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the Tower of Babel was built in the land of Shinar after the Flood.

The account records that people gathered to build a city and a tower. God then confounded their language, and they were scattered over the face of the earth.

The Tower of Babel is mentioned only in Genesis 11.
