## Meaning

Eve is a Hebrew proper name meaning "life" or "living."

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Eve is the name of the first woman created by God.

She was made from one of Adam's ribs, became his wife, and is described as the mother of all living.

Eve appears in both the Old and New Testaments.
