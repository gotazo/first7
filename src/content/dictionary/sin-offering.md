## Meaning

A sin offering was an offering presented according to God's commandments in connection with sin.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the sin offering was one of the offerings prescribed under the Law of Moses.

It was presented in accordance with God's commandments for specific sins and formed part of Israel's system of worship.
