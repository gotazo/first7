## Meaning

An ephod was a priestly garment worn as part of the official clothing of the high priest and, in some instances, by other priests.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the ephod was a sacred garment associated with priestly service.

It formed part of the high priest's official garments and is also mentioned in connection with certain other priests.
