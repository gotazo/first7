## Meaning

A cord is a length of flexible material made by twisting or braiding strands together, used for tying, binding, fastening, or lowering.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, a cord is used for practical purposes such as binding, tying, measuring, and lowering people or objects. Cords also appear in descriptions of strength, restraint, inheritance, and relationships.

Rahab used a cord to let the spies down from the window of her house at Jericho. The spies later referred to the same scarlet line when giving instructions concerning her household.
