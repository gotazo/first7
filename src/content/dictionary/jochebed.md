## Meaning

Jochebed is a Hebrew proper name traditionally understood as "the LORD
is glory"; the etymology has been questioned.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Jochebed was the daughter of Levi, the wife of Amram, and
the mother of Moses, Aaron, and Miriam.

She preserved Moses' life by hiding him after his birth and placing him
in an ark among the flags by the river.

Jochebed is named explicitly in Exodus 6:20 and Numbers 26:59.
