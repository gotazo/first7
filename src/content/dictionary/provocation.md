## Meaning

Provocation is an action or circumstance that causes anger, irritation,
or a strong response.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, provocation refers to an action or conduct that arouses
anger or displeasure, especially rebellion or disobedience against God.

The Bible repeatedly describes Israel's unbelief, idolatry, and rebellion
in the wilderness as provoking the LORD.
