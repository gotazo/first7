## Meaning

Gath was an important Philistine city associated in Scripture with Goliath and with David's period of refuge among the Philistines.

---

## Biblical Usage

Gath was one of the principal Philistine cities. It appears in accounts of conflict between Israel and the Philistines, including the account of Goliath, who was from Gath.

David later went to Achish, king of Gath, while fleeing Saul. In 2 Samuel 1:20, David mentions Gath in his lament concerning Saul and Jonathan, telling Israel not to publish the news of their deaths there.
