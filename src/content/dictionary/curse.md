## Meaning

A curse is a pronouncement of harm, judgment, or misfortune upon a person or thing. The word may also refer to a condition of being under such a pronouncement.

---

## Biblical Usage

Scripture uses *curse* for spoken imprecations, declared consequences of disobedience, and conditions associated with judgment. Curses appear in accounts concerning individuals and in passages setting out the consequences of covenant disobedience.

In 2 Samuel 16:5-13, Shimei curses David as David leaves Jerusalem. Elsewhere, the term appears in passages concerning the Fall, the law, and redemption.

---

## Key References

- **Genesis 3:14-17** — Records the pronouncements of judgment following the disobedience in Eden.
- **Genesis 12:3** — Includes a curse in the declaration concerning those who curse Abram.
- **Deuteronomy 28:15-20** — Describes curses associated with disobedience.
- **2 Samuel 16:5-13** — Records Shimei cursing David.
- **Proverbs 26:2** — Compares a causeless curse with a wandering bird.
- **Zechariah 5:3-4** — Describes a curse going forth over the face of the earth.
- **Galatians 3:10-13** — Discusses the curse of the law and Christ's redemption.
