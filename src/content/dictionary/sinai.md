## Meaning

Sinai is the name of a mountain and wilderness region associated with
God's covenant with Israel.

The origin and meaning of the name are uncertain.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Sinai is the mountain and wilderness region where the
children of Israel camped after leaving Egypt.

At Sinai, God spoke to Moses, established His covenant with Israel, and
gave the commandments to the nation.
