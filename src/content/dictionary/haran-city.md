## Meaning

Haran, also known as Harran, is an ancient city of northern Mesopotamia,
located in present-day southeastern Turkey near the Syrian border.

The ancient city was an important settlement in northern Mesopotamia and
was situated along major routes connecting Mesopotamia with the regions
to the west.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Haran was a city where Terah settled with Abram, Sarai, and
Lot after leaving Ur of the Chaldees.

Terah died there, and Abram departed from Haran at the command of the LORD
to go into the land of Canaan.

Haran was also associated with the family of Rebekah and Rachel through
their relatives who lived there.
