## Meaning

A burden is a heavy load carried by a person or animal; something difficult to bear.

The word may also refer to a responsibility or obligation.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, burden refers to a physical load, a responsibility, a hardship, or a message declared by a prophet.

The term is used both literally and figuratively throughout the Bible.
