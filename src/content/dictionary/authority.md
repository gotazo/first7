## Meaning

Authority is the right or power to command, govern, or make decisions.

It may refer to the legal or rightful ability to direct, rule, or exercise control.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, authority refers to the right or power to rule, govern, command, or act.

It is attributed to God, delegated to individuals and governing powers, and exercised in religious and civil contexts.
