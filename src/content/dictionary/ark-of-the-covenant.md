## Meaning

The Ark of the Covenant was a sacred chest that contained the testimony and was kept in the Most Holy Place.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the Ark of the Covenant was constructed according to God's instructions and placed in the Most Holy Place of the tabernacle and later the temple.

It is associated with God's covenant with Israel.
