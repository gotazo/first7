## Meaning

Kindred refers to people connected by family relationship or common descent.

---

## Biblical Usage

The Bible uses **kindred** for relatives and wider groups connected through family or ancestry. The term can refer to a person's immediate or extended family and, in some contexts, to a broader ancestral group.

Kindred is important in biblical accounts involving marriage, inheritance, family obligations, and the movement of people from one land or family group to another.
