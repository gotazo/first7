## Meaning

The Garden of Eden is the garden planted by God in Eden.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the Garden of Eden is the garden planted by God in Eden.

It was the home of Adam and Eve and contained the Tree of Life and the Tree of the Knowledge of Good and Evil.

After Adam and Eve were driven out, cherubim were placed to guard the way to the Tree of Life.
