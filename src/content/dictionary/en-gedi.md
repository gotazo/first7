## Meaning

A place name meaning “spring of the kid” or “spring of the young goat,” associated with an oasis and wilderness area near the western shore of the Dead Sea.

---

## Biblical Usage

En-gedi was a settlement and wilderness area in the territory of Judah. It was known for its springs and surrounding rocky wilderness.

David and his men hid in the strongholds of En-gedi while Saul pursued them. Later Scripture identifies En-gedi among the towns associated with Judah and refers to its vineyards and wilderness.

---

## Key References

- Joshua 15:62 — En-gedi is listed among the cities of Judah.
- 1 Samuel 24:1 — David is in the wilderness of En-gedi while Saul pursues him.
- 1 Samuel 24:2 — Saul searches for David among the rocks of the wild goats.
- 2 Chronicles 20:2 — En-gedi is identified geographically in relation to the Dead Sea.
- Song of Solomon 1:14 — The vineyards of En-gedi are mentioned.
- Ezekiel 47:10 — En-gedi is mentioned in the prophetic description of the waters.
- Ezekiel 47:12 — Trees associated with the waters are described in the same geographical context.
