## Meaning

A mountain is a natural elevation of the earth's surface rising prominently above the surrounding land. *Mount Sinai* is the name of a mountain associated with the biblical account of Israel's journey from Egypt.

## Biblical Usage

Mount Sinai is where the LORD descended in the sight of the people of Israel and where Moses received the commandments and the tables of testimony (Exodus 19:11, 20; 31:18).

The Israelites camped before the mountain after arriving in the wilderness of Sinai (Exodus 19:1-2). The mountain is also associated with the covenant made with Israel and is referred to in later biblical books (Deuteronomy 5:2; Nehemiah 9:13; Hebrews 12:18-21).

## Key References

- **Exodus 19:11** — The LORD was to come down upon Mount Sinai in the sight of the people.
- **Exodus 19:20** — The LORD came down upon the mountain and called Moses up.
- **Exodus 31:18** — Moses received the two tables of testimony.
- **Deuteronomy 5:2** — The LORD made a covenant with Israel in Horeb.
- **Nehemiah 9:13** — God came down upon Mount Sinai and spoke with Israel.
