## Meaning

Peace is a state of calm, harmony, or freedom from conflict.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, peace refers to harmony, well-being, safety, and reconciliation.

The term is used of relationships between people, peace with God, and the absence of conflict, depending on the context.
