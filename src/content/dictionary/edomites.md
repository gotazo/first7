## Meaning

Edomites are the descendants of Esau who inhabited the region of Edom.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the Edomites were the descendants of Esau, the son of
Isaac and brother of Jacob.

Esau was also called Edom, and his descendants became a people and
nation in the region of Seir.
