## Meaning

A meal offering is an offering made from grain or fine flour and presented
to God according to the law of Moses.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the meal offering was made from fine flour and was
accompanied by oil and, in the prescribed form, frankincense.

A portion was burned upon the altar, while the remainder belonged to the
priests according to the instructions of the law.

The KJV commonly calls this offering a "meat offering," using "meat" in
its older sense of food.
