## Meaning

A long weapon with a pointed head, used for thrusting or throwing.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the spear is a weapon used in warfare and personal combat.

Spears appear among the weapons carried by soldiers, warriors, kings, and military commanders. Scripture also mentions related pointed weapons such as javelins and lances.

In Joshua 8, Joshua stretches out the spear in his hand toward Ai as a signal during the battle.
