## Meaning

A scapegoat is a person or thing that is blamed or made to bear the fault
or consequences of others.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the scapegoat was the goat selected on the Day of Atonement
and sent into the wilderness after the high priest had confessed over it
the iniquities of the children of Israel.

The account is given in Leviticus 16.
