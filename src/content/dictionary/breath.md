## Meaning

Breath is the air taken into and released from the lungs during breathing.

It is essential for the life of living creatures.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, breath refers to the breath of living creatures and is associated with life.

The term is also used in connection with God's breath and the giving or taking of life.
