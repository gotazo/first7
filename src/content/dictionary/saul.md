## Meaning

A Hebrew male personal name meaning “asked for” or “asked,” and a name borne by more than one biblical person.

---

## Biblical Usage

Saul was the son of Kish of the tribe of Benjamin and the first king of Israel. Samuel anointed him after the people asked for a king, and Saul ruled Israel during a period of conflict with the Philistines and other enemies.

Scripture also records other people named Saul, including Saul the king of Edom and Saul of the tribe of Simeon. The New Testament later uses Saul as the Hebrew name of the apostle Paul.
