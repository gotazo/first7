## Meaning

A precept is a rule, instruction, or principle given to guide conduct.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, a precept is an instruction, rule, or principle given for
conduct or obedience.

The term is especially prominent in the Psalms in reference to God's
instructions and commands.

It can also refer more generally to an established principle or
instruction.
