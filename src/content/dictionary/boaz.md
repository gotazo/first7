## Meaning

A biblical Hebrew proper name of uncertain etymology; the precise meaning of the name is not established with certainty.

---

## Biblical Usage

Boaz was a kinsman of Elimelech and a prominent man of Bethlehem. Ruth came to his field to glean, and Boaz instructed his reapers to allow her to gather safely and generously.

As a near kinsman, Boaz became involved in the redemption of Naomi's family inheritance. He married Ruth and became the father of Obed, who became the father of Jesse.
