## Meaning

To heave is to lift, raise, or move something upward with effort.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, heave is used in connection with the ceremonial setting
apart of portions of offerings for the LORD.

A heave offering was a portion separated from certain sacrifices or gifts
and given for the service of the LORD and His priests.
