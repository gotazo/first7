## Meaning

A place name meaning “garden-land,” “orchard,” or “fruitful field”; the name is used for more than one place in Scripture.

---

## Biblical Usage

Carmel most prominently refers to Mount Carmel, a prominent mountain or ridge on the Mediterranean coast in northern Israel. It is the setting of Elijah's confrontation with the prophets of Baal.

Scripture also refers to Carmel as a town or district in the territory of Judah. This Carmel was associated with Nabal and Abigail and was visited by David while he was in the wilderness of Paran.

---

- 2 Kings 4:25 — The Shunammite woman goes to Mount Carmel.

- Isaiah 35:2 — Carmel is used in a description of flourishing and fruitful land.
