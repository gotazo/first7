## Meaning

A trespass offering was an offering presented according to God's commandments in connection with certain trespasses or offenses.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the trespass offering was one of the offerings prescribed under the Law of Moses.

It was presented in accordance with God's commandments for specific trespasses and included restitution where required.
