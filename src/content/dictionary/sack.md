## Meaning

A sack is a large bag or container used for carrying, storing, or
transporting goods.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, a sack was a bag or container used for carrying goods,
food, or other possessions.

In Genesis, Joseph's brothers carried grain in sacks during the famine
in Canaan and Egypt.

The word is also used in expressions involving mourning and repentance.
