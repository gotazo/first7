## Meaning

Forever means for all time, without an end, or for an indefinitely long period according to context.

---

## Biblical Usage

Scripture uses *for ever* to express continuing duration or permanence. It appears in declarations concerning God's word and mercy, in promises, and in descriptions of enduring conditions.

In 2 Samuel 7:13-16, the term appears in God's promise concerning David's house, kingdom, and throne. Elsewhere, Scripture uses it in statements about God's word and in descriptions of the duration of a kingdom or condition. The intended duration should be understood from the context rather than assumed to be identical in every occurrence.

---

## Key References

- **2 Samuel 7:13-16** — Uses *for ever* in the promise concerning David's house, kingdom, and throne.
- **1 Chronicles 17:12-14** — Records the promise concerning David's son and the establishment of his kingdom.
- **Psalm 89:28-29** — Describes the continuance of God's covenant with David and his seed.
- **Psalm 119:89** — Declares that the LORD's word is settled in heaven for ever.
- **Isaiah 40:8** — Contrasts the fading of grass and flowers with the enduring word of God.
- **Daniel 7:14** — Describes dominion that shall not pass away and a kingdom that shall not be destroyed.
- **John 6:51** — Records Jesus speaking of the living bread and eternal life.
- **Revelation 22:5** — Describes the reign of the servants of God for ever and ever.
