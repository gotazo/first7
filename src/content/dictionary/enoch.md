## Meaning

Enoch is a Hebrew proper name meaning "dedicated" or "trained."

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, one person named Enoch was a descendant of Seth who walked with God.

Genesis also mentions another person named Enoch, the son of Cain.

Enoch is mentioned in both the Old and New Testaments.
