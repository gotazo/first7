## Meaning

Doorposts are the upright parts at the sides of a doorway or gate. In the King James Version (KJV), related expressions include “side posts,” “door post,” and “posts.”

The Hebrew word מְזוּזָה (*mezuzah*) means a doorpost or gatepost.

## Biblical Usage

In Exodus 12:7, 22–23, the Israelites were instructed to apply the Passover blood to the side posts and the upper doorpost of their houses. Exodus 21:6 mentions a doorpost in the instruction concerning a servant who chose to remain with his master.

Deuteronomy 6:9 and 11:20 instruct the Israelites to write God's commandments upon the posts of their houses and gates. Judges 16:3 records Samson carrying away the gates of Gaza with their posts.

Posts are also mentioned in descriptions of the temple and its doorways (1 Kings 6:31, 33; 7:5) and in Ezekiel's visions (Ezekiel 41:21; 43:8; 45:19; 46:2). Proverbs 8:34 and Isaiah 57:8 contain further references to doors and their posts.

## Key References

- **Exodus 12:7** — The Israelites were instructed to apply the Passover blood to the side posts and upper doorpost.
- **Exodus 12:22–23** — The blood was placed on the lintel and side posts.
- **Deuteronomy 6:9** — God's words were to be written upon the posts of the house and gates.
- **Judges 16:3** — Samson carried away the gates of Gaza with their posts.
- **1 Kings 6:31, 33** — Posts are mentioned in the description of the temple's doorways.
- **Ezekiel 45:19** — Posts are mentioned in the instructions concerning the house and gate.

## Related Terms

- **Lintel** — The horizontal part above a doorway.
- **Passover** — The observance associated with the blood placed on the doorposts and lintel in Egypt.
