## Meaning

Consecration is the act of setting apart or dedicating a person or thing
for a sacred or special purpose.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, consecration refers to the act of setting apart persons,
objects, places, or offerings for God or for sacred service.

The Bible especially uses the term in connection with the priesthood, the
tabernacle, the altar, and other things dedicated to God.
