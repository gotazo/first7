## Meaning

Teraphim are household images or objects associated with false worship and, in some passages, divination. Their exact physical form and function are not fully described in Scripture.

---

## Biblical Usage

Teraphim are first mentioned in the account of Rachel taking the household images belonging to Laban. They later appear in connection with Micah's household shrine and the Danites.

Later passages associate teraphim with practices condemned by the LORD, including false worship and seeking supernatural guidance apart from Him. Scripture does not provide enough information to establish one exact physical form or function for all references to teraphim.
