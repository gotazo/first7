## Meaning

Bloodguilt is responsibility or liability for bloodshed, especially the unlawful taking of human life.

---

## Biblical Usage

Scripture connects bloodguilt with responsibility for the shedding of human blood. The term concerns liability for bloodshed rather than merely the physical act of killing.

In 2 Samuel 3:28-29, David declares himself and his kingdom guiltless concerning the blood of Abner after Joab kills him. Psalm 51:14 uses the word *bloodguiltiness* in David's plea for deliverance from guilt associated with bloodshed.

---

## Key References

- **Genesis 9:5-6** — Addresses accountability for the shedding of human blood.
- **Numbers 35:33** — Connects the shedding of blood with defilement of the land.
- **Deuteronomy 19:10** — Addresses the prevention of innocent bloodshed.
- **2 Samuel 3:28-29** — Records David's declaration concerning responsibility for Abner's death.
- **Psalm 51:14** — Records a plea for deliverance from bloodguiltiness.
- **Isaiah 1:15** — Describes hands full of blood in a rebuke concerning wrongdoing.
- **Joel 3:21** — Refers to the cleansing of blood that had not been cleansed.
