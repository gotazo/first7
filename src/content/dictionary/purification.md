## Meaning

Purification is the process of removing impurity or making something pure.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, purification refers to the process of removing impurity or
making a person or thing pure.

The Bible uses purification in ceremonial, physical, moral, and spiritual
contexts, including preparation for worship and cleansing from sin or
uncleanness.
