## Meaning

Benjamin is a Hebrew proper name meaning "son of the right hand."

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Benjamin was the youngest son of Jacob and Rachel.

Rachel died while giving birth to him. She initially called him Benoni, but Jacob called him Benjamin.

Benjamin became the ancestor of the tribe of Benjamin.
