## Meaning

Abel is a Hebrew proper name traditionally understood to mean "breath," "vapor," or "vanity."

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Abel is the second son of Adam and Eve and the younger brother of Cain.

He was a keeper of sheep and brought an offering to the LORD.

Abel is mentioned in both the Old and New Testaments.
