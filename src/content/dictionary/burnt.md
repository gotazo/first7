## Meaning

Burnt means damaged, spoiled, or made black by fire, heat, or overcooking.

It is the past tense and past participle of "burn."

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, burnt is used in its ordinary sense of something affected by
fire and in descriptions of things consumed or offered by fire.

The term is also used in the expression "burnt offering," referring to a
particular sacrifice prescribed in the law.
