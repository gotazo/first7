## Meaning

Burial is the act or process of placing a dead person in a grave, tomb, or other resting place.

---

## Biblical Usage

Scripture records burial in caves, graves, family tombs, and other designated places. Accounts may mention the preparation of the body, the people who carried out the burial, and the mourning that accompanied it.

Genesis 50:13 records the burial of Jacob in the cave of Machpelah. In 2 Samuel 3:31-32, David commands mourning for Abner, who is buried in Hebron. The New Testament records the burial of Jesus Christ and other individuals.
