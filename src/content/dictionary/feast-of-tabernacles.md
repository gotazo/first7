## Meaning

The Feast of Tabernacles is an appointed feast during which the Israelites lived in temporary shelters in remembrance of their journey after leaving Egypt.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the Feast of Tabernacles was observed for seven days in the seventh month.

During the feast, the Israelites dwelt in booths according to God's commandments and rejoiced before the LORD.
