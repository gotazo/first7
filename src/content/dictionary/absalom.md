## Meaning

Absalom is a Hebrew masculine personal name meaning “father of peace.”

---

## Biblical Usage

Absalom was a son of David and the brother of Tamar. After Amnon assaulted Tamar, Absalom arranged Amnon's death and fled. David later permitted Absalom to return to Jerusalem.

Absalom subsequently sought the support of the people and rebelled against David. During the battle between David's forces and Absalom's men, Joab killed Absalom. David mourned deeply when he learned of his son's death.
