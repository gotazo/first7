## Meaning

A wave offering was an offering presented before the LORD by being waved according to God's commandments.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the wave offering was presented before the LORD by waving it as prescribed under the Law of Moses.

It is mentioned in connection with priestly ordination, peace offerings, and the Feast of Weeks.
