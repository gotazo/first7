## Meaning

A garment is an article of clothing worn to cover or protect the body.

---

## Biblical Usage

Scripture mentions garments in descriptions of ordinary clothing, family life, royal dress, priestly service, and mourning. The kind of garment and its significance depend on the passage.

In 2 Samuel 13:18, Tamar wears a garment of divers colours, described as the clothing worn by the king's virgin daughters. Other passages describe garments being torn in grief, worn by priests, or used in figurative descriptions.

---

## Key References

- **Genesis 3:21** — God makes coats of skins for Adam and his wife.
- **Genesis 37:3** — Jacob makes Joseph a coat of many colours.
- **Exodus 28:2** — Describes holy garments made for Aaron.
- **2 Samuel 13:18-19** — Describes Tamar's distinctive garment and her response after the wrong done to her.
- **1 Kings 22:30** — Records the king of Israel disguising himself for battle.
- **Psalm 104:2** — Uses clothing figuratively in a description of the LORD's majesty.
- **Matthew 27:35** — Records the dividing of Jesus' garments.
- **Revelation 19:8** — Uses fine linen as a description associated with the righteousness of saints.

---

## Related Terms

- **Clothing** — Items worn to cover the body.
- **Robe** — A long, loose garment.
- **Tunic** — A garment worn over the body, often extending toward the knees.
- **Sackcloth** — Coarse cloth worn or used as an expression of mourning or distress.
