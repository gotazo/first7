## Meaning

To consecrate is to set apart or dedicate a person or thing for a sacred
or special purpose.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, consecrate means to set apart or dedicate persons, objects,
places, or offerings for God or for a particular sacred purpose.

The term is used in connection with priests, the tabernacle, the altar,
sacrifices, and other things dedicated to God.
