## Meaning

Hebron is a place name associated with fellowship or association.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Hebron was a city in the land of Canaan associated with
Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.

Abraham dwelt near Hebron, and Sarah was buried there in the cave of
Machpelah.

Hebron later became a city of Judah and was associated with David's reign
over Judah and Israel.
