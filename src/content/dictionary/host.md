## Meaning

A host is a large organized group, especially an army or multitude.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, host refers to an organized group or multitude, especially
an army or military force.

The term is also used for the organized divisions of Israel and for the
heavenly host of angels or other heavenly beings.

The expression "LORD of hosts" refers to the LORD as ruler over hosts.
