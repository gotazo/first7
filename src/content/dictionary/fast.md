## Meaning

To fast is to voluntarily abstain from food, or from food and drink, for a period of time.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, fasting refers to voluntarily abstaining from food for a time.

Fasts are recorded during times of prayer, mourning, repentance, seeking God, and special occasions.
