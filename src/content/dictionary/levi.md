## Meaning

Levi is a Hebrew proper name associated with "joined" or "attached."

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Levi was the third son of Jacob and Leah.

He was the brother of Simeon, Judah, and the other sons of Jacob.

Levi became the ancestor of the tribe of Levi, which was set apart for
service connected with the tabernacle and later the temple.
