## Meaning

Haran is a place name of uncertain meaning.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Haran was a place where Terah settled with Abram, Sarai,
and Lot after leaving Ur of the Chaldees.

Abram departed from Haran after the LORD called him to go into the land
of Canaan.

Haran was also associated with the family of Rebekah, Isaac's wife.
