## Meaning

Haran is a Hebrew and biblical masculine proper name traditionally
associated with "mountaineer."

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Haran was a son of Terah and the brother of Abram and Nahor.

He was the father of Lot, Milcah, and Iscah.

Haran died in Ur of the Chaldees during the lifetime of his father Terah.
