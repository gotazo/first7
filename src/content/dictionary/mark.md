## Meaning

A mark is a sign, impression, symbol, or visible indication used to identify or distinguish something.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, mark refers to a sign, token, impression, or identifying symbol.

The term is used in various contexts throughout the Bible, including protection, identification, and prophecy.
