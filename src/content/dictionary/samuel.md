## Meaning

A Hebrew male personal name; its exact etymology is uncertain, and 1 Samuel 1:20 explains the name in connection with Hannah's asking of the LORD.

---

## Biblical Usage

Samuel was the son of Elkanah and Hannah. Hannah dedicated him to the LORD, and he was brought to Eli at Shiloh after he was weaned.

Samuel served the LORD from his youth, became a prophet and judge in Israel, and was used in the anointing of Saul and David as kings. He continued to speak to Israel and its rulers as a prophet and died after a lifetime of service in Israel.
