## Meaning

Nadab is a Hebrew proper name meaning "generous," "noble," or "willing."

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Nadab was the firstborn son of Aaron and Elisheba and was
appointed to the priesthood with his brothers.

He and his brother Abihu died after offering strange fire before the
LORD.
