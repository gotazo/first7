## Meaning

Carnal means relating to the flesh or physical nature.

The word may describe things that belong to the natural or earthly realm.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, carnal describes that which pertains to the flesh, human nature, or worldly things.

The term is used in contrast to spiritual matters in several New Testament passages.
