## Meaning

Salvation is deliverance, rescue, or preservation from danger or destruction.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, salvation refers to deliverance, rescue, preservation, or redemption.

The term is used throughout both the Old and New Testaments in various contexts according to each passage.
