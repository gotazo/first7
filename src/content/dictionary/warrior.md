## Meaning

A person who fights in warfare or is skilled in battle.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, warriors are people engaged in warfare or recognized for military strength and ability.

Israel's armies included mighty men of valour who fought in battles against opposing peoples. The Bible also uses military language to describe God's power and actions.
