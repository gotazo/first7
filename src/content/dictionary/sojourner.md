## Meaning

A sojourner is a person who lives temporarily in a place without being
a permanent resident.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, a sojourner is a person who lives temporarily in a land
or among a people without being a native inhabitant.

Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, and their descendants are described as
sojourners, and the Law also gives instructions concerning the
treatment of the sojourner among Israel.
