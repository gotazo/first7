## Meaning

The Day of Atonement is an annual appointed day observed by Israel according to God's commandments.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the Day of Atonement was observed on the tenth day of the seventh month.

On that day the high priest carried out duties prescribed by God, including entering the Most Holy Place.

The people were commanded to afflict their souls and refrain from work.
