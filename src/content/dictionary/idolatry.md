## Meaning

Idolatry is the worship of idols or the giving of worship to anyone or anything other than God.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, idolatry refers to the worship of idols, false gods, or anything that takes the place of God.

It is consistently condemned throughout both the Old and New Testaments.
