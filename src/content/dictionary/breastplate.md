## Meaning

A breastplate is a piece of armor worn to protect the chest.

It may also refer to a ceremonial breastplate worn as part of special clothing.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, a breastplate refers to protective armor worn in battle.

The term is also used for the high priest's breastplate and in figurative descriptions of spiritual armor.
