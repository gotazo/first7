## Meaning

Passover is an appointed feast commemorating the LORD's passing over the houses of the Israelites in Egypt.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the Passover was established by God before Israel's departure from Egypt.

It commemorated the LORD's passing over the houses of the Israelites when the firstborn of Egypt were struck and became one of Israel's appointed feasts.
