## Meaning

A meal is food eaten at a particular time or on a particular occasion.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, meal refers generally to food eaten by people.

In the KJV, the word is also used in the expression "meat offering" for
an offering made from fine flour and other prescribed ingredients.

The Hebrew term translated "meat offering" in the KJV refers to a grain
or meal offering rather than a sacrifice of animal flesh.
