## Meaning

A plague is a severe affliction, calamity, or destructive judgment.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, plague refers to severe affliction or judgment brought upon
people or nations.

In Exodus, the LORD brought plagues upon Egypt because Pharaoh refused to
let the children of Israel go.

The plagues included water turned to blood, frogs, lice, flies, pestilence,
boils, hail, locusts, darkness, and the death of the firstborn.
