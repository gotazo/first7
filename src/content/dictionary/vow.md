## Meaning

A vow is a solemn promise or commitment to do or refrain from doing
something.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, a vow is a solemn promise made to God concerning an action,
offering, service, or abstention.

The Bible gives instructions concerning the making, keeping, and fulfillment
of vows and warns against making vows carelessly or failing to fulfill them.
