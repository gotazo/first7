## Meaning

Gilgal is the name of several places mentioned in the Old Testament, including the Israelite camp near Jericho after the crossing of Jordan.

---

## Biblical Usage

The most prominent Gilgal in Joshua was the Israelite camp east of Jericho after the crossing of the Jordan. There Joshua circumcised the children of Israel, kept the Passover, and the manna ceased after they ate the produce of the land.

Other places called Gilgal appear in Scripture, so each occurrence must be understood from its context. The name is associated with several events involving Israel's leaders, worship, and prophetic ministry.
