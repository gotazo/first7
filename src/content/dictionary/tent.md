## Meaning

A tent is a temporary or movable shelter made of cloth, leather, or
other material.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, tents were used as dwellings and temporary shelters.

Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, and their households lived in tents during their
journeys.

Tents are also mentioned in connection with Israel's wilderness journey
and the tabernacle.
