## Meaning

A pillar is an upright column or post used to support, mark, or
commemorate something.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, pillars were used for different purposes.

They could serve as memorials or markers, support structures, or form
part of buildings.

Jacob set up pillars as memorials at several places. Pillars were also
used in the construction of the tabernacle and the temple.
