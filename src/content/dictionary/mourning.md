## Meaning

Mourning is the expression of grief or sorrow, especially following the death of someone.

---

## Biblical Usage

Scripture uses *mourning* for grief following death, loss, calamity, and other distressing events. Its outward expressions may include weeping, fasting, tearing garments, and wearing sackcloth.

In 2 Samuel 1:11-12, David and his men mourn after hearing that Saul, Jonathan, and others of Israel have fallen by the sword. In 2 Samuel 3:31-34, David commands mourning for Abner following his death.

---

## Key References

- **Genesis 37:34-35** — Records Jacob's mourning for Joseph.
- **2 Samuel 1:11-12** — David and his men mourn for Saul, Jonathan, and Israel.
- **2 Samuel 3:31-34** — David orders mourning for Abner.
- **2 Samuel 13:31-37** — Records mourning following the events involving Amnon and Absalom.
- **Job 1:20** — Job expresses grief after receiving news of his losses.
- **Ecclesiastes 3:4** — Identifies a time to weep and a time to mourn.
- **Matthew 5:4** — Records the Lord's words concerning those that mourn.

## Related Terms

- **Grief** — Deep sorrow, especially following a loss.
- **Lamentation** — An expression or composition of sorrow.
- **Weeping** — The shedding of tears as an expression of emotion.
- **Sackcloth** — Coarse cloth worn in Scripture as an expression of grief or distress.
