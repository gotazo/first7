## Meaning

A blessing is a gift, favor, benefit, or expression of goodwill.

It may refer to something beneficial received or words expressing goodwill.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, blessing refers to God's favor, benefits, or words of blessing pronounced upon individuals, families, or nations.

It is also used of people speaking blessings over others.
