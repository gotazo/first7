## Meaning

A blessing is a favor, benefit, or expression of good bestowed upon
another.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, blessing may refer to a favor or benefit given to a
person, an expression of good spoken over another, or a benefit received
from God.

Blessings are spoken, given, received, and passed from one generation to
another in the biblical record.
