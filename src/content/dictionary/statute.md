## Meaning

A statute is an established rule, law, or decree prescribed by an authority.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, a statute is an established rule, regulation, or decree given
by an authority.

The term is frequently used for commands and regulations given by God to
Israel concerning worship, conduct, justice, and the ordering of the nation.

Statutes may also refer to established rules or decrees made by earthly
rulers.
