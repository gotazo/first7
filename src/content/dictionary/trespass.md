## Meaning

A trespass is an unlawful or wrongful act that violates a boundary, right,
law, or duty.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, trespass refers to an offense or wrong committed against God
or another person.

The term is also used in the KJV for certain offenses requiring atonement
and a trespass offering under the law.
