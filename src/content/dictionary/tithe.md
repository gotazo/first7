## Meaning

A tithe is a tenth part of produce, possessions, or income given according to God's commandments.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the tithe was the giving of a tenth according to God's commandments.

Tithes are mentioned before the Law, under the Law of Moses, and in the New Testament.
