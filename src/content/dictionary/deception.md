## Meaning

Deception is the act of misleading, deceiving, or causing someone to believe what is false.

It involves presenting something false as though it were true.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, deception refers to misleading others through falsehood, error, or deceit.

The term is used of human deception, false teaching, and other forms of deceit described throughout the Bible.
