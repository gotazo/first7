## Meaning

Mamre is a place associated with Hebron where Abraham dwelt.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Mamre was a place near Hebron where Abraham lived.

The LORD appeared to Abraham there, and Abraham built an altar there.

Mamre was also associated with the field and cave of Machpelah where
Sarah was buried.

Isaac and Jacob were later associated with the same area.
