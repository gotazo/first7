## Meaning

A spy is a person sent secretly to observe, investigate, or gather
information about a place, people, or situation.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, a spy is a person sent to secretly observe or investigate a
land, city, army, or people and report what was discovered.

The Bible records spies being sent to examine the land of Canaan, as well as
other instances involving military or political intelligence.
