## Meaning

A shield is a defensive object carried or worn to protect the body from weapons or attacks.

---

## Biblical Usage

Scripture describes shields as military equipment used to defend warriors in battle. They appear in accounts of armed men, descriptions of armour, and narratives involving kings and armies.

The word is also used figuratively. The LORD is described as a shield in passages concerning protection, while Ephesians 6:16 uses the shield as part of the description of spiritual armour.

---

## Key References

- **1 Samuel 17:7** — Goliath's armour-bearer carries his shield.
- **1 Samuel 17:45** — David approaches Goliath in the name of the LORD of hosts.
- **2 Samuel 1:21** — David's lament refers to Saul's shield.
- **2 Samuel 22:3** — David describes the God of his salvation as his shield.
- **Psalm 3:3** — The LORD is described as a shield.
- **Psalm 18:2** — David calls the LORD his shield and deliverer.
- **Proverbs 30:5** — Describes God as a shield to those who put their trust in Him.
- **Ephesians 6:16** — Refers to the shield of faith.
