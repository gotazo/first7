## Meaning

To sanctify is to make holy, set apart, or treat as holy.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, sanctify means to make holy, set apart for a sacred purpose,
or declare and treat as holy.

The term is used of God, people, places, objects, days, and things
dedicated to Him.

Scripture also uses sanctify in relation to God's work in His people and
their conduct toward Him.
