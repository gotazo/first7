## Meaning

A peace offering was an offering presented according to God's commandments as part of Israel's worship.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the peace offering was one of the offerings prescribed under the Law of Moses.

It was presented in accordance with God's commandments and could be offered for thanksgiving, vows, or freewill offerings.
