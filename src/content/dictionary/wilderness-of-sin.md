## Meaning

The wilderness of Sin is a wilderness region between Elim and Sinai.

The meaning of the place name Sin is uncertain.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the wilderness of Sin was the region between Elim and Sinai
where the children of Israel arrived during their journey from Egypt.

There the congregation murmured because of a lack of food, and God
provided manna and quail.
