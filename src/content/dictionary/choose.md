## Meaning

To choose is to select or decide in favor of one person, thing, course, or group from among alternatives.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, choose refers to the act of selecting or deciding in favor of a person, people, thing, place, or course of action. Choices may be made by individuals, leaders, or groups.

Scripture also describes God's choosing of people, places, and purposes. The term therefore occurs in contexts ranging from ordinary decisions to national and religious selection.
