## Meaning

Possession is something owned, held, or obtained as property, or the state
of having or controlling something.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, possession refers to something owned, held, or acquired,
and especially to land or property given to a person or people.

The Bible frequently uses the term in connection with Israel receiving the
land promised by God.

It is also used more broadly for property, inheritance, and things belonging
to individuals or families.
