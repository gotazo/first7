## Meaning

A **landmark** is a physical marker or established point used to identify the limit of a property or area.

---

## Biblical Usage

In the Bible, a landmark is a marker used to preserve the established boundary of an inherited possession. Under Israel's law, removing a neighbour's landmark was prohibited because doing so could unlawfully change the extent of another person's property.

The Bible speaks of the **ancient landmark** established by earlier generations. The term therefore refers not merely to a geographical point, but to an established marker that preserves a recognized boundary.

A landmark is different from a **boundary** itself. The boundary is the limit of the area; the landmark is the physical marker used to identify that limit.

---

## Key References

**Deuteronomy 19:14** — Israel was commanded not to remove a neighbour's landmark from his inheritance.

**Proverbs 22:28** — The ancient landmark established by the fathers was not to be removed.

**Proverbs 23:10** — The ancient landmark was again associated with protection of inherited fields and the property of the fatherless.
