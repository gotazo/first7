## Meaning

A Hebrew male personal name commonly understood to mean “beloved”; the name is chiefly associated with David, son of Jesse and king of Israel.

---

## Biblical Usage

David was the youngest son of Jesse of Bethlehem. He was a shepherd, musician, warrior, and servant of Saul before becoming king over Israel.

David was anointed by Samuel, defeated Goliath, became king first over Judah and later over all Israel, and established Jerusalem as his royal city. Scripture records his military victories, family history, failures, repentance, and his covenant with the LORD concerning his house.
