## Meaning

Amram is a Hebrew proper name traditionally understood as "exalted
people" or "people of exaltation."

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Amram was a son of Kohath, a descendant of Levi, and the
husband of Jochebed.

He was the father of Aaron, Moses, and Miriam.

Amram is named in the genealogical records of Exodus and Chronicles.
