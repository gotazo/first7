## Meaning

The Table of Shewbread was a table in the Holy Place that held the shewbread before the LORD.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the Table of Shewbread stood in the Holy Place of the tabernacle and later the temple.

It held the shewbread according to God's commandments.
