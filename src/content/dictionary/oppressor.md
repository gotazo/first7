## Meaning

An oppressor is a person or group that subjects others to unjust, cruel, or abusive treatment.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, an oppressor is a person, ruler, nation, or other group that subjects others to hardship, exploitation, violence, or unjust control. Oppressors may exercise authority over individuals or whole populations.

Scripture describes both foreign powers that oppressed Israel and individuals who abused the weak or used authority unjustly. The term identifies the agent responsible for oppression, while oppression describes the unjust treatment itself.
