## Meaning

Mephibosheth is a Hebrew masculine personal name. Its precise etymology is uncertain, so a definite English meaning should not be assigned without stronger evidence.

---

## Biblical Usage

The best-known person named Mephibosheth was the son of Jonathan and grandson of Saul. He was lame in both feet after an accident in childhood. David sought him out because of his relationship with Jonathan, restored Saul's land to him, and gave him a permanent place at the king's table.

A different man named Mephibosheth was a son of Saul and Rizpah. He is mentioned in the account concerning the descendants of Saul put to death during David's reign.

---

## Key References

- **2 Samuel 4:4** — Describes Jonathan's son and his lameness.
- **2 Samuel 9:1–13** — David restores land to Jonathan's son Mephibosheth and receives him at his table.
- **2 Samuel 16:1–4** — Ziba speaks to David about Mephibosheth during Absalom's rebellion.
- **2 Samuel 19:24–30** — Mephibosheth meets David after the rebellion.
- **2 Samuel 21:7–9** — Mentions another Mephibosheth, a son of Saul and Rizpah.

## Related Terms

- **Jonathan** — The father of the Mephibosheth whom David received into his household.
- **Saul** — Jonathan's father and the grandfather of Jonathan's son.
- **David** — The king who showed kindness to Jonathan's son.
- **Ziba** — Saul's servant who served Mephibosheth's household.
