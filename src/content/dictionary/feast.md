## Meaning

A feast is a special occasion or celebration marked by a meal, gathering, or festival.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, feasts are appointed times of celebration or observance.

Some feasts were established by God for Israel, while others refer to meals, celebrations, or festive gatherings in various biblical contexts.
