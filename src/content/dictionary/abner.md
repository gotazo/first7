## Meaning

Abner is a Hebrew masculine personal name commonly understood to mean “my father is a lamp” or “father of light.”

---

## Biblical Usage

Abner was the son of Ner and the captain of Saul's army. After Saul's death, he made Ish-bosheth king over Israel while David reigned over Judah. Abner later negotiated with David concerning the kingdom.

Joab killed Abner at Hebron in revenge for the death of Asahel, Joab's brother. David publicly mourned Abner and condemned his death.
