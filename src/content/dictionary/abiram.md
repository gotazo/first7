## Meaning

Abiram is a Hebrew proper name traditionally understood as "my father
is exalted" or "father of exaltation."

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Abiram was a son of Eliab of the tribe of Reuben.

He joined Korah and Dathan in opposing Moses and Aaron in the
wilderness and was among those judged by the LORD during the rebellion.
