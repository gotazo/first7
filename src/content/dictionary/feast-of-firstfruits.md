## Meaning

The Feast of Firstfruits is an appointed feast in which the firstfruits of the harvest were presented before the LORD.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the Feast of Firstfruits was observed by presenting the first sheaf of the harvest before the LORD according to God's commandments.

It is listed among the appointed feasts of Israel.
