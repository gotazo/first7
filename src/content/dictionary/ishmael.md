## Meaning

Ishmael is a Hebrew proper name meaning "God hears."

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Ishmael was the son of Abraham and Hagar, Sarah's Egyptian maidservant.

God promised Abraham that Ishmael would become the father of a great nation.

Ishmael later became the father of twelve princes.
