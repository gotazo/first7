## Meaning

Amorites are a people who inhabited various parts of the land of Canaan
and surrounding regions.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the Amorites were one of the peoples inhabiting the land
of Canaan.

They are mentioned among the peoples of the land promised to Abraham and
later encountered by the children of Israel.
