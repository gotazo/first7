## Meaning

A tower is a tall structure built to rise above its surroundings.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, towers were constructed for purposes such as defense, observation, protection, and habitation.

They appear throughout the Bible in historical accounts, parables, and prophetic passages.

One of the best-known towers in Scripture is the Tower of Babel.
