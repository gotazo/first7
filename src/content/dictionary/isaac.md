## Meaning

Isaac is a Hebrew proper name meaning "he laughs" or "laughter."

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Isaac was the son of Abraham and Sarah, the father of Esau and Jacob, and the husband of Rebekah.

His name was given because Sarah laughed when she heard that she would bear a son in her old age.

Isaac is mentioned in both the Old and New Testaments.
