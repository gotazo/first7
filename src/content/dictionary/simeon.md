## Meaning

Simeon is a Hebrew proper name meaning "hearing" or "heard."

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Simeon was the second son of Jacob and Leah.

He was the brother of Reuben, Levi, Judah, and the other sons of Jacob.

Simeon became the ancestor of the tribe of Simeon.
