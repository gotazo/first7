## Meaning

Beersheba is a Hebrew place name associated with "well of the oath" or
"well of seven."

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Beersheba was a place where Abraham made a covenant with
Abimelech after a dispute concerning a well.

The name is associated with the oath and the seven ewe lambs involved in
the account.

Beersheba later became an important location associated with Abraham,
Isaac, and Jacob.
