## Meaning

A tabernacle is a portable sanctuary or tent used for worship.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the tabernacle was the portable sanctuary built by the Israelites according to God's instructions.

It served as the place where God met with His people and where priests carried out the duties of worship during Israel's journey through the wilderness.
