## Meaning

The soul is the immaterial or spiritual part of a person.

The word may also refer to a person's life or inner being.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the word soul is used in various ways, including to refer to a living person, the inner being, life itself, or an individual, depending on the context.
