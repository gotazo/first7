## Meaning

Shechem is a Hebrew place name meaning "shoulder" or "ridge." A city and region in the land of Canaan.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Shechem was a city and region in the land of Canaan.

Abraham passed through the area, Jacob settled near the city, and
Joseph was later associated with the burial place of his bones at
Shechem.

Shechem appears throughout the Old Testament and is also mentioned in
the New Testament.
