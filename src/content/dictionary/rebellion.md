## Meaning

Rebellion is an act of resisting, opposing, or refusing to obey an authority
or established rule.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, rebellion refers to deliberate resistance or disobedience
against an authority, especially against God and His commandments.

The Bible uses the term for individuals and groups who reject God's
instruction, oppose His appointed servants, or turn away from His ways.
