## Meaning

A siege is a military operation in which an army surrounds or blocks a fortified place to capture it, force its surrender, or prevent supplies and assistance from reaching it.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, a siege is used in warfare against cities and fortified places. Besieging forces may surround a city, construct earthworks, and attack its walls to gain entry.

The Bible records sieges during conflicts involving Israel and other nations. Jerusalem was besieged by Babylon before its fall (2 Kings 25:1-4). Siege imagery also appears in prophetic passages and in Jesus' description of the coming encirclement of Jerusalem (Luke 19:43).
