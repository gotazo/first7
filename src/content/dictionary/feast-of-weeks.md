## Meaning

The Feast of Weeks is an appointed feast observed after counting seven weeks from the Feast of Firstfruits.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the Feast of Weeks was one of the appointed feasts of Israel.

It was observed after counting seven weeks from the Feast of Firstfruits and included offerings presented before the LORD according to God's commandments.

In the New Testament, the feast is referred to as Pentecost.
