## Meaning

Bread is food made from flour, usually baked into loaves.

It has long been a basic food in many cultures.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, bread refers to ordinary food and daily provision.

It is also used in connection with offerings, the Passover, the Lord's Supper, and in figurative expressions.
