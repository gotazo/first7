## Meaning

Bondage is the state of being under the control of another; slavery or captivity.

It refers to a condition in which freedom is restricted.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, bondage refers to slavery, captivity, or servitude.

The term is used both for literal bondage, such as Israel's slavery in Egypt, and for other forms of bondage described throughout the Bible.
