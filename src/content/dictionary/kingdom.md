## Meaning

A kingdom is a realm, territory, or people ruled by a king.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, kingdom refers to earthly kingdoms, the kingdom of God, the kingdom of heaven, and other realms ruled by kings or rulers, depending on the context.
