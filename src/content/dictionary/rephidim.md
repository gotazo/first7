## Meaning

Rephidim is a place-name mentioned in the biblical account of the Israelites' journey from Egypt toward Mount Sinai. Bible dictionaries offer different explanations of the name, including “resting places” and “supports,” so its precise meaning should not be treated as settled.

## Biblical Usage

The Israelites camped at Rephidim after travelling from the wilderness of Sin. There was no water for the people to drink, and they contended with Moses. At the LORD's command, Moses struck the rock at Horeb, and water came out for the people (Exodus 17:1-7).

The Amalekites then fought against Israel at Rephidim. Joshua led the fighting men, while Moses stood on the hill with the rod of God in his hand. Aaron and Hur supported Moses' hands until Israel prevailed (Exodus 17:8-13).

Moses built an altar and called its name Jehovah-nissi (Exodus 17:15). The Israelites later departed from Rephidim and camped in the wilderness of Sinai (Exodus 19:2; Numbers 33:14-15).

The exact modern location of Rephidim is uncertain.
