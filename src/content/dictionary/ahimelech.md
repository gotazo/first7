## Meaning

A Hebrew male personal name meaning “the king is my brother” or “my brother is king”; the name is borne by more than one priest in Scripture.

---

## Biblical Usage

Ahimelech was a priest at Nob who gave David the shewbread and the sword of Goliath when David was fleeing from Saul. Doeg the Edomite later reported the encounter to Saul.

Saul ordered the priests of Nob to be killed because Ahimelech had helped David. Ahimelech and the other priests were killed by Doeg, while Abiathar, the son of Ahimelech, escaped and fled to David.
