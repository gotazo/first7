## Meaning

The mercy seat was the gold cover placed on top of the Ark of the Covenant.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the mercy seat was located in the Most Holy Place above the Ark of the Covenant.

God declared that He would meet with Moses from above the mercy seat.
