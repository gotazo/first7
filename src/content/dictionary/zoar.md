## Meaning

Zoar is a Hebrew place name meaning "small" or "little." A city in the plain of Jordan to which Lot fled from Sodom.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Zoar was one of the cities of the plain.

Lot fled there after leaving Sodom, and the city was spared from the
destruction that came upon Sodom and Gomorrah.

The name is connected in Genesis with the city's small size.
