## Meaning

A court is an enclosed area surrounding a building or structure.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, courts were enclosed areas associated with the tabernacle, the temple, royal residences, and other buildings.

The courts of the tabernacle and temple were places where worship and various activities were carried out according to God's commandments.
