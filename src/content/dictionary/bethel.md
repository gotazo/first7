## Meaning

Bethel is a Hebrew place name meaning "house of God."

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Bethel was the place where Jacob saw a ladder reaching
from earth to heaven in a dream.

Jacob named the place Bethel after the LORD appeared to him there. The
place was previously called Luz.

Bethel is mentioned throughout the Old Testament in connection with the
history of Israel.
