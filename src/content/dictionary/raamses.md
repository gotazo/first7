## Meaning

Raamses is a city in Egypt built for Pharaoh by the Israelites during
their oppression.

The name is associated with the Egyptian royal name Rameses.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Raamses is one of the two treasure cities that the
Israelites were forced to build for Pharaoh.

It is also named as the starting point from which the children of Israel
journeyed out of Egypt at the Exodus.
