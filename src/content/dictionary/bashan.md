## Meaning

Bashan is a region east of the Jordan River known for its fertile land and pastures.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Bashan is a region east of the Jordan River.

It is known for its fertile land, cattle, forests, and cities. Bashan was ruled by Og before it was conquered by Israel and allotted to the tribes east of the Jordan.
