## Meaning

Eliezer is a masculine personal name meaning "My God is help." In Exodus 18:4, Moses explained the name by saying that the God of his father was his help and had delivered him from the sword of Pharaoh.

## Biblical Usage

Eliezer was the second son of Moses and Zipporah. Moses named him in remembrance of the LORD's help and deliverance from Pharaoh.

First Chronicles 23:15-17 records Eliezer among the sons of Moses and states that his son was Rehabiah. His descendants are also mentioned in 1 Chronicles 26:24-25.
