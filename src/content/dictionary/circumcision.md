## Meaning

Circumcision is the removal of the foreskin from the male sexual organ.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, circumcision is a physical practice given to Abraham as a
sign of the covenant between God and Abraham and his descendants.

It was to be performed on male children on the eighth day and became a
prescribed practice among the people of Israel.
