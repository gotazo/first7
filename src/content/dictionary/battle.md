## Meaning

A battle is a fight or armed conflict between opposing people or groups.

The word commonly refers to a military engagement during a war.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, a battle is a conflict between opposing armies or forces.

Battles are recorded throughout the Bible involving Israel, surrounding nations, and other conflicts described in historical and prophetic passages.
