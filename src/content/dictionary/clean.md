## Meaning

Clean means free from impurity or acceptable according to a particular
standard.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, clean describes persons, animals, objects, places, and
conditions that are free from specified impurity or are acceptable
according to God's commands.

The term is used in ceremonial, physical, moral, and spiritual contexts
throughout the Bible.
