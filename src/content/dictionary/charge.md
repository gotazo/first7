## Meaning

A charge is a responsibility, duty, instruction, command, or accusation
entrusted or given to a person.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, charge has several related meanings.

It can refer to a responsibility or duty entrusted to a person, an
instruction or command given to someone, or an accusation brought against
a person.

The term is frequently used of responsibilities given to priests, Levites,
leaders, and others serving God.
