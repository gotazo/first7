## Meaning

Truth is the quality or state of being true, accurate, or in accordance with fact.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, truth refers to what is true, faithful, reliable, or in agreement with reality.

The term is used of God's word, His character, His ways, and His teachings.
