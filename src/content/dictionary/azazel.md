## Meaning

Azazel is a term associated with the goat that was sent into the wilderness
on the Day of Atonement.

The precise meaning of the Hebrew term is uncertain.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Azazel appears in Leviticus 16 in connection with the second
goat selected for the Day of Atonement.

Aaron was instructed to confess over the goat the iniquities of the children
of Israel and send it away into the wilderness.

The precise meaning or identification of the Hebrew term Azazel is uncertain,
and Scripture does not explicitly identify it with a particular person or
being.
