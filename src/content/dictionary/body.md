## Meaning

A body is the physical structure of a person or living creature.

It consists of the parts that make up a living being.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, body refers to the physical body of a person or animal.

The term is also used figuratively for a unified group of people and for the body of Christ in various passages.
