## Meaning

A Hebrew male personal name meaning “lofty” or “high.”

---

## Biblical Usage

Eli was a priest at Shiloh and served as a judge of Israel for forty years. He instructed Samuel while Samuel was ministering before the LORD.

Eli's sons, Hophni and Phinehas, served as priests but acted wickedly. Eli was rebuked because he did not restrain them adequately. He died after hearing that the ark of God had been taken and that his sons had died in battle.
