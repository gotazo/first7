## Meaning

A place is a particular location or area. Rameses is a biblical place-name associated with Egypt.

## Biblical Usage

Rameses is mentioned in Genesis as part of the land where Joseph settled Jacob and his family (Genesis 47:11). In Exodus, it is named as one of the treasure cities built for Pharaoh (Exodus 1:11). It is also the place from which the Israelites departed on their journey out of Egypt (Exodus 12:37; Numbers 33:3, 5).

The Bible identifies Rameses with Egypt, but its precise historical location is not established by these passages.

## Key References

- **Genesis 47:11** — Joseph settles his family in the land of Rameses.
- **Exodus 1:11** — Rameses is named as a treasure city built for Pharaoh.
- **Exodus 12:37** — The Israelites journey from Rameses to Succoth.
- **Numbers 33:3, 5** — Rameses is recorded as the starting point of Israel's journey.

## Related Terms

- **Goshen** — The region in Egypt where Jacob's family settled.
- **Pithom** — Another treasure city named with Raamses.
- **Succoth** — The place where the Israelites camped after leaving Rameses.
