## Meaning

Mahanaim is a place east of the Jordan River whose name means “two camps.”

---

## Biblical Usage

In Genesis 32:1-2, Jacob encounters the angels of God and names the place Mahanaim. The name is later associated with a location in the territory east of the Jordan.

After Saul's death, Abner brings Ish-bosheth to Mahanaim. During Absalom's rebellion, David also takes refuge there, and supporters bring supplies to him at the place.
