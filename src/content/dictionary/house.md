## Meaning

A house is a building or dwelling place, and by extension can refer to the household, family, lineage, or people associated with it.

---

## Biblical Usage

Throughout Scripture, **house** commonly refers to a physical dwelling where people live. The word can also refer to the people belonging to a household or family, rather than to the building itself.

The Bible also uses **house** for a family lineage or dynasty, such as the house of David, and for places dedicated to worship, such as the house of the LORD. The meaning is determined by the context in which the word occurs.
