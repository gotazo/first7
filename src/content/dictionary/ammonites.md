## Meaning

Ammonites are a people descended from Ammon who inhabited the region
east of the Jordan River.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the Ammonites were descendants of Ammon, the son of Lot.

They inhabited the land of the children of Ammon and frequently appeared
in the history of Israel.
