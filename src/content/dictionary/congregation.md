## Meaning

A congregation is an assembly or gathering of people brought together for
a common purpose.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, congregation refers to an assembly or community of people,
especially the gathered people of Israel.

The term is used for the whole nation, tribal or local assemblies, and
gatherings before God for worship, instruction, judgment, or other purposes.
