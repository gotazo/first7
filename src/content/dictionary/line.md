## Meaning

A line is a cord, thread, boundary, measured extent, or other continuous mark or object.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, line can refer to a cord or thread, a measuring line, a boundary, or a measured extent. Lines were used in practical activities such as lowering or measuring objects and determining the limits of land.

The term can also describe a succession or family descent and can be used figuratively in descriptions of judgment, order, and measurement. In Jericho, Rahab was instructed to bind a line of scarlet thread in her window.
