## Meaning

Holy means dedicated or set apart for a sacred purpose; associated with God
or sacred things.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, holy describes God, people, places, objects, times, and
things that are set apart or associated with God.

The term is also used to describe God's character and the conduct required
of His people.
