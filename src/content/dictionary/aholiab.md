## Meaning

Aholiab is a Hebrew proper name meaning "the tent of the father."

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Aholiab was the son of Ahisamach of the tribe of Dan.

He was appointed with Bezalel to assist in the work of the tabernacle
and its furnishings and was skilled in engraving, design, and
embroidery.
