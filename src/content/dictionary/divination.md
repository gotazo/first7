## Meaning

Divination is the practice of seeking knowledge or guidance through
supernatural or occult means.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, divination refers to practices used to seek knowledge,
guidance, or predictions through supernatural or occult means rather than
through God.

The Bible mentions divination in connection with false prophets, heathen
practices, and those who sought hidden knowledge by forbidden means.

Scripture consistently distinguishes such practices from seeking God.
