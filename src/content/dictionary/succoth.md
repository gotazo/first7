## Meaning

Succoth is a Hebrew place name meaning "booths" or "shelters."

In Genesis, it was a place where Jacob settled and built booths for
his cattle.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Genesis, Succoth was a place where Jacob settled after meeting Esau
and built booths for his cattle.

Another place called Succoth is mentioned in the account of Gideon and
was located east of the Jordan.
