## Meaning

The Jubilee was the fiftieth year observed by Israel according to the Law of Moses.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the Jubilee followed seven sabbatical cycles.

During that year liberty was proclaimed, inherited property returned to its original family, and regulations concerning the land were observed according to God's commandments.
