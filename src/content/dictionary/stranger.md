## Meaning

A stranger is a person who is not a native or established member of a
place, household, or community.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, stranger refers to a person who is outside a particular
family, people, household, or community.

The term is used for foreigners living among Israel, persons unknown to
others, and people who are not members of a particular household or
group.

The Bible also uses the term in describing the temporary or pilgrim
condition of God's people.
