## Meaning

A jawbone is the bone forming part of the jaw of a person or animal.

---

## Biblical Usage

The term **jawbone** is especially associated with Samson's battle against the Philistines. Samson found a new jawbone of an ass and used it as a weapon, killing a thousand Philistines.

The biblical account identifies the jawbone as an ordinary animal bone used by Samson in the conflict. It is not presented as a sacred object or as having special power in itself.
