## Meaning

A **widow** is a woman whose husband has died and who remains without a living husband.

---

## Biblical Usage

Throughout Scripture, widows are frequently included among people who require protection and fair treatment. Biblical laws provide for their food and property rights and prohibit their oppression.

The historical books give examples of widows receiving or requiring assistance, while the prophets condemn those who exploit them. The New Testament records the care of widows as an important responsibility of the early Christian community.
