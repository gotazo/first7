## Meaning

Zebulun is a Hebrew proper name associated with "dwelling" or "honor."

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Zebulun was the tenth son of Jacob and the sixth son of
Leah.

He became the ancestor of the tribe of Zebulun.
