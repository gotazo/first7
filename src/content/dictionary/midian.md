## Meaning

Midian is the name of a region and people associated with the descendants
of Midian, a son of Abraham and Keturah.

The name is also used for the people descended from him.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Midian refers both to a people descended from Midian and
to the region associated with them.

Moses fled to Midian after leaving Egypt and lived there with Jethro
before returning to Egypt.

The Midianites are also mentioned in later accounts of Israel.
