## Meaning

A giant is a person or creature of unusually great size or strength.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, giant refers to a person of exceptional size or stature.

The Bible mentions several groups or individuals described as giants or as
being of great stature, including the Nephilim, Anakim, and Rephaim.

The term is also used in connection with Goliath and other men noted for
their extraordinary size.
