## Meaning

Judgment is the act of making a decision, forming an opinion, or pronouncing a sentence.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, judgment refers to decisions, justice, legal rulings, and the acts of God in judging individuals and nations.

The term is also used for future judgment described in both the Old and New Testaments.
