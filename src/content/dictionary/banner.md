## Meaning

A banner is a piece of cloth or other sign displayed as a symbol, signal,
or means of identification.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, a banner is a visible sign used to identify, gather, guide,
or represent a group of people.

Banners were associated with the tribes and divisions of Israel, and the
Bible also uses the term figuratively for God's protection, love, power,
or a raised signal.
