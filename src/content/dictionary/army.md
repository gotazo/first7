## Meaning

An army is a large organized group of people trained and equipped for war.

The word may also refer to a military force assembled for defense, conquest, or protection.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, an army refers to an organized force gathered for battle.

The term is used for the armies of Israel, foreign nations, and the heavenly host.
