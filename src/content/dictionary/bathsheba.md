## Meaning

Bathsheba is a Hebrew feminine personal name commonly understood as “daughter of the oath” or “daughter of seven.” The precise interpretation of the name is not certain.

---

## Biblical Usage

Bathsheba was the daughter of Eliam and the wife of Uriah the Hittite. David took her after seeing her while she was bathing, and Uriah was subsequently killed in battle after David arranged for him to be placed in the forefront.

Bathsheba later gave birth to Solomon. During David's final illness, she and Nathan approached David concerning Adonijah's attempt to become king, and David confirmed that Solomon would succeed him.
