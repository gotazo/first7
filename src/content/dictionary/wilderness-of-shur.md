## Meaning

The wilderness of Shur is a wilderness region through which the children
of Israel traveled after crossing the Red sea.

The meaning of the place name Shur is uncertain.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the wilderness of Shur was the wilderness into which Moses
led the children of Israel after they crossed the Red sea.

They traveled there for three days without finding water before coming to
Marah.

The name Shur is also associated with a region mentioned in the accounts
of Hagar, Abraham, and Saul.
