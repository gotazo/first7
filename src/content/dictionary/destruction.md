## Meaning

Destruction is the act or result of destroying; ruin, devastation, or complete overthrow.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, destruction refers to ruin, overthrow, loss, or the complete end of people, cities, nations, or things.

The term appears throughout both the Old and New Testaments in historical, poetic, prophetic, and instructional passages.
