## Meaning

Esau is a Hebrew proper name associated with "hairy."

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Esau was the elder twin brother of Jacob and the son of Isaac and Rebekah.

He was a skilful hunter and was also called Edom.

Esau became the father of the Edomites, whose descendants are listed in Genesis 36.
