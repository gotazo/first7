## Meaning

Reuben is a Hebrew proper name meaning "behold, a son."

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Reuben was the firstborn son of Jacob and Leah.

He was the brother of Simeon, Levi, Judah, and the other sons of Jacob.

Reuben became the ancestor of the tribe of Reuben.
