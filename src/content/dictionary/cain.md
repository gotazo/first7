## Meaning

Cain is a Hebrew proper name of uncertain meaning, traditionally associated with "acquired" or "possession."

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Cain is the first son of Adam and Eve and the older brother of Abel.

He was a tiller of the ground and brought an offering to the LORD.

Cain later built a city and named it after his son Enoch.

Cain is mentioned in both the Old and New Testaments.
