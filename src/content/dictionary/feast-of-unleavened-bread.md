## Meaning

The Feast of Unleavened Bread is an appointed feast observed for seven days using unleavened bread.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the Feast of Unleavened Bread was observed immediately after the Passover.

During the seven-day feast, the Israelites were commanded to eat unleavened bread and remove leaven from their homes.
