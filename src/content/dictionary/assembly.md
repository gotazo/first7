## Meaning

An assembly is a gathering of people brought together for a particular purpose.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, an assembly is a gathering or organized group of people brought together for a common purpose. Assemblies may include the whole congregation of Israel, representatives of the people, local gatherings, or other groups meeting for worship, judgment, instruction, or public decisions.

The term is used throughout Scripture for gatherings of God's people and for other organized groups. The meaning of a particular assembly is determined by its context.
