## Meaning

A bladed weapon used for cutting or thrusting.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the sword is a common weapon of warfare and personal combat. It is associated with soldiers, warriors, kings, and judgment.

The Bible also uses the sword figuratively. Ephesians 6:17 calls the word of God "the sword of the Spirit," while Hebrews 4:12 compares the word of God to a two-edged sword.
