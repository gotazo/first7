## Meaning

Blameless means free from fault, accusation, or legitimate blame.

It describes someone whose conduct is without justified reproach.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, blameless describes a person whose conduct is upright, faithful, or without charge in a particular context.

The term is used of individuals and as an expected characteristic of God's people.
