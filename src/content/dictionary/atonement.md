## Meaning

Atonement is the act of making reconciliation or covering in relation to sin.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, atonement is associated with sacrifices, offerings, priestly ministry, and the Day of Atonement.

The term is used throughout both the Old and New Testaments in connection with reconciliation.
