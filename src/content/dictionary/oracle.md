## Meaning

An oracle is a person, place, or message associated with the giving or
receiving of divine or supernatural knowledge.

The word may refer to:

- **A person** believed to communicate divine or supernatural knowledge.
- **A place** where divine or supernatural answers are sought.
- **A message** or answer believed to come from a deity or supernatural
  source.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, oracle refers primarily to a divine message, utterance, or
declaration.

The New Testament uses the expression "oracles of God" for the words
entrusted to God's people.

The KJV also uses "oracle" for the inner sanctuary or most holy place of
the temple in certain Old Testament passages.

In Numbers, Balaam spoke words that he received from God, but the
Scripture does not identify Balaam himself as an "oracle."
