## Meaning

A bush is a woody plant that is generally smaller than a tree and has
several stems arising near its base.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, a bush is mentioned in the account of Moses when the angel
of the LORD appeared to him in a flame of fire from the midst of a bush.

The bush burned with fire but was not consumed, and the LORD spoke to
Moses from it.

The event occurred while Moses was keeping the flock of Jethro in the land
of Midian.
