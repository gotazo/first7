## Meaning

Sarah is a Hebrew proper name meaning "princess."

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Sarah was the wife of Abraham and the mother of Isaac.

She was originally called Sarai before God changed her name to Sarah.

Sarah is mentioned in both the Old and New Testaments.
