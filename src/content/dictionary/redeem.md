## Meaning

To redeem means to recover, rescue, or buy back a person or possession according to an established right or payment.

---

## Biblical Usage

In the Old Testament, redemption could involve recovering family property or a family member through the action of a qualified relative. The Law established provisions for the redemption of land and persons who had entered into conditions of poverty or servitude.

Ruth presents this family and property practice through Boaz, who acted as a near kinsman and redeemed the property associated with Elimelech's family. Scripture also uses redemption more broadly for God's deliverance of His people from bondage, trouble, and death.
