## Meaning

A Passover lamb is a lamb selected and sacrificed for the Passover
observance according to the LORD's command.

The lamb was to be without blemish and was eaten by the household as part
of the Passover.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the Passover lamb was selected by each household in Israel
and was to be without blemish.

It was killed at the appointed time, and its blood was placed upon the
doorposts and lintel of the house.

The lamb was then eaten with unleavened bread and bitter herbs.

The Passover lamb was central to the Passover observance instituted in
Egypt.
