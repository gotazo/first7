## Meaning

Defilement is the condition of being made impure, polluted, corrupted, or
unfit for a particular purpose.

It can refer to physical or ceremonial impurity, as well as moral,
sexual, or religious corruption.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, defilement refers to the condition of being made impure,
polluted, or unclean.

The Bible uses the term in physical, ceremonial, moral, sexual, religious,
and spiritual contexts.

Persons, animals, objects, the land, the sanctuary, and other things may
be described as defiled.

Scripture also speaks of people being defiled by their actions, sin,
idolatry, or association with uncleanness.
