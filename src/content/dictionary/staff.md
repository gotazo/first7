## Meaning

A staff is a long stick or rod used for support, walking, or other
purposes.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, a staff was commonly carried by travelers, shepherds, and
others.

It was also used as a symbol or instrument of authority and appears
prominently in the accounts of Jacob, Judah, Moses, and Aaron.

The Hebrew word translated "staff" can also be translated "rod" and is
used in some contexts for a tribe.
