## Meaning

A married woman in relation to her husband.

---

## Biblical Usage

Scripture uses wife for a woman who is married to a man. The term appears throughout accounts of marriage, family relationships, households, inheritance, and personal relationships.

The Bible also uses wife in figurative descriptions. Israel and Jerusalem are sometimes described using marital imagery, while the New Testament gives instruction concerning the relationship between wives and husbands.
