## Meaning

Murmuring is complaining or speaking against someone or something in
dissatisfaction.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, murmuring refers to expressions of complaint or
dissatisfaction.

During the wilderness journey, the children of Israel repeatedly murmured
against Moses and Aaron and against the LORD because of difficulties,
hunger, thirst, and other circumstances.
