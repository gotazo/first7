## Meaning

Separation is the act or condition of being set apart, divided, or kept
apart from something or someone.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, separation refers to being divided or set apart from persons,
things, places, or practices.

The Bible uses the term in ordinary physical separation as well as in
religious contexts, including separation unto God, separation from
uncleanness or idolatry, and the special separation associated with the
Nazirite vow.
