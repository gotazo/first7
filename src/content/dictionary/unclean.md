## Meaning

Unclean describes a condition of impurity or being unsuitable according to
a particular standard.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, unclean describes persons, animals, objects, places, and
conditions that are considered impure or unsuitable according to God's
commands.

The term is used in ceremonial, physical, moral, and spiritual contexts
throughout the Bible.
