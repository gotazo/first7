## Meaning

Pithom is a place name of uncertain meaning. It was a treasure city in
Egypt built for Pharaoh by the Israelites.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Pithom is one of the two treasure cities that the
Israelites were forced to build for Pharaoh during their oppression in
Egypt.

It is mentioned together with Raamses in the account of the Egyptian
oppression of Israel.
