## Meaning

Law is a rule, principle, or system of rules governing conduct, order, or
judgment.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, law refers to a rule or body of instruction governing
conduct.

The term can refer broadly to God's revealed commands, to the law given
through Moses, to specific laws or principles, and in the New Testament
to the Scriptures or a governing principle in certain contexts.

The meaning of law is determined by its context.
