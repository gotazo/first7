## Meaning

Goshen is a region of Egypt where the children of Israel dwelt during
the time of Joseph and the Exodus.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Goshen was a region of Egypt given to Jacob and his family
for dwelling.

The children of Israel lived there during the latter part of Joseph's
life and during the period preceding the Exodus.

During the plagues of Egypt, Goshen is specifically mentioned as a place
where the children of Israel dwelt.
