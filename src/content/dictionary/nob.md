## Meaning

A biblical place name associated especially with a priestly settlement near Jerusalem during the time of Saul.

---

## Biblical Usage

Nob was a priestly city where the priests served during the reign of Saul. David came to Nob while fleeing from Saul and received provisions and the sword of Goliath from Ahimelech the priest.

Saul later accused Ahimelech and the priests of helping David. At Saul's command, Doeg the Edomite killed the priests of Nob, and the city was attacked. Scripture later refers to Nob in geographical descriptions.

---

## Key References

- 1 Samuel 21:1 — David comes to Nob and meets Ahimelech the priest.
- 1 Samuel 22:9 — Doeg reports David's visit to Nob.
- 1 Samuel 22:18 — Doeg kills the priests at Saul's command.
- 1 Samuel 22:19 — Nob, the city of the priests, is destroyed.
- Nehemiah 11:32 — Nob is mentioned among the places associated with Benjamin.
- Isaiah 10:32 — Nob appears in Isaiah's geographical description.
