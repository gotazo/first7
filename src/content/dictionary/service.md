## Meaning

Service is work or duties performed for another person, purpose, or
authority.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, service refers to work, duties, or acts performed for a
person, household, king, nation, or God.

The Bible uses the term for ordinary labor, military or governmental
duties, and especially service performed in worship and ministry.

The Levites were appointed for particular service connected with the
tabernacle and later the temple.
