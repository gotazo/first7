## Meaning

Conversion is the act of turning or changing from one state, direction, or condition to another.

It refers to a change in direction, condition, or course.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, conversion refers to turning to God or turning back to Him.

The word is used of a change in direction or response as described in various biblical passages.
