## Meaning

A person who commands or leads a group, especially a military force.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, captain refers to a person who holds a position of command or leadership.

The KJV uses the term for military commanders and other persons exercising authority. In Joshua 5:14, the expression "captain of the host of the LORD" is used of the figure who appears to Joshua before the conquest of Jericho.

Hebrews 2:10 uses the term "captain" for the leader of salvation.
