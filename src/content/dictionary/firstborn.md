## Meaning

Firstborn is the first child born to a parent, especially the first son.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, firstborn refers to the first child born in a family and
especially to the first son.

The firstborn had a distinct place in family inheritance and was subject
to particular laws and responsibilities.

The term is also used of Israel and of Jesus Christ.
