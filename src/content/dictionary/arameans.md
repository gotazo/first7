## Meaning

Arameans are a people associated with Aram and the regions of Syria and
Mesopotamia.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the Arameans are associated with Aram and its people.

The King James Version commonly refers to them as Syrians.

They appear in the accounts of the patriarchs and in later conflicts
involving Israel and Judah.
