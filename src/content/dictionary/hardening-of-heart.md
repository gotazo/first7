## Meaning

Hardening of heart is the condition of being resistant or unwilling to
hear or obey.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, hardening of heart refers to a condition in which a person
becomes resistant or unwilling to hear or obey.

In Exodus, Pharaoh's heart is repeatedly described as hardened, and the
LORD also declares that He would harden Pharaoh's heart.

The account also records Pharaoh hardening his own heart.
