## Meaning

The Brazen Altar was the bronze altar in the court of the tabernacle where sacrifices were offered.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the Brazen Altar stood in the court of the tabernacle and later the temple.

It was the altar on which sacrifices and burnt offerings were presented according to God's commandments.
