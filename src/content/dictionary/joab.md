## Meaning

Joab is a Hebrew masculine personal name commonly understood to mean “the LORD is father” or “Yahweh is father.”

---

## Biblical Usage

Joab was the son of Zeruiah and a nephew of David. He became a leading commander in David's army and participated in the establishment and defence of David's kingdom.

Joab killed Abner at Hebron and later killed Absalom during Absalom's rebellion, despite David's instruction concerning his son. During Solomon's reign, Joab supported Adonijah's claim to the throne and was put to death by order of Solomon.
