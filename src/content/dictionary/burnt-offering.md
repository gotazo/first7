## Meaning

A burnt offering is a sacrifice that was completely burned upon the altar.

It was a type of offering presented according to God's instructions.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the burnt offering was an offering presented to God in which the entire animal was consumed by fire upon the altar according to the Law of Moses.
