## Meaning

The practice of using magic, spells, or supernatural means in an attempt to gain knowledge, influence, protection, or power.

---

## Biblical Usage

Scripture uses witchcraft for practices involving forbidden forms of magic or supernatural activity. Such practices are associated with seeking power or knowledge through means other than obedience to God.

The Bible includes witchcraft among practices forbidden to Israel and also uses the term in describing the practices of individuals such as Jezebel. In the New Testament, witchcraft appears among the works of the flesh.

---

## Key References

- Exodus 22:18 — The law addresses the practice of witchcraft.
- Leviticus 19:31 — Israel is warned against those who have familiar spirits and wizards.
- Deuteronomy 18:10 — Witchcraft and related practices are forbidden.
- 1 Samuel 15:23 — Samuel compares rebellion with the sin of witchcraft.
- 2 Kings 9:22 — Jehu refers to Jezebel's whoredoms and witchcrafts.
- 2 Kings 21:6 — Manasseh practices witchcraft and other forbidden activities.
- Micah 5:12 — God declares that witchcrafts will be cut off.
- Galatians 5:20 — Witchcraft is listed among the works of the flesh.
- Revelation 21:8 — Sorcerers are included among those described in the passage.
