## Meaning

A tribe is a group of people connected by common ancestry, family, or
lineage.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, tribe refers to a distinct group descended from a common
ancestor.

The tribes of Israel were descended from the twelve sons of Jacob and
formed the principal divisions of the nation.

The Bible also uses tribe for other peoples and groups connected by
ancestry.
