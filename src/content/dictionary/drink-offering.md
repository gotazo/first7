## Meaning

A drink offering was an offering of wine poured out before the LORD according to God's commandments.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the drink offering was one of the offerings prescribed under the Law of Moses.

It consisted of wine poured out before the LORD and was presented together with certain sacrifices and offerings according to God's commandments.

The term is also used figuratively in the New Testament.
