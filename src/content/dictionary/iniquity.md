## Meaning

Iniquity is immoral or wrongful conduct; a violation of what is right.

---

## Biblical Usage

Scripture uses *iniquity* for wrongdoing, moral corruption, and guilt. It appears in passages concerning individual conduct, the sins of a people, and the consequences of wrongdoing.

The term frequently occurs alongside *sin* and *transgression*. These words have overlapping meanings, but the particular wording and context of each passage should be preserved when studying them.

---

## Key References

- **Genesis 4:13** — Records Cain's response concerning his punishment after his wrongdoing.
- **Exodus 34:7** — Describes the LORD as forgiving iniquity, transgression, and sin.
- **Psalm 51:2** — David asks to be cleansed from his iniquity.
- **Psalm 103:10** — Declares that the LORD hath not dealt with His people according to their sins.
- **Isaiah 53:5-6** — Connects iniquity with the suffering described in the passage.
- **Ezekiel 18:20** — Discusses the relationship between personal wrongdoing and its consequences.
- **Romans 6:19** — Uses iniquity in a discussion of sinful conduct.

## Related Terms

- **Guilt** — Responsibility for wrongdoing.
- **Wickedness** — The quality or practice of evil conduct.
