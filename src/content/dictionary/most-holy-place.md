## Meaning

The Most Holy Place was the innermost room of the tabernacle and later the temple where the Ark of the Covenant was kept.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the Most Holy Place contained the Ark of the Covenant and was separated from the Holy Place by the veil.

It was the innermost part of the tabernacle and later the temple.
