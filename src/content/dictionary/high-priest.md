## Meaning

The high priest was the chief priest who held the highest priestly office among the Israelites.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the high priest served on behalf of the people in matters relating to worship and sacrifice.

His responsibilities included entering the Most Holy Place on the Day of Atonement according to God's commandments.
