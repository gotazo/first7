## Meaning

**Ashtaroth** is the name used in Scripture for a Canaanite goddess and the worship associated with her.

---

## Biblical Usage

Ashtaroth appears in passages describing Israel's worship of the gods of surrounding peoples. The term occurs particularly in Judges and Samuel, where Israel is repeatedly described as serving Baal and Ashtaroth and is called to abandon these foreign gods.

The KJV also uses the related form **Ashtoreth** in other passages. Ashtaroth is the form found in the Judges passages and is retained here as the canonical dictionary term rather than treating every grammatical form as a separate concept.
