## Meaning

Ish-bosheth is a Hebrew masculine personal name meaning “man of shame.” It is associated with the name Ish-baal; the element *bosheth* means “shame.”

---

## Biblical Usage

Ish-bosheth was a son of Saul. After Saul's death, Abner took him to Mahanaim and made him king over Israel, while David ruled over Judah.

Following a conflict between Abner and Ish-bosheth, Abner negotiated with David. After Abner was killed, two of Ish-bosheth's captains murdered him while he rested in his house. They took his head to David at Hebron.
