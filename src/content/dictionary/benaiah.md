## Meaning

Benaiah is a Hebrew masculine personal name meaning “the LORD has built” or “Yahweh has built.”

---

## Biblical Usage

Benaiah son of Jehoiada was one of David's distinguished warriors. He was placed over the Cherethites and Pelethites and was noted for his military deeds, including killing two lionlike men of Moab and a lion in a pit.

When David's succession was contested, Benaiah supported Solomon. Solomon later ordered Benaiah to execute Joab and appointed him over the army in Joab's place.
