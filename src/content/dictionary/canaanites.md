## Meaning

Canaanites are a people who inhabited the land of Canaan before and
during the settlement of the children of Israel.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the Canaanites were among the peoples inhabiting the land
of Canaan.

They are frequently mentioned in the accounts of Abraham, Isaac, Jacob,
the Exodus, and the conquest of the land.
