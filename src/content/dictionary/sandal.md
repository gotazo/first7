## Meaning

A sandal is a simple piece of footwear worn on the foot, commonly secured by straps.

---

## Biblical Usage

Sandals were common footwear in biblical times and are mentioned in connection with travel, ordinary life, and various customs.

In Ruth, the removal and giving of a sandal served as a testimony confirming a transaction involving redemption and property. A related custom appears in Deuteronomy when a man who refused his duty to his brother's family had his sandal removed as a public sign.
