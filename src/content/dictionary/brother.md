## Meaning

A brother is a male sibling or a male member of the same family or group.

The word may also refer to a close relative or companion.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, brother refers to a male sibling, a close relative, or a fellow member of God's people.

The term is also used for believers who share a common faith.
