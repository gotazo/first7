## Meaning

To serve is to perform duties for another person or to carry out a particular function, obligation, or purpose.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, serve refers to performing duties, laboring for another, attending to responsibilities, or giving allegiance to a person or authority. The term is used for ordinary service to people, rulers, households, and communities, as well as service to God.

Service can involve labor, obedience, worship, ministry, or assistance. The meaning is determined by the person or purpose being served and the context in which the term occurs.
