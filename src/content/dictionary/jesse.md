## Meaning

A biblical Hebrew proper name of uncertain etymology; Jesse was the father of David.

---

## Biblical Usage

Jesse was the son of Obed and the father of David. He lived in Bethlehem and had several sons and daughters. Samuel was sent by the LORD to anoint one of Jesse's sons, and David was chosen.

Jesse is also mentioned in later prophetic and New Testament passages. Isaiah speaks of the stem and root of Jesse, and the New Testament identifies Jesus Christ with the promised Davidic line associated with Jesse.
