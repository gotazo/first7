## Meaning

A commandment is a command or authoritative instruction that is given to
be obeyed.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, a commandment is an authoritative command or instruction.

The term is especially used of commands given by God concerning faith,
worship, conduct, and obedience.

Scripture also uses commandment for instructions given by people or
authorities in particular contexts.
