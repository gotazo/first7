## Meaning

Jealousy is a strong feeling arising from perceived rivalry, unfaithfulness,
or threatened possession or relationship.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, jealousy can refer to a strong concern arising from rivalry,
unfaithfulness, or the claim to exclusive devotion.

The Bible uses the term both of God, whose jealousy concerns the worship and
faithfulness of His people, and of human beings.

Jealousy may therefore be righteous or sinful depending on its cause and
character.
