## Meaning

Ephraim is a Hebrew proper name meaning "fruitful."

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Ephraim was the younger son of Joseph and Asenath.

Joseph named him Ephraim because God had caused him to be fruitful in
the land of his affliction.

Ephraim later became the ancestor of the tribe of Ephraim.
