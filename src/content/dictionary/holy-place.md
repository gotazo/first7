## Meaning

The Holy Place was the first room of the tabernacle and later the temple where priests carried out regular duties of worship.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the Holy Place contained the golden lampstand, the table of shewbread, and the golden altar of incense.

It was the place where priests ministered according to God's commandments.
