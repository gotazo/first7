## Meaning

A Hebrew male personal name of uncertain origin; the Bible uses the name for more than one Israelite.

---

## Biblical Usage

Phinehas is the name of more than one man in Scripture. The most prominent are Phinehas the son of Eleazar and grandson of Aaron, and Phinehas the son of Eli and brother of Hophni.

The Phinehas associated with 1 Samuel was a priest at Shiloh and the son of Eli. He and his brother Hophni were condemned for their wicked conduct and were killed when the Philistines captured the ark of God.

---

- Psalm 106:30 — Phinehas is remembered for his action during Israel's rebellion.
- Ezra 7:5 — Phinehas appears in the priestly genealogy.
