## Meaning

A camp is a temporary place where people live, stay, or gather, especially
while traveling or engaged in military activity.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, camp refers to a temporary dwelling place or organized
gathering of people, especially Israel during its wilderness journey.

The Israelites were arranged around the tabernacle according to their tribes
and families.

The Bible also uses camp for military encampments and other temporary
gatherings.
