## Meaning

Horeb is the name of a place called the mountain of God in Scripture.

The name is associated with the Hebrew word for "dryness" or
"desolation."

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Horeb is the place where Moses encountered the LORD in the
burning bush.

It is also associated with events involving Israel and the giving of
God's covenant and commandments.

Scripture uses the names Horeb and Sinai in passages concerning the
mountain and wilderness where God met with Israel. The biblical text
should be allowed to establish the relationship between these names
without requiring a separate geographical identification.
