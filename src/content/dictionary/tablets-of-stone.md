## Meaning

The tablets of stone were stone tablets upon which God wrote the words of
His covenant and commandments.

They were given to Moses at Mount Sinai.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the tablets of stone were the tablets given by God to Moses
at Mount Sinai.

God wrote His commandments upon them, and Moses brought them down from the
mountain.

After Moses broke the first tablets because of Israel's idolatry, the LORD
commanded him to prepare another two tablets and wrote the words upon them
again.
