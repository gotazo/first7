## Meaning

Tamar is a Hebrew proper name meaning "palm tree."

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Tamar was the daughter-in-law of Judah.

She bore Perez and Zerah by Judah and is included in the genealogy of
Jesus Christ recorded in Matthew.
