## Meaning

An oath is a solemn declaration or promise made as a pledge of truth
or commitment.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, an oath is a solemn declaration or promise used to
confirm a statement, agreement, covenant, or commitment.

Oaths are made between individuals and are also used in connection with
covenants and promises made before God.
