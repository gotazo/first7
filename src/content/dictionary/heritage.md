## Meaning

A **heritage** is something inherited or passed down from an earlier generation, including property, possession, status, or a lasting legacy.

---

## Biblical Usage

In the Bible, heritage refers to something received or possessed as an inheritance. It is commonly associated with land, possessions, families, and peoples, but its use is broader than material property.

Israel is described as having an inheritance from the LORD, while the LORD Himself is described as the inheritance or portion of His people. Scripture also uses heritage in connection with children, nations, and the lasting possessions or relationships established by God.

Heritage and **inheritance** are closely related. Inheritance emphasizes what is received, while heritage can emphasize what is possessed or passed down as an inherited possession or legacy.

---

## Key References

**Deuteronomy 32:9** — The LORD's portion is described as His people, and Jacob as the lot of His inheritance.

**Psalm 16:6** — David speaks of having a goodly heritage.

**Psalm 33:12** — The nation whose God is the LORD is described as the people whom He has chosen for His own inheritance.

**Psalm 78:71** — David was taken from following the sheep to feed Jacob, God's people, and Israel, His inheritance.

**Psalm 127:3** — Children are described as an heritage of the LORD.

**Isaiah 19:25** — Israel is called the inheritance of the LORD of hosts.
