## Meaning

Enchantment is the use of words, practices, or supposed supernatural powers
to influence, charm, or affect others.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, enchantment refers to practices associated with occult or
supernatural attempts to influence, predict, or control events or persons.

In Numbers, Balaam's inability to curse Israel is contrasted with the
absence of enchantment or divination against Jacob.

The Bible also places enchantment among practices forbidden by God.
