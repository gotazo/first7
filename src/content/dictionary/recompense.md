## Meaning

Recompense is something given or done in return for an action, service, loss, or injury. It may be a reward or a repayment for wrongdoing.

---

## Biblical Usage

Scripture uses *recompense* for repayment, reward, or a response corresponding to what has been done. Depending on the passage, it may describe a reward for conduct or the consequences of wrongdoing.

The term also appears in passages concerning justice and the LORD's judgment. In 2 Samuel 3:39, David declares that the LORD will reward the doer of evil according to his wickedness.

---

## Key References

- **Deuteronomy 32:35** — Declares that vengeance and recompense belong to the LORD.
- **2 Samuel 3:39** — David speaks of the LORD rewarding the doer of evil according to his wickedness.
- **Psalm 28:4** — Asks for a response according to the deeds and wickedness of the people addressed.
- **Proverbs 24:12** — Addresses the LORD's knowledge of human actions and His rendering to every man according to his works.
- **Isaiah 35:4** — Speaks of the coming of God with vengeance and recompense.
- **Jeremiah 51:6** — Declares the LORD's vengeance and recompense concerning Babylon.
- **Romans 12:19** — Quotes the declaration that vengeance belongs to the LORD.
- **Hebrews 10:30** — Repeats the scriptural declaration concerning vengeance and recompense.
