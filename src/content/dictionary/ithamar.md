## Meaning

Ithamar is a Hebrew proper name traditionally understood to mean
"island of palms."

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Ithamar was the fourth son of Aaron and Elisheba and served
as a priest.

He was placed in charge of certain responsibilities concerning the
tabernacle and its service.
