## Meaning

Seed is the part of a plant from which another plant grows. The word can also refer to a person's descendants or offspring.

---

## Biblical Usage

Scripture uses *seed* for planting and the reproduction of plants, as well as for descendants and offspring. In genealogical and covenant passages, the term may refer to the future generations of an individual or family.

In 2 Samuel 7:12-16, God promises David that his seed will follow him and that his kingdom will be established. The word also appears in promises made to Abraham and in New Testament passages discussing Abraham's seed.

---

## Key References

- **Genesis 3:15** — Refers to the seed of the woman and the seed of the serpent.
- **Genesis 12:7** — Records God's promise concerning Abraham's seed.
- **Genesis 17:7** — Describes the covenant with Abraham and his seed throughout their generations.
- **Genesis 22:17-18** — Refers to Abraham's seed in the promise concerning his descendants.
- **2 Samuel 7:12-16** — Records God's promise concerning David's seed, kingdom, and throne.
- **Psalm 89:3-4** — Refers to David's seed and the establishment of his throne.
- **Galatians 3:16** — Discusses the promise made to Abraham and his seed.
- **Galatians 3:29** — Describes those who belong to Christ as Abraham's seed and heirs according to the promise.
