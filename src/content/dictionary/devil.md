## Meaning

The Devil is the chief adversary of God and His people.

He is also called Satan in Scripture.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the Devil is identified as Satan, the tempter, the accuser, and the adversary. He appears throughout both the Old and New Testaments and is especially prominent in the Gospels and Revelation.
