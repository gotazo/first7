## Meaning

Manna is the food provided by God for the children of Israel in the
wilderness.

The name is associated in Exodus with the question, "What is it?"

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, manna was the food that the LORD provided for the children
of Israel during their journey through the wilderness.

It appeared upon the ground with the morning dew and was gathered
according to the LORD's command.

The Israelites ate manna throughout their wilderness journey until they
came into the land of Canaan.
