## Meaning

A mill is a device used for grinding grain or other materials into smaller particles, especially flour.

---

## Biblical Usage

Mills were used to grind grain for food and were part of ordinary household life. Scripture refers to grinding as a regular daily activity and uses mills and millstones in descriptions of work and household provision.

The Bible also uses the mill in laws concerning property, descriptions of judgment, and figurative descriptions of destruction. Because grinding was necessary for food preparation, taking a person's millstone was equivalent to taking an essential means of livelihood.
