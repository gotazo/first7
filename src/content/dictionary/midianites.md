## Meaning

Midianites are a people descended from Midian, a son of Abraham and
Keturah.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the Midianites were descendants of Midian.

They are mentioned in the accounts of Abraham's descendants, Joseph,
Moses, and the judges of Israel.
