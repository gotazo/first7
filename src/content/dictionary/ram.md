## Meaning

A ram is an adult male sheep.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, a ram is an adult male sheep. Rams are mentioned as livestock and are also used in various sacrifices and offerings.

Rams appear in accounts involving worship, consecration, flocks, and other biblical events.
