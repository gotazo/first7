## Meaning

A coat is an outer garment worn over the body or other clothing.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, coat refers to a garment worn by a person.

Coats are mentioned in accounts involving clothing, priestly garments,
and ordinary dress.

Joseph's special coat given to him by Jacob is one of the best-known
examples in Genesis.
