## Meaning

A cedar is a large evergreen tree valued for its strong and durable wood.

Its wood has long been used for building and construction.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, cedar refers especially to the cedars of Lebanon.

Cedar wood was used in the construction of buildings, the temple, palaces, ships, and other important structures.
