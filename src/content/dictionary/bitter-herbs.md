## Meaning

Bitter herbs are herbs with a bitter taste. In Exodus 12:8, they are specified as part of the meal eaten with the roasted passover lamb and unleavened bread.

## Biblical Usage

In the instructions for the first Passover, the LORD commanded the children of Israel to eat the passover lamb with unleavened bread and bitter herbs. Numbers 9:11 repeats this instruction for the observance of the Passover in the second month.
