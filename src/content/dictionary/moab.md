## Meaning

Moab refers both to a people descended from Lot through his elder daughter and to the territory they occupied east of the Dead Sea.

---

## Biblical Usage

Moab was the territory east of the Dead Sea inhabited by the Moabites. The Moabites were related to Israel through Lot but remained a distinct people.

Moab appears in the history of Israel from the wilderness period through the time of the kings and prophets. Ruth was a Moabite who left Moab with Naomi and settled in Bethlehem, becoming part of the family line leading to David.
