## Meaning

An ordinance is an established rule, regulation, order, or prescribed
practice.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, ordinance refers to an established rule, regulation, order,
or practice prescribed by an authority.

The term is used for regulations established by God concerning worship,
sacrifices, appointed times, and the conduct of His people.

It can also refer to an established order or practice in other contexts.
