## Meaning

An ass is a domesticated hoofed animal of the horse family, commonly known
as a donkey.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, ass refers to a donkey, a domesticated animal commonly used
for transportation, carrying burdens, and agricultural work.

The Bible frequently mentions asses in ordinary life and also records their
use in significant biblical events, including Balaam's journey and Christ's
entry into Jerusalem.
