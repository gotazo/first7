## Meaning

Exhortation is an earnest encouragement, instruction, or appeal to action.

It involves urging someone toward a particular course of action or conduct.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, exhortation refers to encouraging, urging, instructing, or strengthening others through words of teaching or encouragement.
