## Meaning

Bezalel is a Hebrew proper name meaning "in the shadow of God."

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Bezalel was the son of Uri and grandson of Hur, of the
tribe of Judah.

God called and equipped him with wisdom, understanding, knowledge, and
craftsmanship to oversee the making of the tabernacle and its
furnishings.

He worked together with Oholiab and the other skilled workers appointed
for the construction.
