## Meaning

A temple is a building set apart for worship or religious purposes.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the temple refers primarily to the place of worship in Jerusalem where sacrifices were offered and God was worshipped according to His commandments.

The term is also used in other contexts depending on the passage.
