## Meaning

An exodus is a large-scale departure or mass movement of people away from
a place.

The word comes from the Greek *exodos*, meaning "going out" or "departure."

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the Exodus refers specifically to the departure of the
children of Israel from Egypt under the leadership of Moses.

The LORD brought Israel out of Egyptian bondage, led them through the
Red sea, and brought them into the wilderness.

The Exodus became a defining event in the history of Israel and is
repeatedly remembered throughout Scripture.
