## Meaning

A census is an official counting or enumeration of people or other members
of a population.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, a census is a formal counting of people, households, or other
persons for an appointed purpose.

Biblical censuses were conducted for purposes such as organizing Israel,
military service, taxation, or determining the number of people belonging
to a tribe or household.
