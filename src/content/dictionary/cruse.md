## Meaning

A small container or vessel used for holding a liquid or other substance.

---

## Biblical Usage

Scripture uses cruse for a small container, particularly one holding oil or another liquid. The term appears in accounts involving household supplies and provisions.

The cruse of oil belonging to the widow of Zarephath continued to provide oil during the famine, according to the word of the LORD through Elijah. The term is therefore associated with a small vessel for storing or carrying a liquid.
