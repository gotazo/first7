## Meaning

A sheaf is a bundle of cut grain or stalks gathered together during harvest.

---

## Biblical Usage

Sheaves were bundles of harvested grain gathered in the field before the grain was processed. Ruth encountered sheaves while gleaning in the field of Boaz.

The Bible also uses sheaves in figurative language. Joseph's dream of binding sheaves represented his brothers, while Psalm 126 uses bringing in sheaves as an image associated with the result of labor and sowing.
