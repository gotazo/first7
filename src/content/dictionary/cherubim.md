## Meaning

Cherubim are heavenly beings represented in Scripture as attending the
presence of God and associated with His glory and holiness.

The singular form is cherub.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, cherubim are associated with the presence and glory of God.

After Adam and Eve were removed from the garden of Eden, cherubim were
placed at the east of Eden to guard the way to the tree of life.

Figures of cherubim were also made upon the mercy seat and curtains of the
tabernacle.

Cherubim are also described in the visions of Ezekiel.
