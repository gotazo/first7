## Meaning

Eden is a Hebrew proper place name traditionally understood to mean "delight" or "pleasure."

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Eden is the region in which God planted the Garden of Eden.

It is first mentioned in Genesis and is associated with the beginning of human history.

Eden is also referenced in later books of the Bible.
