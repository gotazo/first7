## Meaning

Abihu is a Hebrew proper name traditionally understood as "he is my
father" or "my father is he."

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Abihu was the second son of Aaron and Elisheba and was
appointed to the priesthood with his brothers.

He and his brother Nadab died after offering strange fire before the
LORD.
