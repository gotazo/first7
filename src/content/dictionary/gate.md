## Meaning

A gate is an entrance or opening that provides access through a wall, fence, or enclosure.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, gates served as entrances to cities, houses, courts, and the tabernacle or temple.

Gates were also places where leaders met, judgments were made, business was conducted, and public matters were discussed.
