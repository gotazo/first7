## Meaning

Jethro is a proper name traditionally associated with "abundance" or
"excellence"; the name is also used as a title or name for Moses'
father-in-law.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Jethro was the priest of Midian and the father of
Zipporah, the wife of Moses.

He received Moses after Moses fled from Egypt and later advised him
concerning the administration of judgment among the people of Israel.

Jethro is also associated with the name Reuel in the account of Moses'
family.
