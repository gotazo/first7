## Meaning

Dinah is a Hebrew proper name meaning "judgment" or "judged."

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Dinah was the daughter of Jacob and Leah.

She is mentioned in the account concerning Shechem and the sons of
Jacob.

Dinah was one of the children born to Jacob in Padanaram and is later
listed among those who went with Jacob into Egypt.
