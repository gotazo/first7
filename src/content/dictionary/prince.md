## Meaning

A person of royal family or a person holding a position of authority or leadership.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, prince can refer to a member of a royal family, a ruler, a chief, or another person holding authority. The term is used for leaders among Israel and other nations, as well as for people exercising authority over particular groups.

The Bible also uses the term in passages concerning spiritual or heavenly rulers. In Isaiah, the title "Prince of Peace" is applied to the promised child, while the New Testament describes Jesus as a Prince and Saviour.
