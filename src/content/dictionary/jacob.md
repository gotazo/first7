## Meaning

Jacob is a Hebrew proper name associated with "heel" and "supplanter."

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Jacob was the son of Isaac and Rebekah and the younger twin brother of Esau.

His name is connected in Genesis with his taking hold of Esau's heel at birth. Isaac later used the related expression "supplanted me" when speaking of Jacob.

God later gave Jacob the name Israel.

Jacob became the father of twelve sons, whose descendants became the twelve tribes of Israel.
