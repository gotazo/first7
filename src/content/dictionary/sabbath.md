## Meaning

The Sabbath is the seventh day of the week set apart as a day of rest.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the Sabbath was the seventh day of the week, set apart by God as a day of rest.

It was observed by Israel according to God's commandments and is mentioned throughout both the Old and New Testaments.
