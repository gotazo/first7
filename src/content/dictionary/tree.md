## Meaning

A tree is a perennial plant with a woody stem or trunk, typically bearing branches, leaves, and roots.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, trees are mentioned throughout the Bible as part of God's creation.

They provide food, wood for construction, shade, and landmarks. Various kinds of trees are identified by name and are associated with people, places, events, and teachings recorded in Scripture.

The Bible also refers to specific trees, including the Tree of Life and the Tree of the Knowledge of Good and Evil.
