## Meaning

Exile is the state of being separated from one's homeland or place of
residence, especially through removal or displacement.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, exile refers to the removal of people from their own land
and their residence in a foreign land.

The Bible records the exile of the northern kingdom of Israel and the
captivity and exile of Judah in Babylon.

The prophets also speak of the return of God's people from exile.
