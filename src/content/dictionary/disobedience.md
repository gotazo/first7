## Meaning

Disobedience is failure or refusal to obey a command, instruction, or
authority.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, disobedience is failure or refusal to obey a command or
authority.

The term is frequently used of refusing God's word or commandments.

Scripture also uses disobedience in relation to unbelief, rebellion, and
refusal to submit to God's will.
