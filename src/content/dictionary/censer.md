## Meaning

A censer is a vessel used for carrying burning coals and burning incense.

It was commonly used in religious ceremonies.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, a censer was a vessel used in the worship of God to hold burning coals and incense.

It is mentioned in the tabernacle, the temple, and in heavenly scenes.
