## Meaning

Fruit is the seed-bearing product of a plant or tree.

It develops from the flowering part of a plant and often contains seeds.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, fruit refers to the produce of plants and trees.

The term is also used figuratively to describe results, conduct, offspring, and spiritual qualities.
