## Meaning

Naphtali is a Hebrew proper name associated with "wrestling" or
"struggle."

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Naphtali was the sixth son of Jacob and the second son
of Bilhah, Rachel's maid.

He became the ancestor of the tribe of Naphtali.
