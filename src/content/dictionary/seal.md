## Meaning

A seal is a mark, device, or impression used to confirm, secure, identify, or authenticate something.

It may also refer to the act of closing or securing an object or document.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, a seal is used to signify ownership, authority, authenticity, security, or confirmation.

Seals were applied to documents, tombs, and other objects, and the term is also used figuratively in several passages.
