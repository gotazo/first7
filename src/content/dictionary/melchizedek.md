## Meaning

Melchizedek is a Hebrew proper name meaning "king of righteousness."

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Melchizedek was the king of Salem and priest of the most high God.

He met Abraham after the defeat of the kings and is mentioned again in Psalm 110 and the Epistle to the Hebrews.
