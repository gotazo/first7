## Meaning

Dothan is a Hebrew place name meaning "two wells." A city in the land of Canaan where Joseph found his brothers.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, Dothan was a place where Joseph found his brothers when
they were tending their father's flock.

It was also the place where Elisha was staying when the king of Syria
sent an army against him.
