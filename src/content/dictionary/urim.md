## Meaning

The Urim was one of the sacred objects associated with the breastplate of the high priest.

---

## Biblical Usage

In Scripture, the Urim was placed in the breastplate of judgment and is mentioned together with the Thummim.

The Bible associates these objects with the ministry of the high priest.
