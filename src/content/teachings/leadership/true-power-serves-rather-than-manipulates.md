truth: Godly authority is expressed through humble service rather than manipulation, domination, or self-interest.

insight:
  - Jesus defined greatness through service rather than through control over other people.
  - Authority becomes corrupt when it is used to manipulate people for personal advantage.
  - Godly leadership protects and serves those entrusted to its care.
  - The person who has authority is responsible to use it for the good of others rather than for self-exaltation.
  - Jesus Himself is the example of authority expressed through humble service and sacrifice.

application:
  - Use whatever authority you have to serve rather than control others.
  - Examine your motives when leading or making decisions that affect others.
  - Refuse manipulation as a means of gaining influence.
  - Protect and serve the people God has entrusted to you.
  - Follow Christ's example of humble leadership.
