truth: Jesus Christ and Him crucified must remain at the center of the Christian faith.
insight:
  - Paul deliberately kept Jesus Christ and His crucifixion at the center of his message.
  - Spiritual experiences, gifts, ministry, and outward manifestations must never replace the central place of Christ.
  - The cross is not merely one part of the Christian message; it is foundational to the gospel.
  - A believer can become distracted by what God does and lose sight of who God has revealed Himself to be through Jesus Christ.
  - Everything built in Christian life and ministry must ultimately rest upon Jesus Christ as the foundation.
application:
  - Keep Jesus Christ at the center of your faith, study, worship, and service.
  - Examine whether your attention is being drawn more toward spiritual experiences or toward Christ Himself.
  - Remember the cross when considering spiritual gifts, ministry, or the power of God.
  - Build your Christian life upon Christ rather than upon human wisdom, personalities, experiences, or outward manifestations.
  - Return to Jesus Christ whenever secondary things begin to compete with Him for your attention.
