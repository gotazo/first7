insight:
  - The Shepherd always knows the right path, even when His sheep do not.
  - God's guidance is motivated by His wisdom and faithfulness, not by our understanding.
  - The Shepherd sometimes leads His people in ways they would not naturally choose.
  - Trusting God's guidance means following Him even when the destination is not yet visible.
  - The Shepherd's direction always aligns with His righteous character and purposes.

application:
  - Seek God's direction through His Word and prayer.
  - Follow Christ with trust rather than relying only on your own understanding.
  - Accept that God's path may differ from your expectations.
  - Thank the Shepherd for faithfully leading you throughout every season of life.
