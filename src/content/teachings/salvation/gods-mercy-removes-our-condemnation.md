truth: God's mercy removes the guilt and condemnation of those whom He cleanses and restores.

insight:
  - Joshua stood before the LORD in filthy garments, showing his condition before God.
  - Satan stood to resist Joshua, but the LORD Himself rebuked the accuser.
  - God did not leave Joshua in his filthy garments; He commanded that they be removed.
  - God's mercy is seen in His action to remove what defiled Joshua rather than leaving him in his former condition.
  - The removal of the filthy garments was followed by God's declaration that Joshua's iniquity had passed from him.
  - God's forgiveness does not leave a person defined forever by the condition from which God has delivered him.
  - God's mercy makes restoration possible and leads into a life of obedience and service to Him.

application:
  - Bring your sin honestly before God rather than hiding it.
  - Do not allow past sin to become the permanent definition of your life after God has forgiven and cleansed you.
  - Receive God's mercy with humility rather than continuing to live under condemnation.
  - When God exposes what needs to be removed, respond with repentance rather than resistance.
  - Remember that God's forgiveness should lead to a changed life and renewed obedience.
  - Serve God from the position of having received His mercy rather than trying to earn His acceptance.
