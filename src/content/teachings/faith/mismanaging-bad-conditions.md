truth: God calls His people to respond to His Word by leaving conditions that hinder faithful obedience.

insight:
  - People can become so accustomed to difficult conditions that they begin managing them instead of seeking God's way forward.
  - The four lepers refused to remain where the outcome was certain and moved forward, entrusting the outcome to God.
  - God's Word is meant to stir His people to action rather than leave them comfortable in spiritual stagnation.
  - Faith requires a willingness to leave behind conditions that keep us from walking in obedience.
  - God often works when His people respond to His Word with faith instead of remaining where they have always been.

application:
  - Ask God to reveal any condition you have accepted instead of bringing before Him.
  - Respond in faith when God's Word calls you to move forward.
  - Choose obedience over comfort, trusting God to direct your steps.
