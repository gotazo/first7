insight:
  - Sheep can rest when they are secure in the presence of their shepherd.
  - Believers do not need to see every danger removed before they can trust God's protection.
  - God's presence provides security even when circumstances around us are uncertain.
  - Fear often reveals where we are placing our trust.
  - Confidence in God enables believers to remain at rest even when trouble is nearby.

application:
  - Place your security in God rather than in circumstances or possessions.
  - Trust God's presence when you cannot see how He is working.
  - Refuse to let fear determine your response to difficult circumstances.
  - Rest in the knowledge that the Shepherd is with you.
