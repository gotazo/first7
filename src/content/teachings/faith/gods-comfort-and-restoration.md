truth: God brings comfort to those who mourn and promises restoration through His redemptive work, so that His righteousness and glory may be made known.
insight:
  - The Lord's anointed work includes bringing good tidings to the meek, binding up the brokenhearted, proclaiming liberty to captives, and comforting those who mourn.
  - Beauty for ashes, the oil of joy for mourning, and the garment of praise for heaviness portray a movement from sorrow toward comfort and renewed hope.
  - The promise of restoration is connected with righteousness and the glory of the Lord, not merely with an improvement in outward circumstances.
  - The rebuilding of ruined places portrays restoration after devastation and the renewal of what has been laid waste.
application:
  - Bring your sorrow and brokenness honestly before God rather than assuming that suffering means He has abandoned you.
  - Seek the comfort and hope God gives through His Word and the ministry of Jesus Christ.
  - Where restoration is possible, take faithful and wise steps toward rebuilding what has been damaged.
  - Encourage those who mourn with compassion instead of assuming you know why they are suffering.
  - Seek God's righteousness and glory rather than measuring His faithfulness only by material success.
