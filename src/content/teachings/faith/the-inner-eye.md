insight:
  - Spiritual vision enables believers to look beyond what is visible and consider what God is able to accomplish.
  - The condition of the dry bones did not limit God's power to bring life where there appeared to be no hope.
  - Faith begins by looking at circumstances from God's perspective rather than being governed by outward appearances.
  - God develops spiritual vision by directing His people to focus on His promises instead of present impossibilities.
  - A believer who sees through the eyes of faith is able to trust God's purpose even when natural circumstances appear hopeless.

application:
  - Ask God to help you see your circumstances through the light of His Word.
  - Focus on God's promises instead of allowing outward situations to determine your perspective.
  - Trust God's power to accomplish what seems impossible.
