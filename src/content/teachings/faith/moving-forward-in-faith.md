insight:
  - Faith refuses to remain where nothing can change and chooses to move forward in obedience.
  - The four lepers refused to remain where the outcome was certain, choosing instead to move forward and entrust the outcome to God.
  - Faith acts upon God's direction instead of being paralyzed by uncertainty.
  - God often works through the steps of faith His people are willing to take.
  - Trusting God means obeying Him without demanding to know every outcome in advance.

application:
  - Take the next step of obedience that God has placed before you.
  - Trust God with outcomes that are beyond your control.
  - Refuse to let uncertainty keep you from faithfully following God's direction.
