truth: God calls His people to look beyond present circumstances and trust what He is able to accomplish.

insight:
  - Natural circumstances do not determine what God is able to do.
  - The valley of dry bones appeared hopeless, yet God revealed that He alone has power to bring life where none exists.
  - Faith looks beyond present conditions and places confidence in God's ability rather than human limitations.
  - God's perspective gives hope even when circumstances seem impossible.
  - Trust grows when believers choose to see situations through God's promises instead of outward appearances.

application:
  - Refuse to let difficult circumstances determine your confidence in God.
  - Remember that no situation is beyond God's power to transform according to His will.
  - Look to God's promises when circumstances tempt you to lose hope.
