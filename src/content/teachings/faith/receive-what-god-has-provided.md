truth: God provides what His people need to leave their former condition and live according to what He has called them to be.

insight:
  - Joshua did not provide his own solution to the condition represented by his filthy garments; God commanded that they be removed and provided a change of raiment.
  - God's work in Joshua's life included both the removal of what was unclean and the provision of new garments.
  - Receiving what God provides requires recognizing that the new condition comes from God rather than from human effort alone.
  - God's provision does not remove the responsibility to respond; Joshua was subsequently called to walk in God's ways and keep His charge.
  - The change of garments represents a transition from the former condition into a condition prepared for continued service to God.
  - Faith should lead a believer to receive God's provision with humility and then live according to what God has provided.

application:
  - Recognize your need for what God alone can provide.
  - Receive God's mercy and provision with humility rather than trying to establish your own righteousness.
  - Do not continue identifying yourself with the condition God has called you to leave.
  - Thank God for what He has provided instead of treating His provision as something you have earned.
  - Let what God provides change the way you live and serve Him.
  - Walk faithfully in what God has called you to after receiving His provision.
