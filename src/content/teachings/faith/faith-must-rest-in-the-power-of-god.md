truth: Biblical faith rests in the power of God rather than in the wisdom or ability of men.
insight:
  - Paul wanted the faith of the Corinthians to rest in God's power rather than human wisdom.
  - Persuasive speech and human reasoning cannot become the foundation of Christian faith.
  - The gospel itself reveals the power of God to those who believe.
  - Trusting God's power does not mean rejecting wisdom; it means refusing to make human wisdom the foundation of faith.
  - A believer's confidence ultimately rests in what God can do rather than in what people can accomplish by themselves.
application:
  - "Examine what your faith is resting upon: God's power or human ability."
  - "Trust God's Word even when circumstances appear beyond your ability to change."
  - "Do not measure the truth of God's promises by your own limitations."
  - "Seek God's wisdom while keeping your confidence anchored in Him."
  - "Let your faith produce dependence upon God rather than confidence in yourself."
