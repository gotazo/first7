truth: Faithfulness to God requires obedience even under threat, without making obedience conditional on the deliverance we desire.
insight:
  - Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego refused to worship the king's image even when their refusal placed their lives in danger.
  - They declared that God was able to deliver them, but they would not worship the image even if He did not deliver them as they hoped.
  - Their response shows that loyalty to God must take priority over a demand to disobey Him.
  - God delivered the three men from the furnace, and their deliverance became a public testimony to the king of the God they trusted.
application:
  - Identify situations in which fear, pressure, or the desire for approval may tempt you to disobey God.
  - Obey Scripture rather than compromise your faith to satisfy others.
  - Trust God's power without demanding that He act according to your preferred outcome.
  - Remain faithful when obedience is costly, remembering that faithfulness is not measured only by whether circumstances improve.
  - Let your conduct point others toward the God you serve.
