truth: Faith trusts who God is and remains faithful to Him even when the outcome of a difficult situation is uncertain.

insight:
  - Faith does not deny the existence of danger but trusts God in the presence of it.
  - Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego believed that God was able to deliver them while remaining faithful even if He did not deliver them in the way they expected.
  - Fear can cause people to question whether God cares, while faith remembers His character and power.
  - Knowing God gives believers confidence to face circumstances without making the outcome the foundation of their faith.
  - Faith rests in God Himself rather than requiring a particular answer before remaining obedient.

application:
  - Remember who God is when circumstances become frightening.
  - Trust God's ability even when you do not know what He will do.
  - Do not make your faith dependent upon receiving the outcome you want.
  - Remain faithful to God when His answer is different from your expectation.
  - Bring fearful prayers back to the truth of God's character.
