insight:
  - Godly vision begins by seeing life from God's perspective rather than being limited by natural understanding.
  - Throughout Scripture, God revealed His purposes to His people before those purposes became reality.
  - Spiritual vision gives believers confidence to pursue God's calling even when the outcome cannot yet be seen.
  - Faith continues to hold firmly to God's promises because His purposes are greater than present circumstances.
  - A clear vision of God's purpose strengthens perseverance and encourages faithful obedience.

application:
  - Ask God to shape your perspective through His Word.
  - Keep your attention upon God's purposes instead of temporary circumstances.
  - Walk faithfully toward what God has revealed, trusting Him to accomplish His will.
