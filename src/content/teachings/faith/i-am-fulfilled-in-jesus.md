truth: True fulfillment is found in Christ rather than in the things we possess or the circumstances around us.

insight:
  - The Lord being our Shepherd means that our deepest satisfaction is found in Him.
  - Contentment does not depend upon having everything we desire but upon being satisfied in God.
  - Circumstances and possessions cannot provide the lasting fulfillment that comes from Christ.
  - Knowing Christ changes what we consider necessary for a fulfilled life.
  - A heart satisfied in Christ can remain content even when outward circumstances change.

application:
  - Examine what you are depending upon for fulfillment.
  - Learn to be satisfied in Christ rather than in possessions or circumstances.
  - Thank God for what He has given you and trust Him for what you need.
  - Make knowing Christ your greatest pursuit.
