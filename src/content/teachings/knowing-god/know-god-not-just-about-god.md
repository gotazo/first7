truth: Knowing Christ is more than possessing information about Him; it involves personally knowing Him and living by faith in Him.

insight:
  - A person can know many things about God without allowing that knowledge to shape the way he or she lives.
  - Paul considered knowing Christ to be of greater value than the things he had previously counted as gain.
  - Knowing Christ involves more than knowing facts about His life; Paul desired to know Him and the power of His resurrection.
  - Knowledge of God should move beyond information into faith, obedience, and a life centered on Christ.
  - Jesus connected true worship with knowing whom we worship rather than merely performing outward religious activity.
  - Growing in the knowledge of God changes the way a believer understands, worships, and follows Him.
  - The goal of learning Scripture is not merely to accumulate biblical information but to know the God revealed through His Word.

application:
  - Read Scripture with the desire to know God rather than simply to collect information.
  - Examine whether what you know about God is shaping the way you live.
  - Spend time learning the character, ways, and teachings of Christ.
  - Let your knowledge of God lead you into greater faith and obedience.
  - Examine your worship and ask whether it is grounded in a true knowledge of God.
  - Continue growing in your knowledge of Christ rather than becoming satisfied with what you already know.
