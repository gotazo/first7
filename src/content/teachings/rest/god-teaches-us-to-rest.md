truth: God calls His people to rest in Him rather than remain controlled by anxiety and striving.

insight:
  - God leads His people into places of rest rather than leaving them to carry every burden themselves.
  - Rest requires trusting the Shepherd instead of continually trying to control every circumstance.
  - Anxiety can make it difficult to rest because the heart continues trying to manage what belongs in God's hands.
  - God's presence gives believers reason to become still even when circumstances are difficult.
  - True rest grows from confidence that God is present and caring for His people.

application:
  - Bring your anxieties and burdens to God instead of carrying them alone.
  - Practice being still before God when fear and worry begin to control you.
  - Surrender the things you cannot control to the Lord.
  - Trust God's care even when your circumstances are unsettled.
