truth: Believers find lasting security in Jesus Christ because He faithfully keeps and cares for His people.

insight:
  - Sheep rest only when they know the shepherd is watching over them.
  - The shepherd's calmness gives the flock confidence even when danger is nearby.
  - Security in Christ enables believers to rest before their circumstances change.
  - The question is not whether God is protecting His people, but whether they trust His care enough to rest.
  - When Christ is our security, we no longer depend upon wealth, circumstances, or human strength for peace.

application:
  - Place your confidence in Christ instead of your own abilities.
  - Listen for the Shepherd's voice by spending time in God's Word.
  - Face uncertainty with confidence, knowing Christ remains faithful.
  - Encourage others by reminding them of the hope and security found in Jesus Christ.
