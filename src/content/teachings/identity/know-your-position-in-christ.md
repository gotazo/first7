truth: Believers have a new position in Christ and should live from the identity and victory they have received through Him.

insight:
  - The believer's position is no longer defined only by earthly circumstances but by being in Christ.
  - God has raised believers together with Christ and given them a position in heavenly places in Him.
  - Knowing our position in Christ changes how we face opposition because our confidence rests in what God has done.
  - Spiritual confidence does not come from personal strength but from belonging to Christ.
  - Understanding who we are in Christ helps us face difficulty without being ruled by fear.

application:
  - Remember who you are in Christ when facing opposition.
  - Do not allow circumstances to define your spiritual identity.
  - Build your confidence on what God has done through Christ rather than your own ability.
  - Let your position in Christ shape how you respond to fear and difficulty.
