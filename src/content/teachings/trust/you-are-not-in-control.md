truth: Recognizing our limited control calls us to surrender our plans and trust God's wisdom and direction.

insight:
  - Life can change in ways that are beyond human control.
  - Believers should not assume that everything must happen according to their own plans and timing.
  - Trusting God means accepting that His way may differ from the way we expected.
  - Surrender means placing our plans and desires under God's will rather than demanding our own way.
  - God's direction remains trustworthy even when His purposes unfold differently from what we planned.

application:
  - Surrender your plans and expectations to God.
  - Trust God's wisdom when circumstances do not unfold as you expected.
  - Seek God's direction instead of assuming that your preferred path is always His will.
  - Pray with the attitude, "Thy will be done."
