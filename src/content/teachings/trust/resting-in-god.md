truth: God invites His people to rest confidently in His care because He is their faithful Shepherd.

insight:
  - The Shepherd provides both rest and refreshment for His sheep, meeting their deepest spiritual needs.
  - Rest is more than the absence of activity; it is the confidence that comes from trusting God's care.
  - Sheep rest because they trust their shepherd, illustrating the security believers have in the Lord.
  - God often teaches His people to stop striving so they will learn to depend on His strength rather than their own.
  - True peace is found in God's presence, not in circumstances that are free from difficulty.

application:
  - Trust God's care when fear or anxiety begins to overwhelm your heart.
  - Spend time in God's presence through Scripture and prayer instead of relying only on your own strength.
  - Choose to rest in God's promises even when you cannot see the outcome.
  - Allow seasons of waiting to strengthen your faith and dependence upon the Lord.
