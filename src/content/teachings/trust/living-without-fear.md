truth: The presence of the Good Shepherd removes the believer's reason to fear.

insight:
  - David wrote these words while walking through the valley rather than after leaving it.
  - The Shepherd calls His people to rest even when circumstances remain difficult.
  - Fear grows when believers focus on danger instead of the Shepherd.
  - The Shepherd's presence is the reason His people can walk confidently through every valley.
  - Rest comes from knowing the Shepherd is watching over His sheep.

application:
  - Remember the Shepherd's presence when fear begins to rise.
  - Choose to trust Christ instead of allowing circumstances to control your thoughts.
  - Encourage yourself with God's promises instead of giving in to fear.
