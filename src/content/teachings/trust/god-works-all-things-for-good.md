truth: God sovereignly works every circumstance for the good of those who love Him and are called according to His purpose.

insight:
  - Closed doors are not always signs of God's absence; they may be the Shepherd directing His sheep along a better path.
  - God's way often differs from our expectations, yet His purpose is always for our good.
  - Delays, disappointments, and difficult seasons can become instruments God uses to prepare His people.
  - Trusting God's providence means believing that He is working even when His purpose is not immediately visible.
  - The Shepherd leads according to His wisdom rather than according to our preferred timing or plans.

application:
  - Trust God's direction when your plans change unexpectedly.
  - Remember that unanswered expectations do not mean God has abandoned you.
  - Commit your plans to the Lord and follow His leading with patience.
  - Thank God that He is working for your good, even when you cannot yet see the outcome.
