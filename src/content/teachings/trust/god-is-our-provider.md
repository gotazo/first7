insight:
  - The Shepherd knows the needs of His sheep before they ask.
  - God's provision flows from His loving care for His people.
  - The Shepherd provides what is truly needed, not merely everything His sheep may desire.
  - Trust in God's provision grows as believers learn to depend on the Shepherd rather than on themselves.
  - Because the Shepherd is faithful, His people can rest instead of living in constant anxiety.

application:
  - Thank God for His faithful provision in every season.
  - Bring your needs to the Lord with confidence instead of anxiety.
  - Learn to distinguish between your needs and your wants.
  - Trust that the Shepherd knows what is best for you.
