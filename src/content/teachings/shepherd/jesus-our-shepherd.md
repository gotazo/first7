insight:
  - Jesus declared Himself to be the Good Shepherd, fulfilling God's promise to care for His people.
  - Unlike a hireling who abandons the flock in danger, Jesus willingly laid down His life to save His sheep.
  - The Shepherd knows each of His sheep personally and faithfully leads them in the right way.
  - Those who belong to Christ learn to recognize His voice and follow Him with trust and obedience.
  - The Shepherd's care provides guidance, protection, and confidence for every season of life.

application:
  - Trust Jesus to lead you through every season of life.
  - Follow Christ's voice by reading and obeying His Word.
  - Rest in the assurance that the Good Shepherd cares for you personally.
  - Thank Jesus for willingly giving His life so that you may have eternal life.
