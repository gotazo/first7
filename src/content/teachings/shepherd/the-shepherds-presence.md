truth: Believers can rest with confidence because the Good Shepherd is with them and faithfully watches over them.

insight:
  - Sheep rest because they know the shepherd is watching over them.
  - The presence of danger does not remove the Shepherd's care or protection.
  - The shepherd's calmness gives the flock confidence to rest, even when danger is nearby.
  - Peace is found in the Shepherd's presence, not in the absence of difficulties.
  - As believers grow in their confidence that the Shepherd is with them, they learn to trust Him more than their circumstances.

application:
  - Remember that Christ is with you in every season of life.
  - Choose to trust the Shepherd instead of allowing fear to control your heart.
  - Rest in God's care, knowing that He watches over His people.
  - Encourage others with the assurance that the Shepherd never abandons His sheep.
