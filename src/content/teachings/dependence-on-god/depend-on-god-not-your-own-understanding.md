truth: Believers should depend upon God rather than their own understanding and trust Him to direct their paths.

insight:
  - Human understanding is limited and cannot provide the complete direction needed for life.
  - Dependence upon God means trusting Him rather than assuming that our own understanding is sufficient.
  - God should be acknowledged in every area of life rather than consulted only when difficulties arise.
  - Trusting God requires surrendering our preferred direction to His wisdom.
  - God is able to direct the paths of those who acknowledge and trust Him.

application:
  - Bring your plans and decisions before God.
  - Do not rely solely upon your own understanding.
  - Ask God for wisdom when you do not know what to do.
  - Trust God's direction even when His way differs from your expectation.
