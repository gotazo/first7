truth: Believers must continually depend upon God because spiritual life and fruitfulness cannot be sustained apart from Him.

insight:
  - Spiritual dependence upon God is not limited to times of trouble.
  - Prayer and communion with God should be part of the believer's continual life.
  - Recognizing our need for God keeps us from relying upon ourselves as though we are spiritually independent.
  - A believer's strength, direction, and spiritual life must remain connected to God.
  - The more we recognize our need for God, the less we depend upon ourselves to sustain what only He can provide.

application:
  - Make prayer and communion with God a continual part of your life.
  - Do not wait for a crisis before seeking God.
  - Acknowledge your dependence upon God in your decisions, work, and daily life.
  - Remain close to Christ rather than trying to live the Christian life independently of Him.
