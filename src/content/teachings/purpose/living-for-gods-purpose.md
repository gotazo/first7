truth: Believers are called to live for God's purpose and faithfully carry out the good works He has given them to do.

insight:
  - Life should be understood in relation to God's purpose rather than merely personal comfort or achievement.
  - Difficult circumstances do not automatically mean that a believer's purpose has ended.
  - The desire to leave this life should not replace the responsibility to continue serving God while He gives us life.
  - God's people are called to use their lives to serve His purposes and impact others.
  - Remaining alive and available to God means continuing to do the work He has given us.

application:
  - Ask God how you can faithfully serve Him where you are.
  - Do not abandon your purpose because of disappointment or hardship.
  - Use your time, abilities, and opportunities for God's glory.
  - Continue faithfully in the work God has entrusted to you.
