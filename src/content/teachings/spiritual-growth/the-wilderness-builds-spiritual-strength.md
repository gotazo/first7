truth: God can use seasons of testing to strengthen believers and teach them to depend upon His Word.

insight:
  - The wilderness came immediately after Jesus was baptized and the Holy Spirit came upon Him.
  - The wilderness was presented as a place of testing rather than comfort.
  - The difficulties of the wilderness exposed what Jesus would depend upon when He was tempted.
  - The wilderness was connected with spiritual strengthening and preparation for what came afterward.
  - Jesus came out of the wilderness in the power of the Holy Spirit, showing the progression from testing to greater effectiveness.
  - The wilderness teaches believers to depend upon what God has spoken rather than their own wisdom and solutions.

application:
  - Do not assume that every difficult season means you have been abandoned by God.
  - Ask what God is teaching you to depend upon during the season.
  - Resist the temptation to rely only on your own wisdom to escape difficulty.
  - Allow God's Word to establish you while you are being tested.
