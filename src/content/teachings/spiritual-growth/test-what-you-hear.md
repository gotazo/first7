insight:
  - A message can sound spiritual without being from God.
  - The fact that someone speaks confidently does not establish that their message is true.
  - Scripture calls believers to examine what they hear.
  - Spiritual maturity includes learning to recognize whether a message agrees with God's revealed truth.
  - Testing what we hear protects us from being directed by words that appear helpful but lead away from God.
application:
  - Do not accept every spiritual claim simply because it sounds convincing.
  - Compare teaching and spiritual messages with Scripture.
  - Take time to examine what you hear before allowing it to direct your decisions.
  - Ask whether a message agrees with the character and truth revealed in God's Word.
  - Remain teachable while also practicing biblical discernment.
