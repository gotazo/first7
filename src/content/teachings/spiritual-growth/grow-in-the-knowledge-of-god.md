truth: Believers are called to continually grow in grace and in the knowledge of the Lord Jesus Christ.

insight:
  - Spiritual growth is a continuing process rather than a destination reached at conversion.
  - Growing in the knowledge of Christ involves becoming increasingly grounded in who He is and what He has revealed.
  - God's Word provides knowledge and understanding that should contribute to spiritual growth.
  - Increased knowledge should produce a life that is increasingly shaped by Christ rather than merely increasing a person's store of information.
  - Spiritual maturity involves continuing to learn, understand, and apply what God has revealed.
  - A believer should not become satisfied with the amount of biblical knowledge already acquired but should continue growing in the knowledge of Christ.
  - Growth in the knowledge of God should be accompanied by growth in faith, obedience, and Christlike living.

application:
  - Continue learning God's Word rather than becoming satisfied with what you already know.
  - Ask God for greater understanding as you study Scripture.
  - Put what you learn into practice so that knowledge produces spiritual growth.
  - Examine whether your understanding of Christ is increasing your faith and obedience.
  - Continue growing in grace even when spiritual maturity takes time.
  - Look for evidence that your knowledge of Christ is shaping your character, decisions, and daily life.
