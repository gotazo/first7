truth: Those who speak as God's representatives must speak faithfully according to what God has revealed.
insight:
  - Speaking in God's name carries responsibility.
  - Scripture warns against speaking from one's own heart while claiming that the Lord has spoken.
  - A person may sincerely believe something and still be wrong about what God has said.
  - God's revealed Word provides a standard by which spiritual speech must be measured.
  - Speaking for God should bring glory to God rather than draw attention to the speaker.
application:
  - Be careful before saying that God has spoken when you are expressing your own thoughts.
  - Compare what you hear and say with Scripture.
  - Speak with humility when you are not certain about something.
  - Do not use God's name to give authority to your personal opinions.
  - Let your words point people toward God and His truth.
