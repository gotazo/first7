truth: Jesus gives true spiritual rest to those who come to Him in faith and learn to follow Him.

insight:
  - Jesus invites everyone who is weary and burdened to come to Him for rest.
  - Spiritual rest is found in a relationship with Christ rather than in favorable circumstances.
  - Christ calls His followers to take His yoke and learn from Him, growing through daily fellowship and obedience.
  - The rest Jesus gives renews the soul and strengthens believers to continue walking faithfully with Him.
  - Spiritual rest comes from trusting Christ's finished work instead of striving in our own strength.

application:
  - Bring your burdens to Jesus instead of carrying them alone.
  - Spend time learning from Christ through His Word each day.
  - Depend on Christ's strength rather than your own abilities.
  - Rest in God's promises even during difficult seasons.
