truth: God calls His people to leave their former ways behind, receive what He provides, and walk in obedience to Him.

insight:
  - Joshua stood before the LORD in filthy garments, representing his condition before God.
  - God commanded that the filthy garments be removed and declared that Joshua's iniquity had passed from him.
  - God did not leave Joshua in the condition in which he was found; He clothed him with a change of raiment.
  - The change in Joshua's garments was followed by a call to walk in God's ways and keep His charge.
  - God's work in a person's life should lead to a changed direction rather than a return to the former way of living.
  - The past should not continue to define a person whom God has called to walk in a new way.
  - Spiritual change involves both receiving what God has done and responding through obedience to His Word.
  - The believer should not continue carrying former patterns, attitudes, or ways of life after God has called him or her to walk differently.

application:
  - Acknowledge the things in your former life that need to be left behind.
  - Do not allow past failures to become the permanent definition of your life.
  - Receive God's mercy and cleansing with humility rather than continuing to condemn yourself.
  - Put away patterns of thought, speech, and conduct that belong to your former way of life.
  - Walk according to God's ways rather than returning to what He has called you to leave.
  - Keep God's charge by taking His Word seriously and putting it into practice.
  - When God brings change into your life, allow that change to be reflected in your daily conduct.
  - Look forward to what God is calling you to become rather than continually living in what is already behind you.
