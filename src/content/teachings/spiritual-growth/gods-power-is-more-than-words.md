truth: The power of the Holy Ghost works in believers so that their faith becomes evident through a life that bears witness to Christ.
insight:
  - The power Jesus promised was connected with the coming of the Holy Ghost.
  - The purpose of this power included becoming witnesses unto Christ.
  - God's power is not limited to outward manifestations; it also works in the transformation of a believer's life.
  - Words alone are not the measure of spiritual life; Scripture calls believers to live out what they hear and believe.
  - The work God has done within a believer can become a testimony to others.
application:
  - Examine whether your faith is visible in the way you live, not only in what you say.
  - Depend upon the Holy Ghost to help you live as a witness for Christ.
  - Allow God's work in your heart to shape your character, conduct, and relationships.
  - Put God's Word into practice rather than being satisfied with hearing it.
  - Let your transformed life point others toward Jesus Christ.
