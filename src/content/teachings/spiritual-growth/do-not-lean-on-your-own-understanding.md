insight:
  - Our own thoughts and feelings are not an infallible guide.
  - Loving God does not mean that every decision we make will automatically be right.
  - Spiritual maturity includes learning God's ways rather than simply following whatever feels right.
  - God's direction should be sought with humility rather than assumed from our own desires.
  - Dependence upon God requires us to acknowledge Him in the decisions we make.
application:
  - Bring important decisions before God instead of relying only on your own reasoning.
  - Compare your desires and conclusions with Scripture.
  - Ask God for wisdom when you do not know what to do.
  - Be willing to change direction when God's Word exposes that your thinking is wrong.
  - Let trust in God become greater than confidence in your own understanding.
