insight:
  - Paul received a calling from God and continued in the work entrusted to him.
  - Following God's direction does not mean refusing to learn from other people.
  - Other people's opinions should not replace God's direction for the work He has entrusted to us.
  - Faithfulness requires continuing even when the path does not immediately make sense.
  - A God-given assignment should be carried out with perseverance rather than constantly abandoned for another direction.
application:
  - Remember what God has clearly entrusted to you.
  - Seek counsel without surrendering your responsibility to obey God.
  - Do not allow pressure from others to pull you away from biblical obedience.
  - Continue faithfully when circumstances become difficult.
  - Keep your attention on Christ as you pursue what God has called you to do.
