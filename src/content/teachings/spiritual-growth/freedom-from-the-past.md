insight:
  - Christ's work of restoration calls people into a transformed way of living.
  - Freedom in Christ is not merely leaving the past but walking in obedience to God.
  - God's restoring work is meant to produce lasting spiritual change.
  - The life Christ gives is not intended to return to former patterns of sin and bondage.
  - True freedom is found by continuing to walk in the new life Christ provides.

application:
  - Leave behind attitudes and habits that belong to your former way of life.
  - Walk daily in the freedom Christ has given you through obedient living.
  - Continue growing in your relationship with Christ instead of returning to old patterns.
