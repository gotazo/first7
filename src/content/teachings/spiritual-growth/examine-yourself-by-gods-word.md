insight:
  - God's Word reveals what is present within a person's heart and life.
  - Hearing Scripture without obeying it leaves the revealed problem unchanged.
  - Self-examination requires honesty about what God's Word reveals rather than focusing only on the faults of others.
  - God's Word should lead believers from recognition of wrong into repentance and change.
  - Continually looking into God's Word and obeying it produces a life that is increasingly aligned with God's truth.

application:
  - Allow Scripture to examine your thoughts, motives, and actions.
  - Ask God to reveal areas of your life that need correction.
  - When God's Word exposes something wrong, respond with repentance and obedience.
  - Examine yourself before focusing on the faults of others.
