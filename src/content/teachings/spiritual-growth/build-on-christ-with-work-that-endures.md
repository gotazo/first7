truth: Believers must build faithfully upon Jesus Christ, knowing that their work will be tested and its true quality revealed.
insight:
  - Jesus Christ is the only foundation upon which believers can build; no other foundation can replace Him.
  - Having the right foundation does not remove the responsibility to build carefully. Paul warns every person to take heed how he builds upon Christ.
  - The different building materials illustrate work of differing quality, and the fire reveals what kind of work has been done.
  - Work that endures receives a reward, while work that is burned results in loss. Paul distinguishes the loss of a person's work from the person's own salvation in this passage.
application:
  - Examine whether your life and service are built upon Jesus Christ and His Word.
  - Consider the quality and motives of the work you do for the Lord.
  - Do not measure the value of your service by outward appearance or immediate success alone.
  - Serve God faithfully and allow Scripture to correct the way you live and work.
