truth: God works through all things for the good of those who love Him and are called according to His purpose.
insight:
  - Scripture does not say that everything that happens is good.
  - God can work through painful or unjust circumstances without approving of the wrong that occurred.
  - Joseph's brothers intended evil against him, yet God used what happened to accomplish His purpose.
  - Difficult experiences do not place believers outside God's ability to work.
  - Looking to God helps believers see their circumstances in light of His purpose rather than only through their pain.
application:
  - Bring painful experiences honestly before God.
  - Do not call evil good simply because God can use it.
  - Trust that God can work through circumstances you cannot undo.
  - Look for how God is shaping your faith and obedience through difficult seasons.
  - Keep your hope in God's purpose rather than allowing past events to determine your future.
