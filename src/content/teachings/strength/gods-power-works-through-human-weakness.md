truth: God's strength is manifested through human weakness as believers depend upon His grace and power.
insight:
  - Paul openly acknowledged that he came in weakness, fear, and much trembling.
  - Human weakness does not mean that God cannot work through a believer.
  - God's power is not dependent upon human strength or ability.
  - Paul learned to glory in his weaknesses because they provided an opportunity for the power of Christ to be manifested.
  - Recognizing weakness can lead a believer to depend more fully upon God's grace and power.
application:
  - Do not allow your weaknesses to convince you that God cannot use you.
  - Bring your limitations honestly before God rather than trying to depend entirely upon your own strength.
  - Depend upon God's grace when you recognize that your own ability is insufficient.
  - Let weakness lead you toward greater dependence upon Christ rather than discouragement.
  - Serve faithfully with what God has given you, trusting His power rather than your own ability.
