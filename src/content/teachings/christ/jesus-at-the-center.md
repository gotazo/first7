truth: Jesus must have the preeminence in every area of the believer's life.

insight:
  - Jesus is not meant to occupy only one part of the believer's life but to have the preeminence in all things.
  - Surrender becomes possible when Christ is valued above everything else.
  - The things we love most can reveal what occupies the center of our hearts.
  - A Christ-centered life places personal desires, plans, and possessions under His lordship.
  - True surrender is not merely giving something up but giving Christ His rightful place.

application:
  - Examine what currently has the greatest place in your heart.
  - Put Christ at the center of your decisions, priorities, and desires.
  - Surrender anything that has begun to compete with Christ for first place.
  - Ask whether your daily life demonstrates that Jesus has the preeminence.
