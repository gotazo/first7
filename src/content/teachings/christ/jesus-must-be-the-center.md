truth: Jesus must remain at the center of the believer's life because spiritual life and fruitfulness depend upon Him.

insight:
  - Believers cannot live spiritually independent of Christ.
  - Righteousness is not something believers can establish through self-reliance.
  - Remaining close to Jesus is more important than merely seeking what He can give.
  - The believer's relationship with Christ must remain central in prayer, obedience, service, and daily life.
  - A life centered on Jesus depends upon Him rather than trusting in personal goodness or ability.

application:
  - Make Jesus the center of your daily life rather than merely seeking His blessings.
  - Remain close to Christ through prayer, God's Word, and obedience.
  - Do not depend upon your own righteousness or ability.
  - Examine whether Jesus is truly at the center of your decisions, desires, and priorities.
