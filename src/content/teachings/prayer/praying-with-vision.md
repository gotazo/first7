insight:
  - Faith gives believers confidence to pray according to what God has revealed rather than according to what they presently see.
  - Spiritual vision strengthens prayer by fixing the heart upon God's promises instead of present circumstances.
  - Doubt focuses on visible obstacles, but faith continues to look toward God's ability to accomplish His purposes.
  - God calls His people to pray with expectation because His power is greater than every impossible situation.
  - Prayer rooted in faith continues to trust God even before His answer becomes visible.

application:
  - Bring your requests to God with confidence in His promises.
  - Allow God's Word to shape your expectations as you pray.
  - Continue praying in faith without allowing present circumstances to weaken your trust in God.
