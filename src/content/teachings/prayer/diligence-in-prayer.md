truth: Diligent prayer approaches God with faith, persistence, and confidence that He hears those who diligently seek Him.

insight:
  - Diligence means continuing faithfully rather than abandoning prayer when an answer does not come immediately.
  - Prayer should be rooted in knowing who God is rather than being driven only by fear or panic.
  - Faith in prayer includes believing that God is and that He rewards those who diligently seek Him.
  - Persistent prayer keeps the believer engaged with God rather than treating prayer as a last response to trouble.
  - Growth in prayer requires consistency rather than continually skipping the disciplines that strengthen our relationship with God.

application:
  - Establish a consistent pattern of prayer.
  - Continue praying when answers appear delayed.
  - Bring your fears to God while remembering who He is.
  - Seek God diligently rather than turning to prayer only in emergencies.
  - Let persistence in prayer strengthen your faith.
