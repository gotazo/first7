truth: Prayer and giving are expressions of trust, worship, and dependence upon God.

insight:
  - Prayer turns the heart toward God in dependence rather than relying upon human strength.
  - Giving provides an opportunity to honor God with what He has entrusted to us.
  - God looks at the heart behind acts of prayer and giving rather than outward performance alone.
  - Prayer and generosity should flow from genuine devotion to God rather than an attempt to manipulate Him.
  - A life of prayer and giving reflects trust in God's provision and faithfulness.

application:
  - Bring your needs and concerns to God through prayer with thanksgiving.
  - Give generously and sincerely as an act of worship.
  - Examine your motives so that prayer and giving remain expressions of genuine devotion to God.
