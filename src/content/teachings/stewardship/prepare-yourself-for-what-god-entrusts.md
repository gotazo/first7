insight:
  - Simon obeyed Jesus even after an unproductive night.
  - The great catch exposed a practical limitation in the fishermen's existing nets.
  - The fishermen needed the help of their partners to handle the catch.
  - Preparation and stewardship matter alongside faith and obedience.
  - Greater responsibility requires corresponding growth in skill, discipline, and capacity.

application:
  - Take practical responsibility for the work God has placed before you.
  - Develop the skills needed to serve faithfully.
  - Strengthen your habits and preparation before responsibilities increase.
  - Seek help when a task exceeds your present capacity.
  - Do not treat prayer as a substitute for preparation and faithful work.
