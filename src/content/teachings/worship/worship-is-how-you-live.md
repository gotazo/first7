truth: Worship is expressed not only through praise and outward acts but through a life surrendered to God.

insight:
  - Worship involves more than singing, instruments, or outward expressions.
  - A believer's whole life can be offered to God as an act of worship.
  - Outward expressions of worship should come from a heart that is genuinely directed toward God.
  - What happens in worship should affect the way a believer lives outside of gathered worship.
  - Surrender to God is part of presenting ourselves to Him as a living sacrifice.
  - The way a believer works, speaks, serves, and treats others should agree with the worship offered to God.
  - Worship is not limited to a particular place, song, or moment; it should shape everyday life.

application:
  - Offer your daily life to God as an act of worship.
  - Examine whether your conduct agrees with the worship you express with your words.
  - Do not limit worship to singing or outward expressions in church.
  - Let your choices, attitudes, words, and actions honor God.
  - Practice surrender to God in ordinary responsibilities and decisions.
  - Ask whether the way you live outside church reflects the God you worship.
