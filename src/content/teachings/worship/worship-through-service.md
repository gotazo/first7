truth: Worship includes giving ourselves to God through faithful service.

insight:
  - Joshua connected choosing whom to serve with his commitment to the LORD.
  - Serving God is an expression of belonging to Him.
  - Worship is not complete as an outward expression if it does not affect how we give ourselves to God in service.
  - Christian service is not limited to activities inside a church building.
  - Believers can honor God by serving others with the abilities and opportunities He has given them.
  - Jesus demonstrated that serving others is consistent with the life of one who belongs to God.
  - Service should flow from a heart surrendered to God rather than from a desire for recognition.

application:
  - Choose to serve the Lord with your life rather than living only for yourself.
  - Look for opportunities to serve others with what God has given you.
  - Serve faithfully even when the service receives little recognition.
  - Do not limit your service to church activities; look for ways to serve at home and in your community.
  - Use your abilities and opportunities to benefit others and honor God.
  - Examine whether your willingness to serve reflects the surrender you profess in worship.
