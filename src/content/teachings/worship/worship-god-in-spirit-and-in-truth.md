truth: True worship is directed toward God and offered in spirit and in truth.

insight:
  - Jesus distinguishes true worship from worship offered without a true understanding of whom we worship.
  - Worship is not merely an outward activity; Jesus says that the Father seeks those who worship Him in spirit and in truth.
  - Knowing God matters to the way we worship Him.
  - Worship should be grounded in truth rather than in human ideas or outward religious activity alone.
  - Because God is a Spirit, those who worship Him must worship Him in the way Jesus describes.
  - True worship begins with God rather than with the worshipper's feelings or preferences.

application:
  - Worship God with a sincere heart rather than relying only on outward expressions.
  - Seek to know God through His Word so that your worship is grounded in truth.
  - Examine whether your worship is centered on God or on your own feelings and experiences.
  - Let what you know about God shape the way you honor and respond to Him.
  - Worship God consistently in both public and private life.
  - Do not separate worship from truth, obedience, and a growing knowledge of God.
