truth: God is able to bring life and restoration into situations that appear completely hopeless.

insight:
  - Ezekiel was brought into a valley filled with dry bones, a picture of a situation where life appeared to be completely gone.
  - The condition of the bones was obvious, yet God asked whether they could live.
  - Situations that appear dead can cause people to become anchored in bitterness, unforgiveness, rejection, broken relationships, or other painful outcomes.
  - God's question to Ezekiel redirected attention from the condition of the bones to what God was able to do.
  - What appears to be a dead end is not necessarily the end of what God can do.

application:
  - Do not allow a painful situation to become the final definition of your hope.
  - Bring situations that appear beyond repair before God.
  - Refuse to become permanently anchored in bitterness or unforgiveness.
  - Trust God's ability to work even when you cannot see how restoration could happen.
