truth: Believers can trust God in difficult seasons because His presence remains with them through the valley.

insight:
  - David spoke of the Shepherd's care while walking through the valley rather than only after leaving it.
  - God's presence does not disappear when His people enter difficult circumstances.
  - The presence of danger does not require believers to live in fear.
  - God's comfort gives believers courage to continue walking through difficult seasons.
  - Trust rests upon who God is and His presence with us rather than upon the absence of trouble.

application:
  - Trust God's presence when you are walking through a difficult season.
  - Do not assume that trouble means God has abandoned you.
  - Bring your fears to God and continue following Him through the valley.
  - Remember God's faithfulness when circumstances make you afraid.
