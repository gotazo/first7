truth: Jesus Christ securely keeps His people, and nothing can separate them from His faithful care.

insight:
  - The security of the sheep rests in the strength of the Shepherd, not in the strength of the sheep.
  - Jesus gives eternal life to His sheep and promises that no one can snatch them from His hand.
  - The Shepherd's presence gives confidence even when His people walk through difficult valleys (Psalm 23:4).
  - Believers can cast every care upon God because He continually cares for them (1 Peter 5:7).
  - Confidence in God's protection enables believers to rest instead of living in constant fear.

application:
  - Trust Christ's promises when fear and uncertainty arise.
  - Rest in God's care instead of relying on your own strength.
  - Bring your worries to the Lord, knowing that He cares for you.
  - Encourage fellow believers with the assurance found in Christ.
