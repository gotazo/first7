insight:
  - God's delays are often seasons of preparation rather than denial.
  - Joseph endured years of suffering before God raised him to preserve many lives.
  - David was prepared through seasons of waiting before becoming king.
  - God often strengthens His servants before entrusting them with greater responsibility.
  - Waiting develops trust in God's wisdom rather than confidence in our own plans.

application:
  - Trust God's wisdom when His answers seem delayed.
  - Continue obeying the Lord while you wait.
  - Resist becoming discouraged by comparing your journey with others.
  - Remember God's past faithfulness as you wait for His future provision.
