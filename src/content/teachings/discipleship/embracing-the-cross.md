truth: Following Christ requires daily self-denial, surrender, and willingness to follow Him regardless of the cost.

insight:
  - Jesus calls His followers to deny themselves rather than making self-interest the center of their lives.
  - Taking up the cross means accepting the cost of following Christ and remaining willing to obey Him.
  - The Christian life is not centered on preserving our own will but on following Christ.
  - Surrender requires yielding our desires and plans to God's will.
  - Following Jesus is a daily commitment rather than a one-time decision.

application:
  - Ask whether your own will or Christ's will is directing your life.
  - Be willing to surrender desires that conflict with God's Word.
  - Choose obedience to Christ even when it requires personal sacrifice.
  - Take up your cross daily and continue following Jesus.
