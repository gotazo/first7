truth: Jesus responded to temptation by standing on God's Word and remaining faithful to God.

insight:
  - Jesus experienced temptation without yielding to it.
  - Each temptation was answered with Scripture rather than with compromise.
  - The enemy even used Scripture in the second temptation, but Jesus answered by correctly applying God's Word.
  - Jesus refused to satisfy a legitimate physical need through disobedience to God.
  - Jesus refused to test God and refused to worship anyone other than God.
  - Temptation does not require sin; sin occurs when a person yields to temptation rather than remaining obedient to God.
  - God's Word provides believers with truth by which tempting suggestions can be examined and rejected.

application:
  - Know God's Word so that you can recognize what agrees with it and what does not.
  - When tempted, refuse to compromise God's commands for immediate satisfaction.
  - Do not use Scripture to justify actions that contradict God's will.
  - Follow Jesus' example by answering temptation with truth and obedience.
  - Pray for strength when facing temptation rather than relying only on your own determination.
  - When tempted, remember that faithfulness to God is more important than immediate relief or reward.
