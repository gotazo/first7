truth: God has given mankind dominion, and believers must understand and faithfully exercise the authority entrusted to them under Christ.

insight:
  - God created mankind in His image and likeness and gave mankind dominion over His creation.
  - Authority is connected with responsibility; what God entrusts to a person is meant to be exercised according to His purpose.
  - Believers should understand what God has entrusted to them rather than living as though they have received nothing from Him.
  - Authority should never become a reason for pride or control over others, because everything entrusted to us remains under God's authority.
  - Understanding what God has given us helps us live with confidence, responsibility, and obedience.

application:
  - Study what Scripture teaches about the authority Christ has given His followers.
  - Do not live as though God has left you without spiritual responsibility.
  - Use whatever authority God has entrusted to you to serve His purposes.
  - Remain submitted to Christ while exercising the authority He has given.
