truth: God can use what His people have endured and how He has sustained them to become a testimony of His work and faithfulness.

insight:
  - What God has brought a person through can become part of the testimony of His work in that person's life.
  - A person's scars can remind others that God has sustained them through what they endured.
  - The visible result may not reveal the brokenness and restoration that took place along the way.
  - What God has rebuilt in a person's life can become a reason for worship rather than shame.
  - The story of God's faithfulness through suffering can encourage others who are facing their own trials.

application:
  - Remember what God has brought you through and give Him the glory.
  - Do not be ashamed of the places where God has restored you.
  - Use your testimony to encourage others who are going through difficult seasons.
  - Let what God has done in your life strengthen your worship and gratitude.
