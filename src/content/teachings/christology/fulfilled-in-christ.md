truth: Jesus Christ completely satisfies His people, so their deepest need is fulfilled in Him.

insight:
  - '"I shall not want" points first to being satisfied in the Shepherd rather than merely receiving things from Him.'
  - The believer's contentment is rooted in Christ Himself, not in changing circumstances.
  - Psalm 23 presents the Shepherd as the source of complete fulfillment, leading His sheep to rest because they are secure in Him.
  - A heart that is fulfilled in Christ is able to trust His leadership instead of constantly striving for more.
  - The Shepherd invites His people to find their satisfaction in His presence before seeking satisfaction anywhere else.

application:
  - Thank Christ for being your greatest treasure.
  - Evaluate whether your satisfaction is rooted in Jesus or in changing circumstances.
  - Practice contentment by remembering what God has already provided in Christ.
  - Rest in the Shepherd, knowing that He is sufficient for every season of life.
