truth: Believers must continue to grow in the truth, depending on the Holy Spirit's guidance and remaining grounded in God's Word.

insight:
  - Jesus calls the Holy Spirit the Spirit of truth and says that He will guide His disciples into all truth.
  - Having begun the Christian life does not mean that a person already understands every truth of Scripture.
  - Growth in understanding requires a willingness to learn, receive correction, and move beyond former ways of thinking.
  - The Holy Spirit's guidance does not make careful attention to Scripture unnecessary; believers are responsible to examine what they hear.
  - As believers grow in truth, they become better equipped to recognize teaching that does not agree with God's Word.
  - Spiritual maturity involves more than gaining information; understanding the truth should shape how a believer thinks and lives.

application:
  - Ask God to help you understand His Word and recognize the truth.
  - Read Scripture carefully rather than relying only on what others tell you it means.
  - Remain teachable and accept correction when your understanding needs to change.
  - Test what you hear against the teaching of Scripture.
  - Do not assume that spiritual maturity means you have nothing more to learn.
  - Put the truth you understand into practice as you continue to grow in Christ.
