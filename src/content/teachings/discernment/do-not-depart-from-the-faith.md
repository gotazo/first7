truth: Believers must remain steadfast in the faith and refuse teachings that lead them away from the truth of God's Word.

insight:
  - Scripture warns that some will depart from the faith by giving heed to seducing spirits and doctrines of devils.
  - Teaching influences the way people understand and respond to spiritual matters.
  - A teaching should not be accepted merely because it sounds spiritual or is presented in a religious setting.
  - Remaining in the faith requires attention to what is being taught and whether it agrees with Scripture.
  - A person can remain outwardly involved in religious activities while becoming confused about what the faith teaches.
  - Recognizing error requires returning to the truth of God's Word rather than relying only on impressions or claims made by others.

application:
  - Examine what you believe in the light of Scripture.
  - Do not accept a teaching merely because a respected person presents it.
  - Compare spiritual claims with the teaching and context of God's Word.
  - Be willing to correct your understanding when Scripture exposes an error.
  - Continue growing in your knowledge of the faith rather than assuming you already understand everything.
  - Hold firmly to the truth of God's Word when other teachings contradict it.
