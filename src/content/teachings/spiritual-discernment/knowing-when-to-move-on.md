  - "Being tired, frustrated, or dissatisfied with something is not by itself proof that it is time to leave."
  - "Outgrowing something should be distinguished from temporary weariness, disappointment, or lack of discipline."
  - "A wise transition considers both the reason for leaving and the responsibility of what comes next."
  - "A new opportunity is not automatically evidence that God is directing a person to change course."
  - "Moving forward wisely does not require despising the season or responsibility that came before."

application:
  - "Seek God's wisdom before making major transitions."
  - "Examine whether the desire to leave is caused by temporary emotion, weariness, or a genuine change in responsibility."
  - "Consider what God has taught you through the present season before moving forward."
  - "Make necessary transitions with wisdom, patience, and responsibility."
  - "Do not allow fear of change or attachment to comfort to prevent obedience to God."
