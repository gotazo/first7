truth: Spiritual maturity requires discernment to recognize our spiritual condition and respond wisely according to God's Word.

insight:
  - Believers can move through different stages and circumstances in their spiritual journey and need to recognize where they are.
  - Failing to understand where we are can cause us to respond to a situation in ways that do not address what God is developing in us.
  - Physical circumstances do not always reveal a person's spiritual condition.
  - Spiritual maturity includes learning to distinguish spiritual realities from what is merely visible in our circumstances.
  - Knowing where we are spiritually helps us understand how to respond rather than treating every situation in the same way.
  - Becoming anchored in Christ and His Word gives believers a foundation from which they can understand what they are facing.

application:
  - Examine your spiritual condition rather than judging your life only by outward circumstances.
  - Ask God for wisdom to understand what you are facing.
  - Let God's Word guide your response to the season you are in.
  - Do not confuse outward success or difficulty with spiritual maturity.
