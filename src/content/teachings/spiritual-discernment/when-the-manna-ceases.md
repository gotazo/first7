truth: The form of God's provision can change as His people enter a different circumstance or responsibility.

insight:
  - Manna was God's provision for Israel during their wilderness journey.
  - The manna ceased when Israel began eating the produce of Canaan.
  - The end of one form of provision was not the end of God's care for Israel.
  - A previous provision should not be treated as the only way God can provide.
  - Faithfulness requires receiving God's provision in the circumstances He has given rather than demanding a former method.

application:
  - Thank God for how He has provided in past seasons.
  - Do not make a past method of provision an object of trust.
  - Pay attention to the responsibilities and opportunities of your present circumstances.
  - Receive God's provision with gratitude rather than comparing it with a former season.
  - Let Scripture shape your trust rather than fear when familiar support changes.
