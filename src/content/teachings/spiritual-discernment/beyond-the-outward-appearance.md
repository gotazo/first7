truth: What people see outwardly does not reveal the whole spiritual condition of a person or people before God.

insight:
  - The woman who came to Jesus was known by the religious leaders according to what they believed about her, and they questioned Jesus because they saw her as a sinner who should not be touching Him.
  - Jesus did not respond to the woman merely according to the reputation the religious leaders attached to her.
  - "The contrast is striking: the priests saw a prostitute, while Jesus saw Israel."
  - What appeared to the priests to be merely a sinful woman at Jesus' feet carried a deeper picture of God's relationship with a people who had turned away from Him.
  - God's perspective can reach beyond what is immediately visible and reveal a deeper spiritual reality.

application:
  - Do not assume that what you see outwardly tells the whole story of what God is doing.
  - Be careful about defining people entirely by their reputation or past.
  - Examine your own spiritual condition rather than trusting in outward religious appearance.
  - Ask God for discernment to see people and situations according to His Word.
  - Remain humble when God reveals a reality that differs from your first impression.
