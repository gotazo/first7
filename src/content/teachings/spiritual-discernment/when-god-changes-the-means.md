truth: God may change the means by which He provides while remaining faithful to sustain His people.

insight:
  - God directed Elijah to the brook and later directed him to Zarephath.
  - The drying brook did not mean that God had abandoned Elijah.
  - A change in circumstances can require renewed attention to God's direction.
  - Faithfulness includes obeying God's present instruction rather than clinging to a former method.
  - God's provision should not be confused with the particular means through which it comes.

application:
  - Do not assume that a changed circumstance means God has failed you.
  - Seek God's wisdom when a familiar source or method changes.
  - Be willing to release an old method when God's Word directs otherwise.
  - Distinguish trust in God from attachment to a particular form of provision.
  - Obey clearly grounded direction without acting impulsively.
