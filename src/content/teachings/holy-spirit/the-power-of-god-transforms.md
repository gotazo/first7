truth: God's power works in believers through the Holy Spirit to produce genuine transformation rather than merely outward display.
insight:
  - The power of God is not limited to miraculous signs or outward manifestations.
  - Transformation begins within the heart as a person recognizes their need for God.
  - The Holy Spirit works within believers to produce a changed life.
  - God's power enables believers not only to be changed themselves but also to serve and help others.
  - Faith should rest in the power of God rather than in human wisdom or persuasive speech.
application:
  - Examine whether your faith is producing genuine transformation in your heart and life.
  - Depend on the Holy Spirit rather than relying only on human ability or persuasive words.
  - Allow God's Word and Spirit to shape your thoughts, character, and actions.
  - Look for opportunities to use what God has given you to serve and strengthen others.
  - Let the reality of God's work in your life become visible through faithful living.
