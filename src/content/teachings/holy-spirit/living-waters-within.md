truth: The Holy Spirit gives believers an inward source of spiritual life that flows from their relationship with Christ.

insight:
  - Jesus describes the Holy Spirit as living water flowing from within those who believe in Him.
  - Spiritual life is not merely something received externally; God works within His people through His Spirit.
  - The life God gives within a believer is intended to flow outward rather than remain hidden.
  - The Holy Spirit produces spiritual life that cannot be created by human effort alone.
  - A believer's inward life with God should become a source of blessing to others.

application:
  - Depend upon the Holy Spirit for spiritual life and strength.
  - Allow God's work within you to influence the way you live and serve others.
  - Guard your inward spiritual life through prayer, God's Word, and obedience.
  - Ask God to make your life a blessing to those around you.
