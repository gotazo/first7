insight:
  - Fear does not come from God but can keep believers from faithfully following His will.
  - God provides power, love, and a sound mind to equip His people for faithful living and service.
  - A sound mind enables believers to think clearly, remain steadfast, and trust God in difficult circumstances.
  - God's presence gives confidence to obey Him instead of yielding to fear.
  - Believers can rely on what God has given rather than being controlled by fear.

application:
  - Trust God's promises when fear tries to discourage you.
  - Choose to respond with power, love, and a sound mind in every situation.
  - Rely on God's strength to remain faithful in the work He has called you to do.
