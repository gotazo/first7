  - The purpose of a spiritual encounter is to draw God's servants into obedience to His will rather than to seek extraordinary experiences.

application:
  - Seek God Himself rather than extraordinary experiences.
  - Respond with humility and obedience when God reveals His truth.
  - Depend on God's strength to accomplish the work He calls you to do.
