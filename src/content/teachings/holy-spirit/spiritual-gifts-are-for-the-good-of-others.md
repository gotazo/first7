truth: God gives spiritual gifts through the Holy Spirit so that believers may benefit and strengthen one another.
insight:
  - Spiritual gifts are given by God and distributed according to His will.
  - Different believers receive different gifts, so no believer possesses everything needed by the body of Christ.
  - What God gives to one believer can become a means of blessing and strengthening another.
  - Spiritual gifts are not given merely for personal distinction but for the benefit of others.
  - The diversity of gifts allows believers to serve one another through what God has entrusted to them.
application:
  - Recognize that what God has given you is also intended to benefit others.
  - Do not compare your gift with another person's gift; different gifts have different purposes.
  - Look for opportunities to use what God has given you to serve and strengthen others.
  - Receive the ministry of other believers with humility when they supply what you do not have.
  - Use spiritual gifts in a way that points people toward God rather than toward yourself.
