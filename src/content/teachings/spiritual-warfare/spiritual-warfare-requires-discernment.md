truth: Believers must understand spiritual opposition and use the spiritual weapons God has provided to stand faithfully against it.

insight:
  - Spiritual warfare is real, but believers are not left without what they need to stand against it.
  - God provides spiritual weapons for spiritual battles rather than leaving believers to depend upon human strength.
  - Effective spiritual warfare requires discernment about what is being faced rather than responding without understanding.
  - The believer's response to spiritual opposition must be governed by God's Word and the resources He has provided.
  - Standing against the enemy requires both spiritual readiness and dependence upon God.

application:
  - Learn what Scripture teaches about spiritual warfare.
  - Examine spiritual claims and situations carefully rather than responding from fear.
  - Put on the whole armour of God.
  - Use God's Word, faith, prayer, and righteousness as He has instructed.
  - Ask God for wisdom when facing spiritual opposition.
