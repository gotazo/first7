truth: God calls His people to walk in His ways and keep His charge through obedient living.

insight:
  - After Joshua's filthy garments were removed and his iniquity had passed from him, he was called to walk in God's ways.
  - God's work of cleansing and restoration was followed by a clear call to obedience.
  - Walking in God's ways means bringing one's conduct into agreement with what God has commanded.
  - Knowing God's ways should lead to obedience rather than remaining only as knowledge.
  - Obedience involves keeping God's charge and taking His instructions seriously.
  - A changed condition should produce a changed direction in life.
  - Continuing to walk in God's ways requires ongoing faithfulness rather than a single act of obedience.

application:
  - Learn God's ways through His Word and seek to understand what He has commanded.
  - Put what you learn into practice rather than being satisfied with knowing it.
  - Examine whether your daily conduct agrees with God's Word.
  - When God reveals something that needs to change, respond through obedience.
  - Do not return to former ways after God has shown you a better way to walk.
  - Practice faithfulness in the ordinary decisions and responsibilities of daily life.
  - Continue walking in God's ways even when obedience requires patience or sacrifice.
