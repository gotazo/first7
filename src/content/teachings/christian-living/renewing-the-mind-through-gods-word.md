  - Continually receiving God's Word helps believers develop a new way of thinking and living.
  - A renewed mind enables believers to recognize and follow the will of God.

application:
  - Read God's Word regularly and allow it to examine your thinking.
  - Do not merely hear Scripture; practice what it teaches.
  - Replace patterns that contradict God's Word with thoughts and actions that honor God.
