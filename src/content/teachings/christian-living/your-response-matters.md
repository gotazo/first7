truth: Believers should respond to difficult situations with wisdom and self-control rather than allowing anger or temptation to control their conduct.

insight:
  - Difficult words or circumstances can provoke a reaction, but believers are responsible for how they respond.
  - A quick and uncontrolled response can increase conflict rather than resolve it.
  - Scripture connects self-control with the ability to restrain anger and respond wisely.
  - Jesus provides an example of responding to opposition without being drawn into sinful behavior.
  - A believer's response should be governed by God's Word rather than by the pressure of the moment.
  - Responding well requires more than avoiding outward conflict; it requires bringing the heart under the rule of God's truth.

application:
  - Slow down before responding when you are angry, offended, or pressured.
  - Consider whether your response will bring peace or increase conflict.
  - Refuse to answer harsh words with words that deepen anger.
  - Ask God for wisdom and self-control when you are being provoked.
  - Let Scripture guide your response instead of allowing emotions to determine your words.
  - When you respond wrongly, acknowledge it before God and make the necessary correction.
