truth: Believers are called to leave what is behind and press forward in the life God has set before them.

insight:
  - Paul describes a deliberate decision to stop looking backward and to reach toward what is ahead.
  - Past experiences can continue to occupy the heart when a person repeatedly returns to them in thought.
  - Unforgiveness can keep past offenses active in a person's present life.
  - Leaving the past behind does not deny that painful things happened; it means refusing to remain governed by them.
  - The sermon uses the picture of an old garment to describe former conditions that should no longer be carried into a new season of life.
  - A person should not allow former failures, disappointments, rejection, or wounds to become a permanent definition of who they are.
  - Moving forward requires turning attention toward what God is doing rather than continually trying to relive or prove something about the past.

application:
  - Identify past experiences that continue to control your thoughts, attitudes, or decisions.
  - Bring painful memories and unresolved offenses before God rather than continually carrying them.
  - Forgive those who have wronged you and refuse to keep rehearsing their offenses.
  - Do not allow former failures or disappointments to become your permanent identity.
  - Stop measuring your present life by what happened in the past.
  - Give your attention to the life God has placed before you.
  - Press forward in faith and obedience instead of continually looking backward.
