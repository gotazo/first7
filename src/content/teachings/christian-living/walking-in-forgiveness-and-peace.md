truth: Believers are called to forgive others and pursue peace as an expression of their identity as children of God.

insight:
  - Forgiveness is part of the believer's response to the forgiveness received from God.
  - Strife and resentment damage relationships and contradict the call to walk in peace.
  - Believers should not participate in gossip or conversations that create division.
  - Peacemaking requires choosing words and actions that build rather than destroy relationships.
  - Forgiveness releases the believer from continuing to hold resentment against others.

application:
  - Forgive those who have wronged you.
  - Refuse to participate in gossip and conversations that create division.
  - Pursue peace whenever it is within your power.
  - Choose words that build others up rather than create strife.
