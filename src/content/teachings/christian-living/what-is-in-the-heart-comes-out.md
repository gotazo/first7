truth: What fills the heart eventually becomes evident through a person's words and actions.

insight:
  - Jesus connects a person's words with what is stored within the heart.
  - Speech can reveal what is abundant within a person's heart.
  - Good words should flow from good treasure rather than merely from outward attempts to appear righteous.
  - Wrong words can expose attitudes, desires, and thoughts that need to be addressed within the heart.
  - Changing speech consistently requires attention to what is being stored within the heart.
  - The words a believer speaks should agree with the truth and character God desires within them.

application:
  - Listen carefully to your own words and consider what they reveal about your heart.
  - When wrong words come out, examine the attitude or desire behind them.
  - Do not merely try to control outward speech while ignoring the condition of the heart.
  - Fill your heart and mind with God's Word and things that are pleasing to Him.
  - Replace bitterness, envy, anger, and fear with attitudes that agree with God's truth.
  - Let your words become an honest expression of a heart that is being changed by God.
