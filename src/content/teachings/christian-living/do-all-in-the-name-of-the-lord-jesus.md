truth: Everything a believer says and does should be done in a way that honors the Lord Jesus.

insight:
  - The command to act in the name of Jesus applies to everything we do in word and deed, not only to prayer.
  - The name of Jesus is not merely a phrase to attach to our words; it represents the person and authority of the Lord Jesus.
  - Living in the name of Jesus means that our conduct should agree with who He is and what He teaches.
  - A believer's words and actions should bring honor rather than dishonor to the name of Christ.
  - Using the name of Jesus rightly involves living under His authority rather than treating His name as a formula for obtaining what we want.
  - Giving thanks to God is part of doing all things in the name of the Lord Jesus.

application:
  - Examine your words and actions in light of the name of Jesus.
  - Before speaking or acting, consider whether what you are doing honors Christ.
  - Do not treat the name of Jesus as a formula; submit your choices to His authority.
  - Let your conduct represent your relationship with Christ in everyday life.
  - Give thanks to God in whatever you do.
  - Practice honoring Jesus not only in prayer but also in your relationships, work, decisions, and daily conduct.
