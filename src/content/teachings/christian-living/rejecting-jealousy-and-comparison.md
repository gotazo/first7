truth: Believers must reject jealousy and comparison and learn to rejoice in what God does for others.

insight:
  - Comparison can produce jealousy, envy, competition, and strife.
  - Believers are called to rejoice with others rather than resent what God has done for them.
  - Another person's blessing does not diminish what God is able to do in your own life.
  - Love does not envy or seek superiority over others.
  - Humility turns attention away from competition and toward serving and building others up.

application:
  - Refuse to measure your worth by comparing yourself with others.
  - Rejoice when God blesses another person.
  - Ask God to remove jealousy and envy from your heart.
  - Use what God has given you to bless others rather than compete with them.
