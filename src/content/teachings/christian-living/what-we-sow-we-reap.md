truth: Our choices and actions have consequences, so believers should carefully consider what they are sowing.

insight:
  - What a person continually sows through thoughts, words, and actions can produce corresponding consequences.
  - Good choices and faithful actions can produce good fruit, while sinful choices can produce harmful consequences.
  - Believers should not treat their choices as insignificant because what is sown eventually produces a harvest.
  - What fills the heart can influence what comes out through a person's words and actions.
  - Understanding the principle of sowing and reaping should lead believers to take responsibility for the way they live.

application:
  - Examine what you are continually sowing through your thoughts, words, and actions.
  - Choose actions that produce godly fruit.
  - Stop feeding habits and attitudes that produce harmful consequences.
  - Be intentional about sowing what is good and pleasing to God.
