truth: Believers should forgive others as an expression of the forgiveness and mercy they have received from God.

insight:
  - Holding on to resentment keeps the wrong before the heart instead of allowing forgiveness to bring release.
  - Forgiveness requires choosing not to continually dwell upon the wrongs committed against us.
  - Believers are called to forgive others because God has shown forgiveness toward them.
  - Those who have hurt us should not become the reason we withhold love or refuse to do good.
  - Forgiveness allows believers to respond to wrongs with grace rather than continuing the cycle of resentment.

application:
  - Forgive those who have wronged you rather than continually rehearsing the offense.
  - Bring painful relationships before God and ask Him to help you forgive.
  - Choose to show kindness even toward those who have hurt you.
  - Refuse to let resentment control your thoughts, words, or actions.
