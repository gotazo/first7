insight:
  - Choices have consequences that should not be treated lightly.
  - What people sow can produce effects that extend beyond the moment in which the choice was made.
  - The actions of one generation can influence the circumstances experienced by later generations.
  - God calls each person to take responsibility for the choices they make.
  - Faithful obedience can become a blessing to those who follow, while destructive choices can create consequences that others must face.

application:
  - Consider the long-term consequences of the choices you make.
  - Choose obedience to God even when disobedience appears easier.
  - Seek to leave a faithful example and a godly inheritance for those who come after you.
