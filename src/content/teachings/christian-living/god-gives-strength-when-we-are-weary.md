truth: God invites the weary and burdened to bring their burdens to Him and find rest in His strength.

insight:
  - God does not call His people to carry every burden in their own strength.
  - Weariness, sadness, loneliness, and heavy burdens are opportunities to turn toward God rather than carry them alone.
  - God's strength is greater than the burdens His people face.
  - Rest requires trusting God with what is too heavy for us to carry.
  - Receiving God's strength enables believers to continue faithfully doing what He has called them to do.

application:
  - Bring your burdens to God instead of carrying them alone.
  - Turn to God when you are weary, discouraged, or overwhelmed.
  - Trust God's strength when your own strength is insufficient.
  - Continue faithfully in the work God has given you while depending upon Him for strength.
