truth: Each person is responsible before God for their own response to Him and can choose to walk in a different way from the patterns of those who came before.

insight:
  - A person's spiritual response to God does not have to remain bound to the failures of previous generations.
  - God calls each generation to choose whether it will continue in disobedience or walk in His ways.
  - Recognizing destructive patterns is an opportunity to turn from them rather than continue them.
  - A new life in Christ provides a new direction instead of requiring believers to remain in former ways.
  - Obedience to God can establish a different pattern for those who come after.

application:
  - Examine patterns in your life that are contrary to God's Word.
  - Turn away from sinful practices instead of allowing them to continue.
  - Choose to establish a godly pattern of obedience for those who come after you.
