truth: Believers must put away bitterness and respond to others with kindness, tenderheartedness, and forgiveness.

insight:
  - Bitterness is not something believers are instructed to preserve or cultivate.
  - Unresolved anger can affect words, attitudes, and relationships.
  - Scripture calls believers to replace bitterness, wrath, anger, and malice with kindness and tenderheartedness.
  - Forgiveness is grounded in the forgiveness God has shown us through Christ.
  - Guarding the heart includes dealing with resentment before it develops into harmful words or actions.
  - A heart being changed by God should increasingly produce mercy and forgiveness rather than hostility toward others.

application:
  - Examine your heart for resentment toward people who have offended you.
  - Refuse to feed bitterness through repeated anger, accusations, or harmful speech.
  - Bring unresolved anger honestly before God.
  - Choose kindness and self-control when dealing with difficult people.
  - Forgive others as God has forgiven you in Christ.
  - Where bitterness has already affected a relationship, seek peace and reconciliation where possible.
