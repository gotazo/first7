truth: Believers must recognize and reject the works of the flesh so that they may walk according to the Spirit.

insight:
  - Scripture identifies the works of the flesh and calls believers to recognize them rather than excuse them.
  - Hatred, jealousy, wrath, strife, envy, and other sinful attitudes are serious matters before God.
  - Sinful behavior cannot be overcome by merely addressing outward circumstances; the heart must be changed.
  - Walking in the Spirit requires turning away from the works of the flesh.
  - God's Word exposes sinful attitudes so believers can repent and walk in a different way.

application:
  - Examine your attitudes and conduct against the standard of God's Word.
  - Repent when the works of the flesh are revealed in your life.
  - Choose to walk according to the Spirit rather than giving place to sinful desires.
  - Replace hatred, envy, strife, and wrath with the character produced by the Spirit.
