truth: Believers should not allow adversity to define their identity or stop them from continuing in God's purpose.

insight:
  - Painful experiences can make a person believe that life has ended when something important is lost.
  - Rejection, divorce, sickness, failure, or loss should not become the reason a believer gives up.
  - A difficult experience does not remove God's purpose from a person's life.
  - Believers are called to rise again and continue rather than remain defined by what happened to them.
  - The believer's future should not be determined solely by past disappointment.

application:
  - Do not allow a painful experience to become your identity.
  - Refuse to give up because of rejection, failure, loss, or disappointment.
  - Bring your past before God and continue moving forward.
  - Ask God to help you remain faithful to His purpose despite adversity.
