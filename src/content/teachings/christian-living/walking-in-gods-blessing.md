insight:
  - God's blessing is connected with hearing His voice and walking according to His commands.
  - Obedience to God is not separate from the believer's walk of faith but is an expression of that faith.
  - God's people are called to seek Him and His ways rather than pursue blessing apart from Him.
  - A life ordered according to God's Word is positioned to experience the good purposes He has established.
  - God's blessing should lead believers toward greater faithfulness rather than away from dependence upon Him.

application:
  - Hear God's Word and put it into practice.
  - Seek God Himself rather than pursuing His blessings apart from Him.
  - Let God's blessings increase your gratitude and faithfulness to Him.
