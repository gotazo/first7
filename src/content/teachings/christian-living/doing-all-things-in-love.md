truth: Believers should let love shape everything they do, including the small and ordinary ways they serve others.

insight:
  - Love is expressed not only through great acts but also through simple acts of kindness and care.
  - Small acts of love can have significance even when they appear ordinary or unnoticed.
  - The way believers treat others should reflect love, compassion, and genuine care.
  - Serving others with love gives value to ordinary responsibilities and everyday actions.
  - Christian service should be motivated by love rather than the desire for recognition.

application:
  - Look for simple opportunities to show love and kindness each day.
  - Treat ordinary responsibilities as opportunities to serve others with love.
  - Serve without seeking recognition or praise.
  - Let love guide the way you speak and act toward others.
