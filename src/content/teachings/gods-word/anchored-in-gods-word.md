truth: Believers become established by anchoring themselves in what God has spoken rather than depending upon their own wisdom and solutions.

insight:
  - The wilderness is a place where believers are rooted in what God has spoken concerning them.
  - Jesus answered temptation by standing on what God had spoken rather than depending upon another solution.
  - Prayer does not replace the need to be anchored in God's Word.
  - God's Word must become more than something we hear; it must be consumed, remembered, meditated upon, and allowed to shape us.
  - The Word of God becomes a believer's spiritual weapon and equips them to face what lies ahead.
  - Anchoring ourselves in God's Word changes our perspective and teaches us to look to God rather than making our circumstances, work, or other people our source.

application:
  - Find the promises and truths of God's Word that speak to the situation you are facing.
  - Read, meditate on, and remember Scripture rather than relying only on your own solutions.
  - Let God's Word correct the way you think about your circumstances.
  - Build your faith upon what God has spoken.
