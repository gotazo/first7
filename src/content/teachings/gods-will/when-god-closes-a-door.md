truth: Believers must surrender their plans to God and trust His direction when circumstances do not unfold as expected.

insight:
  - Our plans may differ from the direction God has appointed for us.
  - A closed path does not automatically mean that the enemy has prevailed.
  - Believers should seek God's direction instead of assuming that every desired opportunity is God's will.
  - Surrender means accepting God's way when it differs from our own plans.
  - God can work through circumstances we did not choose to accomplish His purposes.

application:
  - Bring your plans before God and ask Him to direct your steps.
  - Do not automatically interpret every closed opportunity as spiritual attack.
  - Surrender your preferred outcome when God's direction becomes clear.
  - Trust God's wisdom when His way differs from what you expected.
