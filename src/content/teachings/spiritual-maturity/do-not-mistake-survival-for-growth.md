truth: Spiritual maturity is more than becoming accustomed to struggle; it produces evidence of growth and stability in a believer's life.

insight:
  - Being used to struggle does not necessarily mean that a person has become strong.
  - Survival is different from building; continuing to live through difficulty does not by itself show that something lasting is being established.
  - Hard work should produce evidence of what has been built rather than merely keeping a person alive from one difficulty to the next.
  - The wilderness is not presented as the final place of a believer's development.
  - Spiritual maturity becomes visible when what God is doing in a person's life produces lasting evidence.

application:
  - Examine whether you are merely surviving or actually growing.
  - Look for evidence of spiritual maturity in your character and obedience.
  - Do not become satisfied with simply enduring the same struggles year after year.
  - Ask God to show you what needs to change so that your life produces lasting fruit.
