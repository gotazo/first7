truth: Those who belong to God are called to walk as His children by following Him and walking in love.

insight:
  - Being a child of God is reflected not only in what a believer professes but also in how that believer lives.
  - Paul connects being followers of God with walking in love.
  - Christ provides the example of self-giving love that believers are called to follow.
  - Carrying the name of Christ should lead to conduct that agrees with the character and teaching of Christ.
  - The identity of God's children should shape relationships, attitudes, words, and actions.
  - Walking as God's children means continually bringing everyday conduct into agreement with the life God has called us to live.

application:
  - Follow God by studying His Word and putting it into practice.
  - Let love guide the way you treat other people.
  - Look to Christ's example when deciding how to respond to others.
  - Examine whether your conduct reflects the identity you profess as a child of God.
  - Put away attitudes and behaviors that contradict God's character.
  - Let your everyday life demonstrate that you belong to God.
