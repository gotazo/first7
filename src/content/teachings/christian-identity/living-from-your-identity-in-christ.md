truth: The believer's new life is found in Christ, and that life should be lived by faith in Him.

insight:
  - Coming to Christ involves a new way of understanding who we are and how we live.
  - Paul describes his old life as crucified with Christ and his present life as one in which Christ lives in him.
  - Identity in Christ is not merely a title; it affects the way a believer understands and lives his or her life.
  - The believer's life is now centered on Christ rather than on the former way of life.
  - Faith in Christ should shape the believer's daily conduct and decisions.
  - Knowing who we are in Christ helps us live consistently with the new life God has given us.

application:
  - Remember that your life belongs to Christ and is now lived in Him.
  - Let your identity in Christ shape your choices, priorities, and conduct.
  - Put away patterns that belong to your former life.
  - When facing decisions, consider whether your choices agree with the life you have received in Christ.
  - Live by faith in the Son of God rather than relying on your former identity.
  - Continue growing in your understanding of what it means to belong to Christ.
