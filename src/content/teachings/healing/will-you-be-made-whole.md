truth: Jesus invites people to respond in faith to His offer of restoration rather than remain in the condition they have accepted.

insight:
  - Jesus addressed the man's willingness to be made whole before performing the miracle.
  - Long-standing conditions can become so familiar that people stop expecting change.
  - Christ's invitation calls for a response of faith rather than resignation.
  - Remaining in the same condition is not God's desire when He calls people to respond to Him.
  - God's restoring work begins with a willing response to His invitation.

application:
  - Bring every area of brokenness honestly before Christ.
  - Respond in faith when Jesus calls you to trust and obey Him.
  - Believe that God is able to bring restoration according to His will.
