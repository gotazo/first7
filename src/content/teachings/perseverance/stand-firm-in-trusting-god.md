truth: Believers can remain steadfast through painful circumstances by placing their trust in God, who is their rock, defence, and refuge.

insight:
  - Hurtful words and rejection can cause deep pain, but they should not become the foundation upon which believers determine their worth or direct their lives.
  - Being spiritually fortified means remaining firmly grounded in God even when circumstances are painful and the actions of others cannot be changed.
  - When believers face situations they cannot fix through their own efforts, they can continue trusting God rather than allowing distress to turn them away from Him.
  - God is a refuge for His people; believers can pour out their hearts before Him instead of carrying their burdens as though they must face them alone.
  - Trusting God does not mean that painful experiences are unreal, but that pain does not have to determine whether believers remain steadfast in Him.

application:
  - Bring your hurt, disappointment, and fears honestly before God in prayer.
  - Do not allow hurtful words or rejection to move you away from trusting and obeying God.
  - When you cannot change a painful situation, seek God's direction and remain faithful to Him.
  - Return to Scripture when your feelings or circumstances threaten to shake your confidence in God.
  - Remember that God is your refuge, and continue to stand firmly upon His Word.
