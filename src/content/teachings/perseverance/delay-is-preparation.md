truth: God uses seasons of waiting and testing to prepare His people for His purposes.

insight:
  - God often prepares His people through seasons of waiting, testing, and growth before entrusting them with greater responsibility.
  - Joseph endured betrayal, slavery, and imprisonment before God raised him to save many lives (Genesis 37–50).
  - David was anointed king years before he sat on the throne, learning to trust God through trials and hardship (1 Samuel 16–31).
  - Jesus was led into the wilderness to be tested before beginning His public ministry (Matthew 4:1–11).
  - Seasons of preparation teach dependence upon God, strengthen perseverance, and equip believers for His purposes.

application:
  - Trust God's work even when progress seems slow.
  - Allow difficult seasons to strengthen your faith instead of discouraging you.
  - Remain faithful while God prepares you for future opportunities.
  - Remember that God's timing and preparation are always purposeful.
