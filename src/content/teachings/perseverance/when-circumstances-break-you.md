truth: Believers must not allow painful circumstances to destroy their hope, purpose, or dependence upon God.

insight:
  - Disappointment, rejection, loss, and other painful circumstances can wound a person's inner life.
  - A wounded spirit can make a person lose the desire to continue, even when God has not abandoned them.
  - Painful circumstances should not become the foundation upon which a person builds their reason for living.
  - Failure, loss, or disappointment does not mean that a believer's purpose has ended.
  - God can sustain His people through difficult circumstances and enable them to continue.

application:
  - Bring disappointment and pain to God rather than allowing them to define your life.
  - Do not give up because a relationship, career, plan, or circumstance did not work out as expected.
  - Continue seeking God when your circumstances have wounded you.
  - Ask God for strength to keep moving forward in His purpose.
