truth: God gives His people strength to stand faithfully even when He does not immediately remove the storm.

insight:
  - Not every breakthrough comes through the immediate removal of a difficult circumstance.
  - There are seasons when faithfulness means continuing to stand while the storm remains.
  - God's strength enables believers to endure circumstances they cannot change.
  - Standing through a trial is itself an expression of faith when a believer continues trusting God.
  - A difficult season does not mean that God has abandoned His people.

application:
  - Do not assume that God has failed because your circumstances have not changed.
  - Ask God for strength to remain faithful in the middle of the trial.
  - Continue praying, obeying, and trusting God while you wait.
  - Stand firm rather than allowing hardship to make you abandon your faith.
