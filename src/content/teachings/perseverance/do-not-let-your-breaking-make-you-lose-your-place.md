insight:
  - Difficult seasons can leave a person wounded and tempted to abandon what God has entrusted to them.
  - Being broken does not mean that a believer has lost their place in God's purpose.
  - Faithfulness requires continuing in obedience even when circumstances are painful.
  - A difficult season should not become a reason to abandon God, His work, or the responsibilities He has given.
  - God calls His people to remain steadfast because their labor in Him is not in vain.

application:
  - Do not abandon your faith because of a painful season.
  - Continue doing what God has called you to do even when you feel weary.
  - Ask God for strength when you are tempted to quit.
  - Remain faithful until the season passes.
