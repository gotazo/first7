truth: Salvation and righteousness are received through God's grace in Christ rather than earned through human works.

insight:
  - Human effort cannot earn salvation or establish righteousness before God.
  - Religious performance cannot replace the grace of God.
  - Believers must depend upon Christ rather than trusting in their own goodness.
  - Grace does not give permission to continue in sin but establishes salvation on the finished work of Christ.
  - Good works should flow from a life transformed by God rather than become the basis for earning salvation.

application:
  - Place your confidence in Christ rather than your own performance.
  - Reject the belief that religious works can earn God's acceptance.
  - Thank God for the grace through which you have been saved.
  - Let obedience flow from your relationship with Christ rather than trying to earn salvation through it.
