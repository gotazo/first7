truth: God calls His people to place their ultimate dependence upon Him rather than allowing anything else to take His rightful place.

insight:
  - Abraham was tested in the area of what was most precious to him, revealing whether his trust ultimately rested in God.
  - Something good can become spiritually dangerous when it takes the place that belongs to God.
  - Surrender means being willing to place what we love in God's hands rather than allowing it to control our trust.
  - God desires our hearts rather than merely our possessions, abilities, relationships, or outward service.
  - Dependence upon God requires trusting Him even when we cannot see what He is doing.

application:
  - Examine what occupies the greatest place in your heart.
  - Surrender people, possessions, plans, and ambitions to God.
  - Do not allow a good gift to become more important than the Giver.
  - Trust God with what you cannot control.
