truth: God desires a broken and contrite heart that recognizes its need for Him and submits to His will.

insight:
  - God values a broken and contrite heart above outward religious performance.
  - Brokenness before God means recognizing our need for Him rather than trusting in ourselves.
  - God can use difficult seasons to bring a person to a place of surrender and dependence upon Him.
  - True brokenness does not destroy a person; it brings the heart into a position where God can work.
  - A surrendered heart becomes willing to let God change what needs to be changed.

application:
  - Examine whether you are depending upon yourself or God.
  - Bring areas of resistance and self-reliance before the Lord.
  - Ask God to give you a humble and contrite heart.
  - Surrender your will to God rather than resisting His work in your life.
