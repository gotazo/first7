truth: God desires a humble and contrite spirit that recognizes its need for Him and yields itself to His will.

insight:
  - A broken spirit before God is different from a spirit wounded by disappointment, rejection, or other painful circumstances.
  - God does not require suffering to teach us our dependence upon Him.
  - True brokenness begins when we recognize that without God we cannot sustain our spiritual life.
  - Dependence upon God should become a continual part of the believer's life rather than something practiced only in difficult circumstances.
  - A surrendered spirit allows God to lead, shape, and work through a person's life.

application:
  - Examine whether your dependence upon God is continual or only present when you are in trouble.
  - Bring your desires, plans, and abilities under God's direction.
  - Seek God in prayer and remain conscious of your need for Him.
  - Ask God to give you a humble and contrite heart.
