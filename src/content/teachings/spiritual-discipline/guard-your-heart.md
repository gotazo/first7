truth: Believers should carefully guard their hearts because the condition of the heart influences the course of their lives.

insight:
  - Scripture gives the heart a central place in the direction of a person's life.
  - Guarding the heart requires paying attention to what is being allowed to shape our thoughts, desires, attitudes, and motives.
  - External problems cannot be separated from the condition of the heart because what is within eventually affects what is expressed outwardly.
  - A guarded heart is not merely protected from evil but intentionally filled with what agrees with God's Word.
  - Spiritual discipline includes examining what is taking root within the heart before it becomes evident in words or actions.
  - Allowing God to search the heart helps believers recognize what needs to be corrected and guarded.

application:
  - Examine what you regularly allow to influence your thoughts and desires.
  - Guard your heart from attitudes and influences that oppose God's Word.
  - Fill your mind with things that are true, honest, just, pure, lovely, and of good report.
  - Ask God to reveal attitudes and motives that need to change.
  - Deal with wrong thoughts and desires before they become sinful words or actions.
  - Make guarding your heart a continual part of your walk with God.
