truth: A believer's life must be continually directed by the Word of God rather than by physical needs alone.

insight:
  - Jesus showed that human life cannot be understood only in terms of physical provision.
  - God's Word is essential to the believer's life and should have a continual place in it.
  - What receives our attention can shape our desires, thoughts, and direction.
  - Spiritual growth requires more than outward religious activity; it involves continually turning the heart toward God.
  - Growing in faith includes hearing and receiving the Word of God.
  - A believer should examine what is feeding his or her thoughts, desires, and priorities.
  - Spiritual understanding should lead a person toward greater dependence upon God rather than independence from Him.

application:
  - Make God's Word a regular and meaningful part of your daily life.
  - Examine what you are continually allowing to shape your thoughts and desires.
  - Give greater attention to things that strengthen your faith and draw you toward God.
  - Do not allow physical needs, fears, entertainment, or worldly influences to become the primary influence over your life.
  - Read Scripture with a desire to know God and obey what He has revealed.
  - Let God's Word shape your decisions, priorities, and daily walk.
