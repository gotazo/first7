truth: Confession acknowledges sin before God and receives His promise of forgiveness and cleansing.

insight:
  - Confession requires honestly acknowledging sin rather than hiding or excusing it.
  - Recognizing where we have sinned brings what needs to be addressed into the open.
  - God's forgiveness is grounded in His faithfulness and justice, not in our ability to make ourselves clean.
  - Confession should be an honest response when God reveals sin in our thoughts, motives, words, or actions.
  - A willingness to acknowledge wrongdoing keeps the heart open to correction and change.
  - Confession begins the process of dealing honestly with sin rather than continuing to conceal it.

application:
  - Confess your sins honestly to God instead of hiding or minimizing them.
  - Name what you have done rather than making excuses for it.
  - Receive God's promise of forgiveness and cleansing with humility.
  - When God reveals sin in your heart, respond honestly rather than resisting correction.
  - Where appropriate, acknowledge wrongdoing to those you have sinned against and seek reconciliation.
  - Continue walking in honesty before God as He reveals areas that need to change.
