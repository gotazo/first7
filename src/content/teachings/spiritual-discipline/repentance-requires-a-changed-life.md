truth: Genuine repentance turns a person toward God and produces a life that reflects that change.

insight:
  - Repentance involves turning to God rather than merely acknowledging that something was wrong.
  - A changed mind concerning sin should lead to a changed direction in life.
  - The fruit of repentance can be seen in actions that agree with the decision to turn from sin.
  - Change may require time, patience, and continued obedience rather than an expectation of instant maturity.
  - Repeated struggles should be met with continued honesty before God rather than using struggle as an excuse to remain unchanged.
  - Repentance affects both the heart and the conduct of the believer.

application:
  - Turn to God when you recognize sin rather than remaining in the same pattern.
  - Let your actions agree with your confession of repentance.
  - When you fall again, confess honestly and return to God rather than hiding your failure.
  - Be patient with the process of spiritual growth while continuing to pursue obedience.
  - Examine whether your daily choices demonstrate the change you profess.
  - Put away practices that belong to your former way of life and pursue conduct that agrees with God's Word.
