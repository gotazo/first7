truth: Believers are called to pursue purity of heart before God.

insight:
  - Biblical purity concerns the condition of the heart rather than outward appearance alone.
  - A pure heart is concerned with what is pleasing to God, not merely with avoiding visible wrongdoing.
  - Purity affects the thoughts, desires, attitudes, words, and actions that flow from within.
  - Outwardly correct behavior can be accompanied by wrong attitudes, making examination of the heart necessary.
  - David's prayer for a clean heart shows the need for God to work within a person.
  - Pursuing purity involves both turning away from sinful influences and cultivating what is good before God.

application:
  - Ask God to reveal anything within you that is contrary to His will.
  - Examine your motives as well as your outward actions.
  - Remove influences that continually draw your heart toward sin.
  - Replace sinful thoughts and attitudes with what agrees with God's Word.
  - Guard your words because they can reveal the condition of your heart.
  - Continue pursuing holiness with a sincere desire to please God.
