truth: God calls His people to let Him search their hearts, reveal what needs to change, and lead them in His way.

insight:
  - God is concerned with the condition of the heart as well as outward actions.
  - God knows the thoughts and motives that cannot be seen by other people.
  - The Holy Spirit searches and reveals the things of God, bringing what is hidden into understanding.
  - Honest examination helps believers recognize thoughts, motives, and actions that are not pleasing to God.
  - When God reveals something that needs to change, humility requires acknowledging it rather than resisting correction.
  - A life of purity includes continually allowing God to examine the heart and lead the believer in His way.

application:
  - Ask God to search your heart and reveal anything that is not pleasing to Him.
  - Pay attention when conviction exposes wrong thoughts, motives, or actions.
  - Confess honestly rather than hiding or excusing what God reveals.
  - Examine whether your outward actions agree with the condition of your heart.
  - Respond to God's correction with humility and obedience.
  - Continue asking God to lead you in the way that pleases Him.
