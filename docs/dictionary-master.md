# Mission

The First7 Bible Dictionary exists to help readers understand the words, people, places, objects, events, and concepts found in the Bible through a Scripture-first approach.

It is designed to be a clear, neutral, and interconnected reference that supports Bible study without becoming a commentary.

The dictionary defines biblical terms, explains their biblical usage, and provides key Scripture references while allowing readers to study the Bible for themselves.

---

# Vision

The long-term vision is to build one of the most comprehensive free, Scripture-first Bible dictionaries available online.

The dictionary will contain a minimum of **550 carefully curated entries**, with room to grow as additional biblical terms are identified.

The emphasis is on:

- Accuracy
- Clarity
- Consistency
- Scripture
- Ease of use

Quality will always take priority over quantity.

---

# Editorial Philosophy

Every dictionary entry should answer two questions:

## 1. What does this term mean?

Provide a concise dictionary definition appropriate to the type of entry.

## 2. How is this term used in Scripture?

Describe how the Bible uses the term.

Do not interpret.

Do not speculate.

Do not promote a theological system.

Allow Scripture to speak for itself.

The First7 Bible Dictionary uses the **King James Version (KJV)** exclusively.

- Preserve the exact wording.
- Do not paraphrase Scripture.

# Long-Term Principles

Every new entry should:

- Follow the style guide.
- Be concise.
- Be factual.
- Be Scripture-first.
- Use KJV.
- Avoid interpretation.
- Avoid speculation.
- Link naturally to related terms.
- Strengthen the overall dictionary.

The dictionary should grow intentionally rather than simply becoming larger.

A smaller, well-connected dictionary is more valuable than a larger collection of disconnected articles.

---

# Definition of Completion

The dictionary should eventually provide clear definitions for every major biblical word, person, place, object, event, office, symbol, and concept that a reader is likely to encounter while studying Scripture.

The goal is not merely to reach 550 entries, but to provide comprehensive, interconnected coverage of the Bible through a consistent, Scripture-first reference work.

550 entries is the minimum milestone—not the final destination.
